Bardet-Biedl syndrome 2 (OMIM:615981)

The patient is a 9-year-old male presenting with obesity, visual impairment, and polydactyly. Family history is negative for similar findings. Physical examination reveals truncal obesity, postaxial polydactyly of the hands, and hypogonadism. Ophthalmologic exam identifies retinal dystrophy with diminished night vision. Intellectual development is mildly delayed. Renal ultrasound shows structural anomalies without functional impairment. Multidisciplinary care includes endocrinology, nephrology, and visual rehabilitation.
