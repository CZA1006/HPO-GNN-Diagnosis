Immunodeficiency 121 with autoinflammation (OMIM:620807)

The patient is a 14-year-old male with recurrent infections, chronic inflammation, and intermittent fevers since infancy. He presents with persistent lymphadenopathy, splenomegaly, and hepatomegaly. Laboratory investigations reveal neutropenia, anemia, elevated inflammatory markers, and hypergammaglobulinemia. Immunophenotyping reveals reduced numbers of regulatory T cells and increased proinflammatory cytokine production. Bone marrow biopsy is reactive with no malignant changes. Treatment includes immunosuppressive therapy and supportive infection prophylaxis. Regular follow-up is essential for monitoring disease progression.
