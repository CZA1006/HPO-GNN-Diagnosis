Progressive external ophthalmoplegia with mitochondrial DNA deletions, autosomal recessive 6 (OMIM:620647)

The patient is a 23-year-old female who presents with progressive bilateral ptosis and ophthalmoplegia beginning in late adolescence. She reports increasing difficulty with upward gaze and eyelid drooping interfering with daily activities. Past medical history is notable for exercise intolerance and mild proximal muscle weakness. Family history is noncontributory. On examination, bilateral symmetric eyelid drooping and limitation of eye movements in all directions are observed. Muscle strength testing reveals slight weakness in the proximal muscles of the upper limbs. Laboratory studies demonstrate mildly elevated serum lactate levels. Electromyography indicates a myopathic pattern. Muscle biopsy shows ragged red fibers. MRI of the brain is unremarkable.
