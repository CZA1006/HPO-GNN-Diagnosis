Loeys-Dietz syndrome 6 (OMIM:619656)

The patient is a 9-year-old female presenting with vascular abnormalities, connective tissue manifestations, and characteristic craniofacial features. Clinical examination demonstrates hypertelorism, bifid uvula, translucent skin with easy bruising, and joint hypermobility. Echocardiographic evaluation reveals significant aortic root dilation with mild mitral regurgitation. Vascular imaging shows arterial tortuosity extending to the cerebral and abdominal vessels. Family history includes sudden cardiac death in a paternal uncle. Management includes beta-blockers, regular cardiovascular imaging, and orthopedic assessment to monitor and address skeletal involvement.
