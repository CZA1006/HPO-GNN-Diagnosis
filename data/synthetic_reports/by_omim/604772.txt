Ventricular tachycardia, catecholaminergic polymorphic, 1 (OMIM:604772)

The patient is a 14-year-old female presenting with exercise-induced syncope and palpitations. She reports several episodes of dizziness and fainting during physical activity and emotional stress. Family history reveals sudden cardiac death in a paternal uncle at age 16. Physical examination is unremarkable, with normal heart sounds and no murmurs. Resting ECG shows normal sinus rhythm without abnormalities. Exercise stress testing induces polymorphic ventricular tachycardia documented by continuous ECG monitoring. Echocardiography reveals normal cardiac structure and function. Laboratory tests including electrolytes and thyroid function are normal. Holter monitoring confirms exercise-induced ventricular arrhythmias consistent with a catecholamine-sensitive mechanism.
