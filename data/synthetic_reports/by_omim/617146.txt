Arthrogryposis, distal, with impaired proprioception and touch (OMIM:617146)

The patient is a 12-year-old female presenting with multiple joint contractures predominantly involving the distal limbs, along with a marked deficit in proprioceptive and tactile sensation. Birth history was unremarkable, but delayed motor milestones were noted early. Neurological examination reveals decreased awareness of limb position and vibration, absent tendon reflexes, and distal muscle weakness. Sensory nerve conduction studies show reduced sensory amplitudes, with preserved motor function. Volitional movements are limited by joint contractures and muscle atrophy in hands and feet. Orthopedic imaging demonstrates fixed flexion deformities with no evidence of bone dysplasia. Physical therapy and occupational therapy are ongoing to improve function and prevent progression.
