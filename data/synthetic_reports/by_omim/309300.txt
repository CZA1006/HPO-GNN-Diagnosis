A 7-year-old male is evaluated for large corneas noted at birth and mild visual impairment. There is no family history of ocular abnormalities.

Ophthalmological examination reveals enlarged corneal diameters (>13 mm), normal intraocular pressure, clear corneas, and normal anterior chamber depth. Visual acuity is mildly reduced. Fundoscopic exam is unremarkable. The rest of the physical examination shows no systemic abnormalities.
