A 17-year-old female is assessed for delayed puberty, poor coordination, and progressive balance problems. Menarche has not occurred, and endocrinological evaluation by her pediatrician showed hypogonadotropic hypogonadism. Parents recall that she had clumsy gait and ataxia since early childhood, progressively worsening into adolescence.

On examination, there is truncal and limb ataxia with intention tremor and dysarthria. Ophthalmological exam demonstrates progressive chorioretinal dystrophy with decreased peripheral vision. Neurological evaluation confirms cerebellar signs with reduced smooth pursuit eye movements. MRI reveals cerebellar atrophy. Hormonal testing shows low LH, FSH, and estradiol levels.
