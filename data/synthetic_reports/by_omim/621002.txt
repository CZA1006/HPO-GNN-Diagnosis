Premature ovarian failure 25 (OMIM:621002)

The patient is a 24-year-old female presenting with irregular menstruation progressing to amenorrhea over the last year. She reports vasomotor symptoms including hot flashes and night sweats. Family history includes early menopause in a maternal aunt. Physical exam shows normal secondary sexual characteristics. Laboratory evaluation reveals elevated follicle-stimulating hormone and luteinizing hormone levels with low estradiol. Pelvic ultrasound finds small ovaries with few follicles. Thyroid and adrenal function tests are within normal ranges. Hormone replacement therapy has been started, and the patient is under endocrinological and gynecological follow-up.
