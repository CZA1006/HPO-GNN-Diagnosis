Atrioventricular septal defect, partial, with heterotaxy syndrome (OMIM:606217)

The patient is a newborn male evaluated for cyanosis and feeding difficulties. Prenatal ultrasounds identified complex congenital heart disease. Family history is negative for congenital anomalies. On clinical examination, the infant presents with central cyanosis, mild respiratory distress, and a systolic murmur. Echocardiography confirms a large partial atrioventricular septal defect with an atrial septal defect and ventricular septal defect components. Abdominal ultrasound reveals situs ambiguous with left isomerism and polysplenia. Additional findings include bilateral left atrial appendages and interrupted inferior vena cava with azygous continuation.
