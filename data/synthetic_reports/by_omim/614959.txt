Developmental and epileptic encephalopathy 14 (OMIM:614959)

The patient is a 10-month-old male presenting with early-onset, intractable seizures and severe developmental delay. Seizures began during the neonatal period and include tonic, myoclonic, and focal types. Neurological examination reveals marked hypotonia and poor eye contact. EEG captures a burst suppression pattern in early phases, evolving to multifocal epileptiform activity. Brain MRI reveals cerebral atrophy and delayed myelination without focal lesions. Extensive metabolic and infectious evaluations are normal. Multidrug antiepileptic therapy has provided partial seizure control.
