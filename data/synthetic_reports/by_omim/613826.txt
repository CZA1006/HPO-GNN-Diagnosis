Leber congenital amaurosis 6 (OMIM:613826)

The patient is a 6-month-old female presenting with poor visual tracking and nystagmus since early infancy. Family history is negative for ocular disorders. Ophthalmologic exam reveals absent pupillary reflexes and severe retinal dystrophy. Electroretinogram shows absent rod and cone responses. Funduscopy demonstrates a pale optic disc with attenuated vessels and diffuse retinal pigment epithelium changes. Neurological examination is otherwise unremarkable. Brain MRI is normal. Vision prognosis is poor; supportive care and low-vision aids are recommended.
