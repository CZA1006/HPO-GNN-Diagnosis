Cerebral creatine deficiency syndrome 1 (OMIM: 300352)
A 3-year-old female presents with global developmental delay, speech impairment, and seizures. Parents report poor feeding and hypotonia since infancy. No family history is noted.

On examination, she has poor interactive responses, truncal hypotonia, and mild dysmorphic features. Brain MRI spectroscopy reveals markedly reduced creatine peak in the basal ganglia and white matter. Plasma and urine creatine levels are reduced. EEG shows generalized epileptiform activity. Metabolic screening for other disorders is negative.
