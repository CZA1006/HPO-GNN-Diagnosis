Developmental and epileptic encephalopathy 9 (OMIM: 300088)
A 2-year-old male is referred for evaluation of intractable seizures and severe developmental delay. His parents report onset of multiple seizure types, including tonic and myoclonic seizures, beginning at 6 months of age. Developmental milestones have regressed since seizure onset. There is no family history of epilepsy or neurodevelopmental disorders.

On physical examination, the child is hypotonic with poor head control and limited voluntary movements. Neurologic assessment reveals frequent myoclonic jerks, hyperreflexia, and occasional dystonic posturing. EEG demonstrates multifocal epileptiform discharges and generalized slowing. Brain MRI shows diffuse cerebral atrophy without focal lesions. Metabolic and infectious workups are unremarkable.
