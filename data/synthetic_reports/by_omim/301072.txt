Neurodevelopmental disorder with epilepsy and hemochromatosis (OMIM: 301072)
A 9-year-old female presents with early-onset epilepsy, developmental delay, and recent onset of fatigue and abdominal discomfort. Parents report frequent seizures that are partially controlled with medication.

On examination, she has mild cognitive impairment and mild hepatomegaly. Laboratory studies reveal elevated serum ferritin and transferrin saturation suggestive of iron overload. Brain MRI indicates diffuse cerebral atrophy with no structural abnormalities. EEG shows multifocal epileptiform discharges. Liver function tests are mildly deranged.
