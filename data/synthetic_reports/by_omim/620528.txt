Neuronopathy, distal hereditary motor, autosomal dominant 11 (OMIM:620528)

The patient is a 24-year-old female presenting with progressive distal muscle weakness and atrophy predominantly affecting the lower limbs. She reports difficulty with walking, frequent tripping, and distal hand weakness. Family history reveals a similar disorder affecting her father. Neurological examination reveals distal muscle wasting, pes cavus, and absent ankle reflexes. Sensory examination is normal. Nerve conduction studies show reduced motor amplitudes without sensory involvement, consistent with a distal hereditary motor neuropathy. Electromyography confirms chronic denervation. Supportive care focuses on physical therapy and orthotic interventions.
