A 12-year-old male is followed for insulin-dependent diabetes mellitus diagnosed at age 6. Parents have noticed increasing vision problems over the last year, characterized by difficulty seeing in low light and progressive vision loss. He complains of excessive thirst, frequent urination, and has had hearing difficulties recently.

On examination, he is thin but well-nourished. Ophthalmological exam shows optic atrophy and decreased visual fields. Audiometry confirms sensorineural hearing loss. Neurological exam reveals mild gait ataxia. Laboratory testing demonstrates stable glycemic control, and MRI of the brain reveals mild atrophy of the brainstem and optic nerves. Genetic testing is underway to confirm the diagnosis.
