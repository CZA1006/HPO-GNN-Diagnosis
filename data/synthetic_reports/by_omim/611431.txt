Legius syndrome (OMIM:611431)

The patient is an 8-year-old male presenting with multiple café-au-lait spots and freckling in axillary and inguinal regions. There is a family history of similar skin findings in the father. Developmental milestones are normal, and nohearing or visual impairments are reported. Physical exam reveals no neurofibromas or Lisch nodules on ophthalmologic evaluation. No skeletal abnormalities are noted. MRI brain is normal, with no evidence of optic pathway gliomas.
