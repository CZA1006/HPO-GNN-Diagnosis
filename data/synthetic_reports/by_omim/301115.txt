A 2-year-old female is referred for recurrent mycobacterial infections and failure to thrive. She has had recurrent episodes of lymphadenitis and pneumonia unresponsive to standard antibiotics. No family history of immunodeficiency is noted.

Physical examination shows mild hepatosplenomegaly and multiple enlarged cervical lymph nodes. Laboratory workup reveals lymphopenia and decreased interferon-gamma production in response to mycobacterial antigens. Chest imaging shows mediastinal lymphadenopathy and pulmonary infiltrates. Immunologic assays reveal defects in IL-12 receptor signaling pathways.
