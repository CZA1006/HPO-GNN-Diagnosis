Spondyloepimetaphyseal dysplasia, Guo-Campeau type (OMIM:620663)

The patient is a 12-year-old male with disproportionate short stature and multiple skeletal deformities. Clinical examination reveals short trunk, barrel-shaped chest, and limited joint mobility. Radiographs demonstrate platyspondyly, irregular metaphyseal and epiphyseal changes, and mild scoliosis. Hearing is intact, and no ocular abnormalities are detected. Neurological examination is unremarkable. Growth hormone levels are normal. Management focuses on orthopedic care, physical therapy, and monitoring for complications such as spinal cord compression.
