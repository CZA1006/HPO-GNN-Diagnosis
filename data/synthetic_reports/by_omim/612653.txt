Spherocytosis, type 4 (OMIM:612653)

The patient is a 7-year-old female with a history of anemia, jaundice, and splenomegaly. Family history reveals similar symptoms in the father. Laboratory investigations show reticulocytosis, increased mean corpuscular hemoglobin concentration, and spherocytes on peripheral blood smear. Osmotic fragility test is positive. Direct antiglobulin test is negative. Abdominal ultrasound confirms splenomegaly.
