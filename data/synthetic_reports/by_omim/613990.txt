Dyskeratosis congenita, autosomal dominant 3 (OMIM:613990)

The patient is a 25-year-old male presenting with mucocutaneous abnormalities, nail dystrophy, and bone marrow failure. Family history reveals a father with similar symptoms. Skin examination demonstrates reticulated hyperpigmentation on the chest and neck. Nail plates are dystrophic and thin. Laboratory studies reveal pancytopenia with hypocellular bone marrow. Pulmonary function testing shows mild restrictive lung disease. Telomere length analysis confirms shortened telomeres consistent with dyskeratosis congenita. Hematopoietic stem cell transplantation is under consideration.
