Developmental and epileptic encephalopathy 111 (OMIM:620504)

A 10-month-old female with refractory seizures beginning in the neonatal period and profound developmental delay presents to clinic. Seizure types include tonic, clonic, and myoclonic jerks. Neurological examination reveals marked hypotonia and diminished spontaneous movements. EEG demonstrates burst suppression and multifocal epileptiform discharges. Brain MRI shows diffuse cerebral atrophy and delayed myelination. Metabolic and infectious studies are unremarkable. Multidrug antiseizure therapy and supportive developmental care are ongoing.
