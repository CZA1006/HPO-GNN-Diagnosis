Amyloidosis, hereditary systemic 5 (OMIM:620658)

A 50-year-old female presents with progressive nephrotic syndrome, hepatomegaly, and peripheral neuropathy. Family history reveals similar features in maternal relatives. Physical exam shows edema, orthostatic hypotension, and decreased distal sensation. Renal biopsy confirms amyloid accumulation in the glomeruli. Serum and urine electrophoresis detect monoclonal proteins. Cardiac MRI demonstrates infiltrative cardiomyopathy. Supportive management includes diuretics, proteinuria control, and monitoring for systemic complications.
