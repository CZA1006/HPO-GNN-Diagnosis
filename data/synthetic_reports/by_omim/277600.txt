A 9-year-old female is referred for evaluation of short stature and visual disturbances. Her parents report that she has been significantly shorter than peers since early childhood and complains of blurred vision and difficulty focusing. There is no family history of similar features or ocular disease.

On examination, she exhibits short stature with proportionate limb shortening and brachydactyly. Ophthalmological assessment reveals microspherophakia with lens subluxation and secondary glaucoma. Cardiac examination is unremarkable. Musculoskeletal evaluation demonstrates joint stiffness without hypermobility. Laboratory tests, including metabolic panels and calcium levels, are normal. Imaging studies exclude skeletal dysplasia.
