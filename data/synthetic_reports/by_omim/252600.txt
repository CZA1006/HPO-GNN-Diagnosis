A 5-year-old female is evaluated for developmental delay, joint stiffness, and coarse facial features. Her parents report delayed walking and limited range of motion in multiple joints. There is no history of significant infections or organomegaly.

Physical examination reveals coarse facial features with a depressed nasal bridge, gingival hypertrophy, and mild hepatomegaly. Joint examination shows contractures at elbows and knees with decreased flexibility. Laboratory studies indicate elevated lysosomal enzymes in plasma. Radiographic imaging reveals dysostosis multiplex characterized by oar-shaped ribs and abnormal vertebral bodies.
