Neurodevelopmental disorder with progressive microcephaly, spasticity, and brain anomalies (OMIM:617527)

The patient is a 2-year-old male with progressive microcephaly, generalized spasticity, and profound developmental delay. He was born at term following a normal pregnancy. Neurologic examination reveals hypertonia, brisk reflexes, and persistent primitive reflexes. Brain MRI demonstrates progressive cerebral and cerebellar atrophy with thin corpus callosum. Metabolic and infectious workups are negative. The clinical course includes worsening motor function and limited cognitive development. Supportive measures focus on physical therapy and symptomatic treatment.
