Myopia 6 (OMIM:608908)

The patient is a 15-year-old female presenting with progressively worsening nearsightedness since early childhood. Her vision deteriorates particularly under dim light conditions, causing difficulty in daily activities and schoolwork. Family history reveals multiple relatives affected by early-onset myopia. Ophthalmological examination confirms high myopia with axial elongation of the globe. Fundoscopic exam is otherwise normal without signs of retinal degeneration. Visual acuity is correctable with glasses. There are no signs of systemic or syndromic disease.
