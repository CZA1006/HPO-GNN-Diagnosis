Diarrhea 8, secretory sodium, congenital (OMIM:616868)

The patient is a 3-month-old female presenting with chronic watery diarrhea and failure to thrive since birth. The diarrhea is profuse and persists despite fasting. Family history is negative for intestinal disorders. Physical examination reveals signs of dehydration and malnutrition. Laboratory studies show hyponatremia and metabolic acidosis. Stool electrolytes are consistent with a secretory diarrhea pattern. Upper gastrointestinal endoscopy is unremarkable. Management focuses on fluid and electrolyte replacement alongside nutritional support.
