Dystonia 22, adult-onset (OMIM:620456)

The patient is a 32-year-old male with insidious onset of focal dystonia beginning as writer’s cramp and progressing to involve cervical muscles and upper limbs. Examination reveals sustained involuntary muscle contractions producing abnormal postures and repetitive movements. There is no family history of movement disorders. Neuroimaging is unremarkable. Trial of botulinum toxin injections has provided partial symptomatic relief. Oral pharmacotherapy with anticholinergic agents is being explored. Multidisciplinary neurologic care including physical therapy is ongoing.
