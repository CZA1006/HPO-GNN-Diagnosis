Cortical dysplasia, complex, with other brain malformations 7 (OMIM:610031)

The patient is a 6-year-old female with a history of drug-resistant epilepsy and global developmental delay. Seizures began in infancy. Neurological exam reveals spasticity and hypotonia with delayed milestones. MRI shows extensive polymicrogyria and areas of cortical dysplasia involving multiple lobes, along with ventriculomegaly. EEG demonstrates multifocal epileptiform activity. Routine metabolic screening was negative.
