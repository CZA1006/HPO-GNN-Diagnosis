Rubinstein-Taybi syndrome 2 (OMIM:613684)

The patient is a 4-year-old female presenting with global developmental delay, distinctive craniofacial features, and broad thumbs. Pregnancy and birth history were unremarkable. Family history is negative for genetic syndromes. On examination, the patient exhibits microcephaly, a prominent nasal bridge, downslanted palpebral fissures, and highly arched eyebrows. The thumbs and great toes are broad and angulated. Behavioral evaluation reveals mild intellectual disability with episodes of hyperactivity. Cardiac and renal ultrasounds are normal. Cranial MRI shows mild corpus callosum hypoplasia.
