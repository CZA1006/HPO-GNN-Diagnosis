Congenital disorder of glycosylation, type IIh (OMIM:611182)

The patient is a 7-month-old female presenting with failure to thrive, hypotonia, and developmental delay. There is no significant family history. Physical examination reveals inverted nipples, abnormal fat distribution, and hepatomegaly. Laboratory investigations show abnormal serum transferrin glycosylation patterns. Brain MRI shows mild cerebral atrophy. Metabolic screening and liver function tests show abnormalities consistent with a glycosylation defect.
