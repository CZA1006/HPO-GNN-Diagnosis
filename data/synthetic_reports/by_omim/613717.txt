Treacher Collins syndrome 2 (OMIM:613717)

The patient is a newborn male with craniofacial abnormalities identified at birth. There is no family history of similar anomalies. Prenatal ultrasounds showed micrognathia and facial hypoplasia. Physical examination reveals downslanting palpebral fissures, hypoplastic zygomatic arches, microtia, and cleft palate. Respiratory distress necessitated neonatal intensive care support. Hearing screening reveals bilateral conductive hearing loss. Radiographic imaging of the facial bones confirms mandibular hypoplasia and absent zygomatic processes. Ophthalmologic examination rules out coloboma. Management involves multi-specialty care with planned reconstructive surgery and audiologic support.
