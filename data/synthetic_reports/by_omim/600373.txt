CODAS syndrome (OMIM: 600373)
A 3-year-old male is evaluated for developmental delay, cataracts, and skeletal abnormalities. Parents note delayed milestones and reduced vision. There is no known family history of similar issues.

On exam, the child has bilateral congenital cataracts, microcephaly, and delayed gross motor skills. Skeletal abnormalities include metaphyseal dysplasia and vertebral segmentation defects revealed by radiographs. Neurologic exam reveals mild hypotonia, and brain MRI shows delayed myelination.
