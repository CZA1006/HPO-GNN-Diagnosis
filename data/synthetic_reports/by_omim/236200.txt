A 6-year-old male is referred for evaluation of developmental delay, lens dislocation noted by ophthalmology, and thromboembolic events. The mother reports he has been below average in height and exhibits mild learning difficulties. Previous history includes recurrent episodes of deep vein thrombosis without clear provoking factors.

On physical examination, the patient has a marfanoid habitus with long limbs and arachnodactyly. Lens subluxation is confirmed on slit lamp examination. Cardiovascular assessment demonstrates a systolic murmur and mild aortic root dilation on echocardiography. Laboratory studies reveal elevated plasma homocysteine and methionine levels. Vitamin B6 levels are within normal ranges.
