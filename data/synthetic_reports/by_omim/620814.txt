MHC class I deficiency 3 (OMIM:620814)

The patient is a 10-year-old female presenting with recurrent respiratory and gastrointestinal infections starting in early infancy. She demonstrates failure to thrive and chronic lymphadenopathy. Laboratory analyses reveal markedly reduced expression of MHC class I molecules on peripheral blood mononuclear cells, accompanied by low CD8+ T-cell counts and impaired cytotoxic T-cell function. Serum immunoglobulin levels are within normal limits, and neutrophil function tests are unremarkable. High-resolution chest imaging identifies early bronchiectasis. The patient is managed with prophylactic antibiotics, immunoglobulin replacement, and supportive respiratory care. Immune monitoring and infection prevention strategies are ongoing.
