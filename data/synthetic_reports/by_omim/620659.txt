Amyloidosis, hereditary systemic 6 (OMIM:620659)

The patient is a 38-year-old male presenting with autonomic dysfunction, peripheral neuropathy, and cardiac arrhythmias. Clinical features include orthostatic hypotension, gastrointestinal dysmotility, and sensory loss in the feet. Family history is positive for sudden cardiac death in the father. Neurological examination confirms distal sensory loss and diminished reflexes. Cardiac evaluation shows restrictive cardiomyopathy with ventricular tachyarrhythmias. Tissue biopsy reveals amyloid deposits. The patient is managed with symptomatic treatment and referral for hematological evaluation for possible systemic therapy.
