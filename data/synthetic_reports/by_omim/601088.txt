Ayme-Gripp syndrome (OMIM: 601088)
A 10-year-old female presents with intellectual disability, short stature, and facial dysmorphism. Her parents note delayed language acquisition and behavior problems. No consanguinity or family history is reported.

On examination, she displays a long face, arched eyebrows, prominent nasal bridge, and thin upper lip. Growth parameters are below the 3rd percentile. Neurologic evaluation shows mild hypotonia and poor coordination. Brain MRI is normal.
