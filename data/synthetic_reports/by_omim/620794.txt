Deafness, autosomal recessive 124 (OMIM:620794)

The patient is a 6-year-old male child with congenital bilateral sensorineural hearing loss identified through newborn screening. There is no significant family history or associated vestibular symptoms. Otoscopic examination is normal. Audiological testing including auditory brainstem response and otoacoustic emissions confirms moderate to severe sensorineural hearing loss bilaterally. Imaging of the temporal bones via CT scan shows normal cochlear and vestibular anatomy. There are no additional neurological or systemic findings. Early amplification with hearing aids and enrollment in speech therapy programs has been initiated.
