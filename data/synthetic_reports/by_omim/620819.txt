Rothmund-Thomson syndrome, type 4 (OMIM:620819)

A 9-year-old female born to non-consanguineous parents presents with poikiloderma manifesting as reticulated erythema, hypopigmentation, and telangiectasias of the face and extremities. She also exhibits sparse scalp hair, absent eyelashes, and juvenile cataracts. Growth retardation and skeletal abnormalities including radial ray defects are noted. Developmental milestones are reportedly within normal limits. Laboratory tests show chromosomal instability on cytogenetic analysis. Ophthalmological evaluation confirms early cataract formation. Supportive treatment includes dermatologic care, cataract management, and orthopedic surveillance.
