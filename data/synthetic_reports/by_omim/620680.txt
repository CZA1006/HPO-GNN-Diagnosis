Lipodystrophy, congenital generalized, type 5 (OMIM:620680)

The patient is a 6-year-old male presenting with near-total loss of adipose tissue from birth and associated insulin resistance. Clinical features include muscular hypertrophy, acromegaloid appearance, and hepatomegaly. Laboratory studies reveal severe hypertriglyceridemia and elevated fasting glucose. Liver ultrasound indicates fatty infiltration with hepatomegaly. The patient has no dysmorphic features. Multi-disciplinary management involves strict glycemic and lipid control and nutritional counseling.
