Rett syndrome, congenital variant (OMIM:613454)

The patient is a 2-year-old female presenting with severe developmental delay, hypotonia, and absent speech. She was born following a normal pregnancy. Early neonatal history was notable for feeding difficulties and poor tone. There is no known family history of neurologic disorders. Clinical examination shows lack of purposeful hand movements, reduced eye contact, and episodes of hyperventilation. EEG demonstrates abnormal slowing without epileptiform discharges. Brain MRI is normal. Genetic investigation excludes classic MECP2 mutations, consistent with a congenital Rett-like syndrome of alternative genetic etiology.
