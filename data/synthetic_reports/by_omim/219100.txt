A 2-year-old male is brought for evaluation of loose, sagging skin and delayed motor milestones. His parents describe an “old-looking” appearance since infancy with skin that wrinkles and hangs loosely, particularly around the face and joints. He has exhibited frequent respiratory infections and concerns of poor growth.

On exam, diffuse skin laxity is observed with reduced elasticity and visible wrinkling. There is facial sagging, laxity of the neck and axillae, and joint hypermobility. Cardiopulmonary examination reveals a soft systolic murmur; echocardiography detects mild aortic dilatation. Pulmonary function tests show mild restrictive defect. Laboratory investigations are unremarkable aside from mild anemia.
