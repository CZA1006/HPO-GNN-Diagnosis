Combined oxidative phosphorylation deficiency 58 (OMIM:620451)

The patient is a 5-month-old female presenting with hypotonia, severe developmental delay, and frequent metabolic crises characterized by lethargy and acidosis. Laboratory data reveal elevated lactate and alanine, along with decreased activity of multiple mitochondrial respiratory chain complexes identified in muscle biopsy. Brain MRI demonstrates multifocal cerebral white matter abnormalities and basal ganglia involvement. Supportive management focuses on metabolic stabilization, nutritional support, and seizure control. Prognosis is guarded due to progressive neurodegeneration.
