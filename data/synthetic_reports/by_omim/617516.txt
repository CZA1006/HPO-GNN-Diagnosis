Stankiewicz-Isidor syndrome (OMIM:617516)

The patient is an 8-year-old female presenting with mild intellectual disability, craniofacial dysmorphisms, and short stature. Family history is negative. Physical examination reveals broad nasal tip, thin upper lip, and brachydactyly. Neurologic evaluation shows no focal deficits. MRI brain is unremarkable. Routine blood tests and metabolic screening are normal. Supportive therapies focusing on education and physical therapy are recommended.
