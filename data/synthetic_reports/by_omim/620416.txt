Spastic paraplegia 90A, autosomal dominant (OMIM:620416)

The patient is a 38-year-old female with a 10-year history of progressive spasticity and weakness predominantly in the lower limbs. Family history is positive for a similarly affected parent. Neurological examination demonstrates increased muscle tone, hyperreflexia, and bilateral extensor plantar responses. Sensory examination is normal. MRI brain and spinal cord do not show any structural abnormalities. Electromyography and nerve conduction studies are normal. Treatment is symptomatic with physical therapy to improve walking and reduce spasticity.
