Ullrich congenital muscular dystrophy 1C (OMIM:620728)

The patient is a 9-year-old female with congenital onset muscle weakness, joint contractures, and distal hyperlaxity. Clinical features include delayed motor acquisitions and kyphoscoliosis. Examination shows proximal muscle atrophy, restricted range of motion in major joints, and distal joint hypermobility. Elevated serum creatine kinase is noted. Muscle biopsy confirms muscular dystrophy with collagen VI deficiency. Cardiopulmonary evaluation is normal. Treatment is multidisciplinary, focusing on physical therapy, orthopedic interventions, and monitoring for respiratory complications.
