Osteogenesis imperfecta, type XV (OMIM:615220)

The patient is a 6-year-old female with multiple pathological fractures since infancy with minimal trauma. She has blue sclerae and mild dentinogenesis imperfecta. Examination reveals joint hypermobility and short stature. Radiological imaging demonstrates generalized osteopenia, limb deformities, and vertebral compression fractures. Laboratory tests including calcium, phosphate, and vitamin D are normal. Treatment involves bisphosphonate administration, orthopedic interventions, and physical rehabilitation.
