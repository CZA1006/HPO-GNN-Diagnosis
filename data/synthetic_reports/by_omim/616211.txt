Developmental and epileptic encephalopathy 28 (OMIM:616211)

The patient is a 5-month-old male presenting with ongoing seizures resistant to conventional antiepileptic treatment and severe developmental delay. Seizure onset occurred within the neonatal period, characterized by generalized tonic and clonic movements. Physical examination reveals marked hypotonia and poor visual tracking. EEG studies indicate multifocal epileptiform activity and abnormal background rhythms. Brain MRI shows nonspecific delayed myelination with no focal structural abnormalities. Metabolic and infectious evaluations are unremarkable. The clinical course is characterized by refractory epilepsy and progressive developmental impairment.
