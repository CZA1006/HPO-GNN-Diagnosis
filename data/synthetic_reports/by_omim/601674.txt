EZH1-related neurodevelopmental disorder (OMIM:601674)

The patient is a 7-year-old male referred for developmental delay and behavioral difficulties. Pregnancy and delivery were without complications. He achieved motor milestones late, sitting at 14 months and walking independently at 24 months. Speech development has been significantly delayed, with current expressive language limited to short phrases. He demonstrates hyperactivity and difficulties with attention regulation. Family history is negative for neurodevelopmental disorders. On physical examination, no dysmorphic features are noted, and growth parameters fall within normal ranges. Neurological exam shows low-normal muscle tone and mild clumsiness in coordination tasks. MRI of the brain reveals nonspecific global volume reduction without structural malformations. EEG is normal. Cognitive evaluation shows borderline intellectual function.
