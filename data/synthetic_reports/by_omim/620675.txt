Leukodystrophy, hypomyelinating, 27 (OMIM:620675)

The patient is a 3-year-old female with global developmental delay, hypotonia, and nystagmus. Early milestones were delayed, with no regression observed. Physical examination reveals truncal hypotonia and impaired coordination. Brain MRI demonstrates diffuse hypomyelination involving cerebral and cerebellar white matter with thinning of the corpus callosum. Metabolic investigations are normal. Supportive treatment focuses on developmental therapies and symptomatic management of motor impairments.
