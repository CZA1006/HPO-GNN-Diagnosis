Muscular dystrophy, limb-girdle, autosomal recessive 28 (OMIM:620375)

The patient is a 10-year-old female presenting with progressive proximal muscle weakness, difficulty climbing stairs, and frequent falls. Birth and early developmental history were normal. On examination, there is marked weakness of hip and shoulder girdle muscles with preserved distal strength. Serum creatine kinase is elevated. Electromyography shows myopathic changes. Muscle biopsy reveals dystrophic features with increased connective tissue. Cardiac evaluation is normal. Management includes physical therapy and regular cardiac and respiratory monitoring.
