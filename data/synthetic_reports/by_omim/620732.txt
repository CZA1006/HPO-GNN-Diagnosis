Neurodevelopmental disorder with hyperkinetic movements, seizures and structural brain abnormalities (OMIM:620732)

The patient is a 5-year-old female with developmental delay, frequent generalized seizures, and marked hyperkinetic movement disorders including chorea and dystonia. Birth history was uneventful. Neurologic examination reveals hypotonia with intermittent involuntary movements and orofacial dyskinesia. MRI brain shows cerebral atrophy with ventriculomegaly and delayed myelination. EEG reveals multifocal epileptiform discharges. Seizure control is partial despite multidrug antiepileptic therapy. Supportive care includes physical and occupational therapies alongside seizure management.
