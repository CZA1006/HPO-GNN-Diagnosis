Spermatogenic failure 94 (OMIM:620850)

The patient is a 28-year-old male presenting with infertility and mild hypogonadism. Examination reveals small testes and decreased body hair distribution. Semen analysis shows severe oligozoospermia with reduced motility and abnormal morphology. Laboratory tests reveal low-normal serum testosterone and elevated gonadotropins. Scrotal ultrasound identifies small, heterogeneous testicular parenchyma. The patient denies exposure to toxic agents. He is being evaluated for endocrine and genetic causes. Lifestyle modifications and reproductive counseling have been recommended.
