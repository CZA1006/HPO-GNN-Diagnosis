Loeys-Dietz syndrome 2 (OMIM:610168)

The patient is a 10-year-old female evaluated for arterial aneurysms and connective tissue abnormalities. Physical exam reveals bifid uvula, hypertelorism, and joint hypermobility. Echocardiogram shows aortic root dilation with mild valve regurgitation. There is a history of spontaneous arterial dissections in a maternal relative. Vascular imaging reveals tortuous arteries in the neck and abdomen. Laboratory studies are otherwise normal.
