Maple syrup urine disease, type Ib (OMIM:620698)

The patient is a neonate presenting with poor feeding, lethargy, and hypotonia in the first week of life. Family history is negative. Laboratory studies reveal elevated plasma branched-chain amino acids, particularly leucine, isoleucine, and valine. Urine organic acid analysis identifies characteristic keto acids. Blood gas shows metabolic acidosis. MRI brain demonstrates bilateral edema in the basal ganglia and brainstem. Management includes dietary restriction of branched-chain amino acids and close metabolic monitoring. The patient is stabilized with improved neurologic function.
