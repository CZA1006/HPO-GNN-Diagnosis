A 10-day-old female neonate is admitted with feeding intolerance, jaundice, and lethargy. She was born at term with no complications but developed poor feeding and vomiting in the first week of life. Physical exam reveals hepatomegaly and cataracts.

Laboratory studies show elevated bilirubin, transaminases, and hypoglycemia. Urine reducing substances are positive. Plasma galactose-1-phosphate levels are elevated. Enzymatic assay of galactose-1-phosphate uridyltransferase demonstrates deficiency. Early dietary intervention with galactose restriction has been initiated.
