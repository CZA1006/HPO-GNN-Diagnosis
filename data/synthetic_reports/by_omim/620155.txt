Rabin-Pappas syndrome (OMIM:620155)

The patient is a 5-year-old male with developmental delay, distinctive craniofacial features, and skeletal anomalies. Features include macrocephaly, coarse facial features, and broad nasal bridge. Limb examination reveals short digits and joint laxity. Neurological assessment finds mild intellectual disability and hypotonia. Brain imaging is normal. The patient has behavioral challenges, including difficulties with attention and social interaction. Interventions focus on early developmental therapies and behavioral support.
