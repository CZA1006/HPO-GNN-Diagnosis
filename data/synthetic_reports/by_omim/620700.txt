Intellectual developmental disorder, autosomal recessive 81 (OMIM:620700)

The patient is a 4-year-old female with profound global developmental delay and seizures starting in infancy. Family history includes consanguinity. Physical examination reveals microcephaly, hypotonia, and facial dysmorphism characterized by low-set ears and broad nasal bridge. EEG shows multifocal epileptiform discharges. Brain MRI demonstrates cerebral atrophy and ventriculomegaly. Metabolic screening is unremarkable. Antiepileptic therapy and multidisciplinary rehabilitative support are ongoing.
