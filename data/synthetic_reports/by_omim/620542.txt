Neuronopathy, distal hereditary motor, autosomal recessive 10 (OMIM:620542)

A 14-year-old male presents with progressive distal muscle weakness, pronounced atrophy in lower extremities, and foot drop. The patient also complains of cramps and mild sensory disturbances. Family history is negative. Neurological exam reveals distal weakness, muscle wasting, and absent ankle jerks, with preserved proximal strength. Sensory deficits involve decreased vibration sense. Nerve conduction studies indicate severe motor axonal neuropathy with sensory involvement, consistent with a mixed hereditary motor-sensory neuronopathy. Supportive care and physiotherapy are ongoing.
