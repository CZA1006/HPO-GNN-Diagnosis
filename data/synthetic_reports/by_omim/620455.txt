Neurodevelopmental disorder with hypotonia and speech delay, with or without seizures (OMIM:620455)

The patient is a 4-year-old female with hypotonia, delayed speech, and mild developmental delay. There is a history of recurrent febrile seizures controlled with antiepileptic medication. Physical examination shows generalized low muscle tone and joint laxity. Neurologic assessment reveals normal reflexes and no focal deficits. Brain MRI is normal. Speech therapy and physiotherapy have been initiated, and the patient is undergoing ongoing developmental evaluations.
