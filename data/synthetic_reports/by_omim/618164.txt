Cardiac, facial, and digital anomalies with developmental delay (OMIM:618164)

A 3-year-old male is referred for evaluation of developmental delay and multiple congenital anomalies. The child exhibits midface hypoplasia, upslanting palpebral fissures, and bilateral clinodactyly. Cardiologic examination shows ventricular septal defect and mild pulmonary valve stenosis. Neurological examination reveals hypotonia and delayed gross motor milestones. Brain imaging is within normal limits. Cardiac surgical consultation and developmental therapies are in progress.
