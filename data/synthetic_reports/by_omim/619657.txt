Congenital heart defects, multiple types, 8, with or without heterotaxy (OMIM:619657)

The patient is a newborn male diagnosed with complex congenital heart malformations, including atrioventricular septal defect, double outlet right ventricle, and pulmonary stenosis. Prenatal ultrasound identified abnormal cardiac anatomy and suspected heterotaxy. Postnatal physical examination reveals cyanosis and respiratory distress. Abdominal ultrasound demonstrates situs ambiguous with polysplenia. Echocardiography confirms complex cardiac defects requiring surgical intervention. Additional abnormalities include bilateral left atrial appendages and interrupted inferior vena cava. Multidisciplinary care involves pediatric cardiology, cardiothoracic surgery, and nephrology due to associated renal anomalies.
