Autoimmune disease, multisystem, infantile-onset, 3 (OMIM:620430)

The patient is a 15-month-old male presenting with recurrent fevers, hepatosplenomegaly, autoimmune cytopenias, and failure to thrive. There is no family history of autoimmune conditions. Laboratory workup during febrile episodes shows elevated inflammatory markers, anemia, thrombocytopenia, and neutropenia. Autoantibody panels indicate the presence of multiple autoantibodies, including anti-nuclear and anti-dsDNA antibodies. Immunophenotyping reveals lymphopenia and impaired immune regulation. Bone marrow biopsy demonstrates erythroid hyperplasia without malignancy. The patient is managed with immunosuppressive therapy and supportive care, with close monitoring for infectious complications.
