Combined oxidative phosphorylation deficiency 59 (OMIM:620646)

An infant presents with profound hypotonia, lactic acidosis, and developmental delay. Muscle biopsy shows significant deficiencies in multiple respiratory chain complexes. Brain MRI demonstrates white matter abnormalities and basal ganglia involvement. Laboratory studies confirm elevated lactate and alanine. Supportive care includes metabolic stabilization, nutritional support, and symptomatic management. The clinical course is progressive with neurologic decline.
