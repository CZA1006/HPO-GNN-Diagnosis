Muscular dystrophy, limb-girdle, autosomal recessive 21 (OMIM:617232)

The patient is a 9-year-old female with progressive proximal muscle weakness affecting shoulder and pelvic girdles. The neonatal and early developmental period was unremarkable. Family history is negative for neuromuscular diseases. On examination, the patient exhibits waddling gait, difficulty rising from the floor, and mild scapular winging. Muscle strength is decreased proximally with preserved distal strength. Serum creatine kinase is elevated. Electromyography reveals myopathic features. Muscle biopsy shows dystrophic changes with fibrosis and fiber size variation. Cardiac evaluation is normal. Physical therapy and supportive management are ongoing.
