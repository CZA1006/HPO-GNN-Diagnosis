Neurodevelopmental disorder with motor and language delay, ocular defects, and brain abnormalities (OMIM:620428)

The patient is a 4-year-old female with global developmental delay, severe speech impairment, and ocular abnormalities including microphthalmia and coloboma. She was born at term following a normal pregnancy, with no relevant family history. Physical examination reveals hypotonia, poor head control, and distinctive facial features including hypertelorism and a flat nasal bridge. Neurologic exam detects delayed gross motor milestones and poor coordination. Brain MRI demonstrates ventriculomegaly, cerebral white matter hypoplasia, and mild cortical dysplasia. Ophthalmologic assessment confirms structural defects correlating to visual impairment. Supportive interventions encompass visual rehabilitation, speech therapy, and physical therapy.
