Immunodeficiency 114, folate-responsive (OMIM:620603)

The patient is a 10-year-old male with a history of recurrent bacterial infections, chronic anemia, and developmental delay. Physical examination shows pallor and mild hepatosplenomegaly. Laboratory evaluation reveals macrocytic anemia, leukopenia, and impaired humoral immunity. Serum and cerebrospinal fluid folate levels are low. Administration of folinic acid leads to hematological improvement and reduction in infection frequency. Immunoglobulin levels remain below normal, and immunoglobulin replacement therapy is considered. Long-term monitoring includes developmental assessment and infection surveillance.
