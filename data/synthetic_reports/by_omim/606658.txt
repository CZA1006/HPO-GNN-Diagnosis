Spinocerebellar ataxia 15 (OMIM:606658)

The patient is a 45-year-old male presenting with a 5-year history of progressive gait ataxia and limb incoordination. Family history reveals an affected father with similar symptoms starting in his 40s. Neurological examination shows truncal ataxia, dysarthria, and nystagmus. Muscle tone and strength are normal. MRI of the brain demonstrates marked cerebellar atrophy, predominantly involving the vermis. Sensory and motor nerve conduction studies are normal. Cognitive function is intact. Routine blood work, including vitamin E and copper levels, is within normal limits.
