Neurodevelopmental disorder with intracranial hemorrhage, seizures, and spasticity (OMIM:620371)

The patient is a 9-month-old male with a history of seizure onset at 3 months, developmental delay, and recurrent intracranial hemorrhages. Neonatal history was significant for feeding difficulties and hypotonia. Neurological examination reveals spasticity, increased deep tendon reflexes, and delayed milestones. Brain MRI shows multiple areas of hemorrhage and cerebral atrophy. Coagulation studies are normal. The patient is receiving anticonvulsant medication and supportive care, with close neurologic monitoring.
