A 3-year-old female presents with recurrent syncopal episodes triggered by exercise and loud noises. Birth history is normal. Parents report episodes of sudden collapse with brief loss of consciousness, with return to baseline within minutes. There is no family history of unexplained sudden death but both parents appear healthy.

Physical examination reveals bilateral sensorineural hearing loss confirmed by audiometry. Cardiovascular examination is normal apart from occasional irregular heartbeats. ECG demonstrates a markedly prolonged QT interval with episodes of ventricular arrhythmias. Electrolyte levels are normal. The patient is referred for further cardiac evaluation and genetic testing.
