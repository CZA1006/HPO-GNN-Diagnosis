Stickler syndrome, type II (OMIM:604841)

The patient is a 9-year-old male followed for progressive visual impairment and joint laxity. He reports a history of high myopia diagnosed in early childhood and recurrent episodes of joint pain without significant trauma. Family history reveals a father with early-onset osteoarthritis and visual problems. Physical examination shows midface hypoplasia, cleft palate, and hyperextensible joints, especially in the hands and knees. Ophthalmologic evaluation reveals vitreoretinal degeneration and early signs of retinal detachment. Hearing assessment confirms mild sensorineural hearing loss. Radiographs demonstrate mild spondyloepiphyseal dysplasia.
