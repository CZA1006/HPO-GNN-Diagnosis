Kufor-Rakeb syndrome (OMIM:606693)

The patient is a 21-year-old male presenting with parkinsonism, supranuclear gaze palsy, and early cognitive decline. The symptoms began at age 18 with tremor and rigidity, progressing to bradykinesia and postural instability. Family history is negative. Neurological examination reveals hypomimia, vertical gaze palsy, and pyramidal signs including hyperreflexia and extensor plantar responses. MRI brain shows atrophy of the basal ganglia and midbrain. Neuropsychological assessment reveals deficits in executive and memory functions. Laboratory investigations exclude Wilson disease and other metabolic causes. Response to dopaminergic therapy is limited.
