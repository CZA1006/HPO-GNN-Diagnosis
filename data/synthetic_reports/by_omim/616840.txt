Parkinson disease 23, autosomal recessive, early onset (OMIM:616840)

A 28-year-old male presents with early-onset parkinsonian symptoms including resting tremor, rigidity, and bradykinesia. Symptoms began in his mid-20s with progressive motor deterioration. Neurological exam reveals masked facies, decreased arm swing, and postural instability. Brain MRI is normal with no atypical features. Response to levodopa is partial. Family history is negative for parkinsonian disorders. Supportive management includes pharmacotherapy and physiotherapy.
