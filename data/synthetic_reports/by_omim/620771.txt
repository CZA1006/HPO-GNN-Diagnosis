Jeffries-Lakhani neurodevelopmental syndrome (OMIM:620771)

The patient is a 5-year-old male presenting with intellectual disability, hypotonia, and distinct craniofacial morphology including a prominent forehead and broad nasal bridge. Early motor and speech milestones were delayed. Neurological examination shows decreased tone with no focal deficits. MRI brain reveals mild ventriculomegaly and delayed myelination. Behavioral assessment identifies attention difficulties and mild autistic features. Multidisciplinary developmental support and behavioral therapies are in place.
