Yuksel-Vogel-Bauser syndrome (OMIM:620703)

The patient is a 6-year-old female presenting with developmental delay, hypotonia, and facial dysmorphisms including hypertelorism and micrognathia. Birth was complicated by polyhydramnios. Neurological examination reveals poor head control and delayed motor milestones. Cardiac evaluation shows mild patent foramen ovale without structural defects. Brain MRI reveals mild white matter abnormalities. Supportive care includes physical therapy and developmental interventions.
