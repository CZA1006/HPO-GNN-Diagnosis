Developmental and epileptic encephalopathy 5 (OMIM:613477)

The patient is an 11-month-old male presenting with early-onset, refractory epilepsy and severe developmental delay. Seizures began within the first month of life, characterized by generalized tonic-clonic and focal seizures. Neurological examination reveals hypotonia and impaired visual tracking. EEG shows burst suppression pattern during initial phases and multifocal epileptiform discharges subsequently. Brain MRI demonstrates delayed myelination and mild cerebral atrophy. Laboratory metabolic studies are normal. Genetic testing for causative variants in epilepsy-associated genes is ongoing.
