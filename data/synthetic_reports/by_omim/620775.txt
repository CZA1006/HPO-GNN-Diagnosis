Neuromuscular disorder, congenital, with dysmorphic facies (OMIM:620775)

A 2-year-old male presents with congenital hypotonia, severe muscle weakness, and characteristic facial dysmorphisms including a broad nasal bridge, shallow orbits, and micrognathia. Pregnancy was complicated by polyhydramnios and decreased fetal movements were reported. On examination, the patient exhibits marked global weakness with difficulty attaining head control and no independent sitting. Reflexes are absent, and deep tendon reflexes are decreased. Serum creatine kinase is moderately elevated. Electromyography shows a myopathic pattern. Muscle biopsy reveals dystrophic changes with prominent fibrosis. No cardiac abnormalities are identified. Multidisciplinary management focuses on respiratory support, nutritional supplementation, and neuromotor rehabilitation.
