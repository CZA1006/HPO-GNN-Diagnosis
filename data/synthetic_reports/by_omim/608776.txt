Congenital disorder of glycosylation, type II (OMIM:608776)

The patient is a 9-month-old male with failure to thrive, hypotonia, and developmental delay. Family history is noncontributory. On examination, the patient has a broad nasal bridge, inverted nipples, and generalized hypotonia. Laboratory studies reveal hypoglycemia and elevated liver transaminases. Transferrin isoform analysis shows abnormal glycosylation patterns consistent with a congenital disorder of glycosylation type II. Brain MRI shows mild cerebral atrophy. Cardiac and renal ultrasounds are normal.
