Spermatogenic failure 90 (OMIM:620744)

The patient is a 28-year-old male presenting with primary infertility after two years of unprotected intercourse. Medical history is unremarkable; no history of cryptorchidism or testicular trauma. Physical exam shows normal secondary sexual characteristics and testes of reduced volume bilaterally. Semen analysis reveals azoospermia. Hormonal profile demonstrates elevated follicle-stimulating hormone with normal testosterone levels. Scrotal ultrasound excludes varicocele or structural abnormalities. Genetic karyotyping and Y-chromosome microdeletion studies are pending. The patient is counseled regarding fertility options.
