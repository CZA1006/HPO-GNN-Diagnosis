Cornelia de Lange syndrome 6 (OMIM:620568)

The patient is a 3-year-old female presenting with developmental delay, distinctive facial features, and limb anomalies. Physical examination reveals synophrys, long eyelashes, a wide nasal bridge, and micrognathia. Limb examination shows clinodactyly, small hands with tapering fingers, and partial syndactyly. Neurologically, the patient exhibits hypotonia and delayed speech development. Brain MRI is unremarkable. Behavioral issues, including attention deficits and hyperactivity, are noted. Multidisciplinary care with developmental therapies and orthopedic management is ongoing.
