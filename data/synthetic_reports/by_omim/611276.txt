Glaucoma 1, open angle, H (OMIM:611276)

The patient is a 45-year-old male presenting with gradual, painless vision loss in both eyes. Family history is positive for glaucoma in the mother and maternal grandfather. Intraocular pressure measurements are elevated. Slit-lamp examination reveals open angles with optic nerve cupping and visual field defects consistent with glaucomatous damage. Central corneal thickness is within normal limits. Standard laboratory investigations are unremarkable.
