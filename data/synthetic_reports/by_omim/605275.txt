Noonan syndrome 2 (OMIM:605275)

The patient is a 4-year-old female presenting with short stature, congenital heart defects, and distinctive facial features. She was born at term after an uneventful pregnancy. Family history is negative for genetic syndromes. On examination, there is hypertelorism, down-slanting palpebral fissures, low-set posteriorly rotated ears, and a webbed neck. Cardiological evaluation reveals pulmonic valve stenosis with mild right ventricular hypertrophy. Growth parameters are below the 10th percentile. Developmental milestones are mildly delayed, particularly in speech. Echocardiography confirms valvular abnormalities with no evidence of hypertrophic cardiomyopathy.
