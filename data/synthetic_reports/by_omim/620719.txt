Neurodevelopmental disorder with motor abnormalities, seizures, and facial dysmorphism (OMIM:620719)

The patient is a 6-year-old male featuring global developmental delay, frequent generalized seizures, and distinct craniofacial anomalies including microcephaly, broad nasal bridge, and thin upper lip. Early development showed hypotonia, progressing to spasticity and ataxia. EEG reveals multifocal epileptiform discharges. MRI brain shows cerebral atrophy and simplified gyral patterns. Seizures are partially controlled with polytherapy. Behavioral problems include hyperactivity and impaired social interaction. Supportive care integrates antiepileptic treatment and multidisciplinary developmental interventions.
