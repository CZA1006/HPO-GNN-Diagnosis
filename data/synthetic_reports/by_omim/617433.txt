Retinitis pigmentosa 78 (OMIM:617433)

The patient is a 23-year-old female reporting progressive night blindness and constriction of peripheral visual fields since adolescence. Family history is significant for similarly affected first-degree relatives. Ophthalmic examination displays typical retinitis pigmentosa signs, including bone-spicule pigmentation, attenuated retinal vessels, and optic disc pallor. Electroretinogram shows profound reduction in rod and cone responses. Optical coherence tomography confirms thinning of the outer retina. Visual acuity is moderately diminished. The patient occasionally experiences photophobia but reports no systemic symptoms. Counseling and visual rehabilitation are ongoing.
