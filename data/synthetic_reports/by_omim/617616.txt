Skraban-Deardorff syndrome (OMIM:617616)

The patient is a 5-year-old male with intellectual disability, speech delay, and hypotonia. There are subtle facial dysmorphisms, including broad forehead and downturned mouth corners. Developmental milestones are notably delayed. MRI brain shows mild ventriculomegaly with no structural malformations. Clinical evaluation reveals behavioral difficulties, including irritability and hyperactivity. Supportive management includes special education and occupational therapy focused on communication and motor skills.
