ReNU syndrome (OMIM:620851)

The patient is a 12-year-old female presenting with developmental delay, renal anomalies including chronic kidney disease, and occasional seizures. Birth history was unremarkable. Physical exam reveals mild facial dysmorphism with broad nasal bridge and thin upper lip. Neurological assessment shows hypotonia, poor coordination, and speech delay. Renal ultrasound demonstrates hypoplastic kidneys with decreased cortical thickness. Brain MRI reveals mild cerebral atrophy and delayed myelination. Metabolic workup is pending. Management includes renal support and multidisciplinary developmental therapies.
