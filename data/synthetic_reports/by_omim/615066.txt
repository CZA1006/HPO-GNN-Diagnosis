Osteogenesis imperfecta, type XIV (OMIM:615066)

The patient is an 8-year-old male presenting with recurrent low-impact fractures and blue sclerae. Developmental milestones are appropriate. Family history reveals no similar bone disorders. Physical examination notes mild short stature, dental abnormalities including dentinogenesis imperfecta, and joint laxity. Radiographs reveal multiple healed fractures, osteopenia, and bone deformities. Bone mineral density studies confirm decreased density. Laboratory investigations including serum calcium and vitamin D are within normal limits. Bisphosphonate therapy and orthopedic management are initiated.
