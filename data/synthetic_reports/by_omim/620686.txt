Premature ovarian failure 23 (OMIM:620686)

The patient is a 28-year-old female presenting with secondary amenorrhea and infertility. Menarche occurred at age 12 with regular cycles until 24 years when menstrual irregularities began. She reports hot flashes and mood changes. Family history reveals early menopause in maternal relatives. Physical examination is unremarkable with no dysmorphic features. Laboratory evaluation demonstrates elevated follicle-stimulating hormone (FSH) and luteinizing hormone (LH) levels, along with low estradiol. Pelvic ultrasound shows small, hypoplastic ovaries with reduced antral follicle count. Thyroid and adrenal function tests are normal. Hormonal replacement therapy has been initiated with ongoing monitoring.
