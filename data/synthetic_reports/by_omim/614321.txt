Myopathy, distal, Tateyama type (OMIM:614321)

The patient is a 22-year-old male with slowly progressive distal muscle weakness affecting the hands and forearms. Family history is negative for neuromuscular diseases. On clinical examination, there is atrophy of interosseous and thenar muscles with preserved proximal strength. Sensory examination is normal. Electromyography shows myopathic changes limited to distal muscles. Serum creatine kinase levels are mildly elevated. Muscle MRI reveals selective involvement of distal upper limb muscles. Muscle biopsy demonstrates myopathic features without inflammation. Physical therapy and symptomatic management are provided.
