Oculopharyngeal muscular dystrophy 2 (OMIM:620460)

The patient is a 32-year-old male presenting with slowly progressive ptosis, dysphagia, and proximal muscle weakness involving the limb girdles. Symptoms began in early adulthood with difficulty lifting the arms and episodes of choking. Physical examination reveals bilateral eyelid drooping, reduced facial muscle bulk, and weakness on shoulder abduction and hip flexion. Tongue atrophy with fasciculations is noted. Serum creatine kinase is mildly elevated. Electromyography demonstrates myopathic changes with occasional fibrillation potentials. Muscle biopsy shows variability in fiber size and rimmed vacuoles. Cardiac evaluation is normal. Symptom management includes swallowing therapy and physical rehabilitation.
