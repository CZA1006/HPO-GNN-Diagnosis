Maturity-onset diabetes of the young, type IX (OMIM:612225)

The patient is a 16-year-old female with a family history of diabetes affecting multiple generations. She presents with mild hyperglycemia detected on screening without ketosis. Physical examination is unremarkable, and BMI is within normal range. Laboratory investigations reveal elevated fasting blood glucose and impaired glucose tolerance. Autoimmune markers are negative. Genetic testing for MODY type IX mutations is pending.
