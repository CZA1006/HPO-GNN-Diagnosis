Ciliary dyskinesia, primary, 53 (OMIM:620642)

The patient is a 5-year-old male with recurrent otitis media, chronic sinusitis, and bronchiectasis. Nasal nitric oxide is consistently low. High-speed video microscopy and electron microscopy of respiratory epithelial biopsies reveal defective ciliary motility and ultrastructural abnormalities including absence of inner dynein arms. Pulmonary function tests indicate mild obstructive lung disease. The patient is managed with airway clearance, antibiotic therapy during infections, and ongoing pulmonary surveillance.
