Tubulointerstitial kidney disease, autosomal dominant, 5 (OMIM:617056)

The patient is a 25-year-old female referred for evaluation of chronic kidney disease with a family history of similar renal disease. She presents with mild hypertension and reduced kidney function. Urinalysis reveals low-level proteinuria and sterile pyuria. Renal ultrasound demonstrates small echogenic kidneys with cortical thinning. Kidney biopsy shows tubulointerstitial fibrosis and tubular atrophy. There is no evidence of glomerular disease. Management focuses on blood pressure control and slowing progression of renal impairment.
