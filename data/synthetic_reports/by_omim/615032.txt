Intellectual developmental disorder with autism and macrocephaly (OMIM:615032)

The patient is a 5-year-old male with global developmental delay, autistic behaviors, and macrocephaly. The pregnancy was uneventful, and there is no consanguinity. On assessment, he shows poor eye contact, stereotyped hand movements, and significant speech delay. Head circumference measurements are above the 98th percentile. Neurological exam reveals hypotonia but no focal deficits. MRI brain is unremarkable. Cognitive testing reveals moderate intellectual disability. Behavioral interventions and speech therapy are ongoing.
