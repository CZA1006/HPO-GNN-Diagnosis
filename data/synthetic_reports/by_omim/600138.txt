Retinitis pigmentosa 11 (OMIM: 600138)
A 15-year-old female reports progressive night blindness and peripheral vision loss over the last 3 years. Family history reveals multiple relatives with visual impairment in adulthood.

On ophthalmologic evaluation, fundoscopic examination reveals bone spicule pigmentation, attenuated retinal vessels, and waxy pallor of the optic disc. Visual field testing confirms tunnel vision. Electroretinography shows diminished rod and cone responses. Neurological exam is normal.
