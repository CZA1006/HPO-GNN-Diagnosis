Developmental and epileptic encephalopathy 4 (OMIM:612164)

The patient is a 9-month-old male with refractory seizures and severe developmental delay. Seizures started in infancy and are frequent despite multiple antiepileptic medications. Examination reveals hypotonia and poor visual tracking. EEG shows multifocal epileptiform discharges with hypsarrhythmia. MRI brain reveals delayed myelination and generalized cerebral atrophy. Metabolic screening is normal. Genetic testing is being performed.
