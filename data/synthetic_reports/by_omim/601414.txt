Retinitis pigmentosa 18 (OMIM:601414)

The patient is a 17-year-old female presenting with progressive difficulties seeing in dim light since early adolescence. She notes gradual constriction of her peripheral visual fields and now struggles with night vision. Family history reveals that her maternal grandmother experienced similar vision loss in her teens. On examination, visual acuity is mildly reduced, and visual field testing demonstrates concentric peripheral field restriction. Fundus examination shows attenuated retinal vessels and bone-spicule pigmentary changes in the mid-periphery. Optical coherence tomography demonstrates thinning of the outer retinal layers, and electroretinography shows markedly reduced scotopic response, consistent with diminished rod function.
