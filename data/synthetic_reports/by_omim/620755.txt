Generalized epilepsy with febrile seizures plus, type 12 (OMIM:620755)

The patient is a 3-year-old female with a history of febrile seizures since infancy and subsequent development of generalized tonic-clonic seizures. Seizures are often prolonged and refractory to initial treatment. Neurological exam is normal between episodes. EEG displays generalized spike-wave discharges interictally. MRI brain is normal. She is currently managed with antiepileptic medications with partial seizure control. Development and cognition are age-appropriate.
