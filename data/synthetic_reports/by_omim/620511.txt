Fliedner-Zweier syndrome (OMIM:620511)

The patient is a 3-year-old female with severe bone marrow failure, immunodeficiency, and developmental delay. She has a history of recurrent infections, petechiae, and anemia. Laboratory studies reveal pancytopenia. Bone marrow biopsy shows marked hypocellularity. Neurological evaluation identifies global developmental delay and hypotonia. Family history is negative for hematologic diseases. Management includes supportive transfusions, infection prophylaxis, and consideration of hematopoietic stem cell transplantation.
