Trichothiodystrophy 3, photosensitive (OMIM:616395)

The patient is a 5-year-old female presenting with photosensitivity, brittle hair, and recurrent respiratory infections. Physical exam confirms brittle, short scalp hair and scaling erythematous skin predominantly over sun-exposed areas. Neurological assessment reveals mild intellectual disability and hypotonia. Hair shaft analysis shows a distinctive tiger-tail banding on polarized light microscopy. Photosensitivity rash requires strict sun avoidance measures. Supportive therapies include dermatological management and immunological evaluation for recurrent infections.
