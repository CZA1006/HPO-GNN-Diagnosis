Spermatogenic failure 96 (OMIM:621001)

The patient is a 31-year-old male presenting with infertility of two years duration. He reports no history of testicular trauma or infections. Physical examination shows normal secondary sexual characteristics but bilateral small testes. Semen analysis reveals azoospermia. Hormonal studies highlight elevated follicle-stimulating hormone with normal testosterone levels. Scrotal ultrasound shows reduced testicular volume without focal lesions. Genetic tests for chromosomal abnormalities and Y-chromosome microdeletions are pending. The patient is counseled about assisted reproductive options.
