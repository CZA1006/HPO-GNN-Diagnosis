A 15-month-old male is referred for failure to thrive, delayed motor development, and skeletal deformities. Parents report frequent fractures and delayed walking. There is a history of poor feeding and muscle weakness. No consanguinity or family history of bone disease is noted.

Physical examination reveals frontal bossing, widened wrists, and bowing of the lower limbs. Laboratory tests show hypocalcemia, elevated serum parathyroid hormone, low 1,25-dihydroxyvitamin D levels, and elevated alkaline phosphatase. Radiographs demonstrate metaphyseal cupping and fraying of long bones. Renal function is normal.
