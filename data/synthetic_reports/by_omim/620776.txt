Thrombocytopenia 13, syndromic (OMIM:620776)

The patient is an 8-year-old female presenting with bleeding diathesis since infancy, including easy bruising, epistaxis, and prolonged bleeding from minor injuries. She also exhibits facial dysmorphism characterized by a broad nasal bridge, downturned mouth, and short stature. Laboratory evaluation reveals severe thrombocytopenia with normal platelet morphology, normal coagulation parameters, and reduced platelet aggregation in response to multiple agonists. Bone marrow biopsy shows normal megakaryocyte numbers. There is mild intellectual impairment noted on developmental assessment. The patient is managed with platelet transfusions during bleeding episodes and supportive care.
