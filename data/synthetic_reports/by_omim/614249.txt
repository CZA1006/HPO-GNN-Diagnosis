Intellectual developmental disorder, autosomal recessive 18, with or without epilepsy (OMIM:614249)

The patient is a 3-year-old female presenting with global developmental delay and refractory epilepsy beginning in infancy. She was born at term after an uncomplicated pregnancy. Family history reveals consanguinity but no known neurological disorders. Neurologic examination shows hypotonia and poor visual tracking. EEG reveals multifocal epileptiform discharges. Brain MRI demonstrates delayed myelination and mild cortical atrophy. Metabolic screening is unremarkable. Seizure control remains challenging despite multiple medications. Multidisciplinary supportive care is ongoing.
