Retinitis pigmentosa 27 (OMIM:613750)

The patient is a 21-year-old male with progressive night blindness and peripheral visual field loss since late adolescence. Family history reveals similarly affected maternal relatives with visual deterioration in their 20s. Ophthalmologic examination shows bone-spicule pigmentation in the mid-periphery, optic disc pallor, and arteriolar attenuation. Visual field testing reveals concentric constriction. Optical coherence tomography demonstrates thinning of the outer nuclear layer. Electroretinogram displays markedly reduced rod and cone responses. No extraocular manifestations are noted. Supportive management with low-vision aids and regular surveillance is ongoing.
