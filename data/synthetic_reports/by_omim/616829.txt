Congenital disorder of glycosylation, type Iip (OMIM:616829)

A 9-month-old female presents with failure to thrive, hypotonia, and developmental delay. Examination reveals inverted nipples and behavioral abnormalities. Laboratory studies show abnormal transferrin glycosylation patterns consistent with a glycosylation disorder. Brain MRI shows mild cerebral atrophy. Liver function tests indicate mild cholestasis. Nutritional support and symptom management are ongoing with multidisciplinary follow-up.
