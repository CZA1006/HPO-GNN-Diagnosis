A 12-year-old male is evaluated for growth retardation, progressive alopecia, and visual impairment. Parents report that since infancy he had sparse scalp hair and delayed growth. Over time, thickening of the skin over the face and scalp developed, along with contractures and limited joint mobility. Visual difficulties were noted starting in late childhood.

Physical examination reveals coarse facial features with alopecia, cutis laxa, and prominent forehead. Multiple skin nodules and joint contractures are apparent. Ophthalmologic evaluation detects corneal opacities and optic nerve pallor. Imaging shows ventriculomegaly and white matter changes. Genetic testing is in progress.
