A 2-day-old newborn is admitted with lethargy, vomiting, and poor feeding. The infant was born at term without complications but developed progressive irritability and reduced responsiveness at home. On arrival to the hospital, the baby was hypotonic and drowsy.

Physical examination shows poor arousal, shallow breathing, and hepatomegaly. Neurologic reflexes are depressed. Laboratory testing reveals hyperammonemia with plasma ammonia markedly elevated, respiratory alkalosis, and elevated citrulline on plasma amino acid analysis. Liver function tests are mildly abnormal. Brain ultrasound is normal; however, EEG shows diffuse slowing consistent with metabolic encephalopathy.
