Noonan syndrome 6 (OMIM:613224)

The patient is a 5-year-old female evaluated for mild intellectual disability, short stature, and characteristic facial features. Pregnancy was uncomplicated. Family history reveals a paternal cousin with similar dysmorphic features. Physical examination identifies ptosis, low-set posteriorly rotated ears, webbed neck, and pectus excavatum. Growth measurements are below the 10th percentile. Cardiological assessment reveals mild pulmonic stenosis. Developmental assessment shows moderate speech delay and learning difficulties. Ultrasound abdomen is normal. Molecular genetic analysis targeting RAS-MAPK pathway genes is ongoing.
