Lui-Jee-Baron syndrome (OMIM: 301114)
A 5-year-old male presents with hearing loss, hypotonia, and delayed developmental milestones. The parents noticed poor head control and reduced response to auditory stimuli since infancy. There is no consanguinity or known family history.

On examination, the child demonstrates bilateral sensorineural hearing loss, generalized hypotonia, and mild facial dysmorphism including a flat nasal bridge and micrognathia. Brainstem auditory evoked responses confirm hearing impairment. MRI brain reveals hypoplasia of the cochlear nerves.
