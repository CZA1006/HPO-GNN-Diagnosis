Spastic paraplegia 76, autosomal recessive (OMIM:616907)

A 13-year-old female presents with progressive lower limb stiffness, weakness, and gait disturbance. Family history reveals a similarly affected sibling. Clinical examination reveals spasticity in the lower limbs, hyperreflexia, and bilateral Babinski signs. Muscle strength is slightly reduced in the legs. Sensory function is preserved. MRI brain and spine are normal. Nerve conduction studies do not indicate peripheral neuropathy. Vitamin levels and metabolic tests are unremarkable. Genetic confirmation is pending.
