Vasculitis, autoinflammation, immunodeficiency, and hematologic defects syndrome (OMIM:615688)

The patient is a 3-year-old male with recurrent fevers, cutaneous vasculitis, and immune system abnormalities presenting as frequent infections. Family history is negative for similar immune disorders. Clinical examination reveals livedo reticularis, hepatosplenomegaly, and lymphadenopathy. Laboratory workup demonstrates elevated inflammatory markers, pancytopenia, and hypogammaglobulinemia. Bone marrow biopsy shows erythroid hyperplasia. Immunological assays indicate impaired neutrophil function. Treatment includes immunosuppressive therapy and infection prophylaxis.
