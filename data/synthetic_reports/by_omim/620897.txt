Ovarian dysgenesis 11 (OMIM:620897)

The patient is a 16-year-old female presenting with primary amenorrhea and lack of secondary sexual characteristics. Growth parameters fall below the 5th percentile for age. Physical examination reveals hypoplastic breasts and a short vagina. Laboratory evaluation shows elevated gonadotropins (FSH and LH) and low estradiol indicating hypergonadotropic hypogonadism. Pelvic ultrasound demonstrates streak gonads and a hypoplastic uterus. Thyroid and adrenal function tests are normal. Genetic testing for chromosomal abnormalities is pending. Hormonal replacement therapy has been initiated to induce puberty.
