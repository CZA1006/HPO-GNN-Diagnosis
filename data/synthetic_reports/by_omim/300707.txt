A newborn female is admitted for multiple congenital anomalies including syndactyly, telecanthus, and anovestibular fistula. Family history is negative for major malformations.

Physical examination reveals bilateral cutaneous syndactyly of the fingers and toes, hypertelorism with telecanthus, and imperforate anus with perineal fistula. Additional findings include microcephaly and low-set ears. Cardiac and renal ultrasounds are normal. Skeletal survey shows mild vertebral anomalies.
