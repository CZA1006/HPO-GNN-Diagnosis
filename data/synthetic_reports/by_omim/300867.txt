Kabuki syndrome 2 (OMIM: 300867)
A 4-year-old male is referred for evaluation of developmental delay, distinctive facial features, and growth retardation. His parents report delayed speech and motor milestones with behavioral challenges including inattention and hyperactivity. No family history of genetic conditions is noted.

On examination, he has characteristic facial dysmorphisms including long palpebral fissures with eversion of the lateral lower eyelid, arched eyebrows with sparse lateral halves, a depressed nasal tip, and a prominent ears. Short stature and joint hyperflexibility are also present. Cardiac auscultation is unremarkable, and growth parameters are below the 5th percentile. Neurodevelopmental assessment confirms mild intellectual disability.
