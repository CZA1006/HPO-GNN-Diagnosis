Thrombocytopenia 11 with multiple congenital anomalies and dysmorphic facies (OMIM:620654)

The patient is a 5-year-old male with a history of recurrent bruising and petechiae since infancy. Congenital anomalies include micrognathia, low-set ears, and short hands with clinodactyly. Developmentally, the child shows mild intellectual disability and delayed speech. Laboratory studies reveal persistent thrombocytopenia with normal platelet size but decreased aggregation response. Bone marrow biopsy shows normal megakaryocyte numbers and morphology. Coagulation testing is normal. Imaging studies exclude major organ malformations. Care includes hematologic monitoring and supportive therapies to manage bleeding risk.
