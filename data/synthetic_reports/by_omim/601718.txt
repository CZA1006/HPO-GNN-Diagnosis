Retinitis pigmentosa 19 (OMIM:601718)

The patient is a 26-year-old male with progressive vision problems since early adolescence. He reports worsening night vision and difficulty recognizing faces in low-light situations. Central vision is still relatively preserved but peripheral visual fields are markedly reduced. Family history is significant for visual impairment in his mother, who lost functional vision in her 40s. On ophthalmologic examination, funduscopy reveals narrowed retinal vessels and pigment deposits in the mid-peripheral retina. Optical coherence tomography shows thinning of photoreceptor layers, with central foveal preservation. Electroretinography demonstrates severely reduced rod and cone responses. Other systemic examinations and laboratory studies are unremarkable.
