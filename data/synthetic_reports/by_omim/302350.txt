Nance-Horan syndrome (OMIM: 302350)
A 4-year-old male is referred for congenital cataracts and developmental delay. Parents report poor vision since infancy and delayed speech milestones. Family history is significant for maternal relatives with cataracts and dental anomalies.

On examination, the child has bilateral dense cataracts, dental anomalies including screwdriver-shaped incisors and hypodontia, and mild facial dysmorphisms such as a long narrow face. Hearing is normal. Growth parameters are within normal limits. Cardiac and neurological examinations are unremarkable.
