Pseudopseudohypoparathyroidism (OMIM:612463)

The patient is a 12-year-old female presenting with short stature and round face. Family history is noncontributory. Physical examination reveals brachydactyly and subcutaneous ossifications. Laboratory tests show normal calcium, phosphate, and parathyroid hormone levels. Radiographs show shortened metacarpals suggestive of brachydactyly type E. Endocrinological evaluation is otherwise normal.
