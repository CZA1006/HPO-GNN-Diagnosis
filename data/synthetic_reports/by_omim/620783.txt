Developmental and epileptic encephalopathy 115 (OMIM:620783)

The patient is a 7-month-old female with history of frequent, treatment-resistant seizures including tonic, myoclonic, and focal seizures. Developmental progression is severely delayed with minimal spontaneous movements and absence of social interactions. Neurological exam reveals hypotonia and profound global weakness. EEG demonstrates multifocal epileptiform discharges and burst suppression. Brain MRI shows diffuse cerebral atrophy and delayed myelination. Metabolic and infectious evaluations are normal. Seizure management is challenging, involving multiple antiepileptic drugs. Supportive care includes physical and occupational therapy.
