Joubert syndrome 10 (OMIM: 300804)
A 2-year-old male presents with hypotonia, episodic hyperpnea, and developmental delay. Parents report poor head control and abnormal eye movements since infancy.

On examination, the child displays truncal ataxia, oculomotor apraxia, and abnormal respiratory patterns. Fundoscopy is normal. MRI brain demonstrates the classic “molar tooth sign” with cerebellar vermis hypoplasia and thickened superior cerebellar peduncles. Renal ultrasound reveals cystic changes consistent with nephronophthisis.
