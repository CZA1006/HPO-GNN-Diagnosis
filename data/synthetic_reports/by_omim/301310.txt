A 14-year-old male is evaluated for progressive gait instability and intermittent tremor. History also reveals chronic anemia requiring occasional transfusions. No family history of neurological or hematological conditions is reported.

On examination, he demonstrates ataxic gait, limb dysmetria, and nystagmus. Cardiovascular exam reveals a grade II/VI systolic murmur. Laboratory studies show microcytic, hypochromic anemia with ring sideroblasts on bone marrow smear. MRI brain reveals cerebellar atrophy. Iron studies indicate increased serum ferritin and normal serum iron.
