Bethlem myopathy 1C (OMIM:620726)

The patient is a 12-year-old female presenting with progressive weakness and contractures predominantly involving proximal muscles and distal joints. She reports muscle fatigue and difficulty climbing stairs since early childhood. Clinical examination reveals joint contractures at elbows and ankles, mild scapular winging, and muscle atrophy. Serum creatine kinase is mildly elevated. Electromyography shows myopathic changes. Muscle biopsy reveals variability in fiber size and increased connective tissue. Respiratory function remains normal. Physical therapy and orthopedic management focus on preserving mobility and preventing contractures.
