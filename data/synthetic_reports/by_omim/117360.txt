A 2-year-old male child presents with delayed motor development since infancy. His parents report difficulty with balance and coordination, especially noticeable during walking and when attempting to manipulate objects. The child occasionally displays unsteady gait and frequent falls but has not shown significant progression in symptoms over the past year. There is mild hypotonia and dysarthria noted during speech. He sometimes exhibits intention tremor and difficulty with rapid alternating movements. No seizures or vision problems have been observed. Cognitive development shows mild delay.

On physical examination, the child demonstrates gait ataxia with mild limb incoordination. Eye examination reveals nystagmus and abnormalities in ocular smooth pursuit movements. No dysmorphic features are observed. Muscle tone is mildly reduced with preserved deep tendon reflexes. There is no muscle weakness or sensory deficit.

Brain MRI reveals mild cerebellar atrophy predominantly affecting the vermis. Routine laboratory investigations are unremarkable. Genetic testing has not yet been undertaken.
