Congenital myopathy 23 (OMIM:609285)

The patient is a newborn female presenting with marked hypotonia and respiratory distress. She was delivered after a complicated pregnancy with polyhydramnios. Family history reveals a cousin with similar neonatal hypotonia. Physical examination reveals facial weakness, arthrogryposis, and generalized muscle weakness. Serum creatine kinase levels are normal. Electromyography shows myopathic patterns. Muscle biopsy demonstrates disrupted sarcomere organization and protein aggregates. Genetic studies are ongoing.
