Ullrich congenital muscular dystrophy 1B (OMIM:620727)

A 7-year-old male presents with severe hypotonia and muscle weakness detected in early infancy. The patient has delayed motor milestones and poor head control. Examination reveals proximal muscle weakness and hyperlaxity of distal joints. Serum creatine kinase is elevated. Muscle MRI shows selective involvement of posterior thigh and calf muscles. Muscle biopsy reveals dystrophic changes with fibrosis. Respiratory function tests suggest mild restrictive impairment. Supportive management includes respiratory care, orthotics, and physical therapy.
