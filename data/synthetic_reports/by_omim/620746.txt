Neurodevelopmental disorder with hypotonia and characteristic brain abnormalities (OMIM:620746)

The patient is a 3-year-old male with global developmental delay, truncal hypotonia, and poor head control. Pregnancy and birth history were normal. Physical exam reveals broad forehead, mild facial dysmorphism, and increased tendon reflexes. Brain MRI demonstrates diffuse hypomyelination and corpus callosum thinning with enlarged lateral ventricles. Metabolic workup is unremarkable. Nutritional support and physical therapy are ongoing to promote motor development.
