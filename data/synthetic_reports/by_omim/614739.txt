3-methylglutaconic aciduria with deafness, encephalopathy, and Leigh-like syndrome (OMIM:614739)

The patient is a 2-year-old female presenting with developmental regression, hearing loss, and recurrent episodes of encephalopathy. She was born after a normal pregnancy. Clinical examination reveals hypotonia, ataxia, and dystonia. Hearing evaluation confirms sensorineural deafness. MRI brain shows bilateral basal ganglia and brainstem abnormalities consistent with Leigh-like syndrome. Metabolic screening reveals elevated 3-methylglutaconic acid in urine. Supportive care focuses on symptomatic treatment and seizure control.
