Albinism, oculocutaneous, type IV (OMIM:606574)

The patient is a 7-year-old female presenting with hypopigmented skin and hair since birth. Family history is negative for albinism. On examination, she exhibits generalized hypopigmentation with white hair and pale skin. Ophthalmologic examination shows reduced visual acuity, nystagmus, and foveal hypoplasia. Iris transillumination is marked, and fundus examination reveals hypopigmentation with absence of normal foveal reflex. No signs of skin lesions or other systemic abnormalities are present. Routine laboratory investigations are normal.
