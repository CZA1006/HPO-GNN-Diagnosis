Marfan lipodystrophy syndrome (OMIM:616914)

The patient is a 27-year-old male with a history of tall stature, arachnodactyly, and abnormal fat distribution presenting with prominent lipodystrophy of the trunk and limbs. Physical examination reveals joint hypermobility, scoliosis, and pectus excavatum. Cardiovascular evaluation identifies mitral valve prolapse and aortic root dilation. Metabolic screening shows insulin resistance and dyslipidemia. Musculoskeletal imaging demonstrates reduced subcutaneous fat and normal bone density. Management includes cardiovascular surveillance, metabolic control, and orthopedic support.
