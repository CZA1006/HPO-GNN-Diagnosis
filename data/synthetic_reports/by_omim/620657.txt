Amyloidosis, hereditary systemic 3 (OMIM:620657)

The patient is a 45-year-old male with a history of progressive peripheral neuropathy, autonomic dysfunction, and cardiac involvement. He complains of numbness and weakness in the distal extremities, orthostatic hypotension, and palpitations. Physical examination reveals loss of vibration and pinprick sensation in a stocking-glove distribution and signs of congestive heart failure. Echocardiography shows thickened ventricular walls with diastolic dysfunction. Biopsy of abdominal fat reveals amyloid deposits. Laboratory testing confirms elevated serum free light chains. Management includes symptomatic treatment and consideration for disease-modifying therapy.
