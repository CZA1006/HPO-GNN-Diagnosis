Spinocerebellar ataxia 49 (OMIM:619806)

The patient is a 40-year-old female with a decade-long history of progressive gait instability, limb incoordination, and dysarthria. Family history includes an affected sibling with similar symptoms. Neurological examination demonstrates truncal and appendicular ataxia, nystagmus, and mild spasticity. Brain MRI reveals marked cerebellar atrophy predominantly affecting the vermis. Nerve conduction studies are normal. Cognitive function remains intact. Symptomatic treatment with physical rehabilitation aims to preserve function and mitigate symptoms.
