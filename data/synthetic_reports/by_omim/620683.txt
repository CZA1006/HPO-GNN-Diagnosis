Lipodystrophy, familial partial, type 9 (OMIM:620683)

The patient is a 35-year-old male presenting with regional fat loss, particularly in the limbs and abdomen, accompanied by metabolic syndrome features including insulin resistance and dyslipidemia. Clinical examination reveals protuberant abdomen, muscular extremities, and acanthosis nigricans. Laboratory tests show hyperglycemia and elevated triglycerides. Cardiovascular risk factors are aggressively managed. Lifestyle modifications and pharmacological therapy are underway, with regular monitoring for hepatic steatosis and cardiovascular complications.
