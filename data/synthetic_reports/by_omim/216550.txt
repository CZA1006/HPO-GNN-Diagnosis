A 7-year-old female is evaluated for developmental delay, intellectual disability, and distinctive facial features. Parents report delayed speech and motor milestones with behavioral difficulties, including social withdrawal. She has a history of neutropenia with recurrent respiratory infections.

Physical examination shows microcephaly, high nasal bridge, and prominent upper central incisors. She exhibits truncal obesity with slender extremities. Ophthalmologic examination reveals myopia and pigmentary retinopathy. Neurologic evaluation confirms hypotonia and mild ataxia, but reflexes are normal. Complete blood count shows persistent mild neutropenia.
