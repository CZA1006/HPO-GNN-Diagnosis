A neonate is examined for severely shortened limbs, small thorax, and facial dysmorphism noted at birth. Prenatal ultrasound indicated severe micromelia.

On physical evaluation, there is marked rhizomelic shortening of all limbs, prominent forehead, depressed nasal bridge, and micrognathia. Radiographs show delayed ossification of epiphyses, shortened long bones with metaphyseal flaring, and hypoplastic vertebral bodies. Respiratory effort is compromised due to thoracic constriction.
