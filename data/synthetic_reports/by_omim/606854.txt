Cortical dysplasia, complex, with other brain malformations 14A, (bilateral frontoparietal) (OMIM:606854)

The patient is a 3-year-old female presenting with developmental delay and refractory epilepsy since infancy. Prenatal history was uneventful, but early postnatal seizures began in the first month of life. Family history is negative for neurological disorders. On examination, the child displays global developmental delay with hypotonia and poor head control. MRI of the brain reveals bilateral frontoparietal polymicrogyria with other associated malformations including ventriculomegaly and abnormal gyral pattern. EEG shows multifocal epileptiform discharges. Metabolic screening and routine laboratory investigations are normal.
