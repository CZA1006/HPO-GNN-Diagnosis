Intellectual developmental disorder, autosomal recessive 80, with variant lissencephaly (OMIM:620653)

The patient is a 2-year-old female presented for evaluation of global developmental delay, poor head control, and seizures starting in infancy. Prenatal history was unremarkable. Family history reveals consanguinity but no known neurological disorders. Examination shows microcephaly, axial hypotonia, and hyperreflexia. Brain MRI displays diffusely thickened cortex with abnormal gyral pattern consistent with variant lissencephaly, along with ventriculomegaly. EEG demonstrates multifocal epileptiform discharges. Metabolic screening is within normal limits. The patient is on anticonvulsants with partial seizure control.
