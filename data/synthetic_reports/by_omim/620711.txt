Leukoencephalopathy, porphyria-related (OMIM:620711)

The patient is a 30-year-old female with progressive cognitive decline, motor dysfunction, and recurrent neuropsychiatric episodes. MRI brain shows diffuse white matter hyperintensities with patchy enhancement. Laboratory tests reveal increased urinary porphyrin levels and elevated delta-aminolevulinic acid. Neurological exam demonstrates spasticity, tremor, and ataxia. Management focuses on avoiding porphyrinogenic agents and supportive neurorehabilitation. Symptom control with appropriate medications is ongoing.
