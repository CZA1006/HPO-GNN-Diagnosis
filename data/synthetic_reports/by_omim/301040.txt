Alpha-thalassemia/impaired intellectual development syndrome (OMIM: 301040)
A 3-year-old male is evaluated for microcytic anemia and developmental delay. Family history is unremarkable.

Physical examination reveals pallor, mild hepatosplenomegaly, and subtle facial dysmorphism with hypertelorism. Laboratory evaluation shows hypochromic microcytic anemia and reduced hemoglobin A2 levels. Cognitive testing demonstrates mild intellectual disability. Hemoglobin electrophoresis and genetic studies are pending.
