Developmental and epileptic encephalopathy 50 (OMIM:616457)

A 3-year-old male with autosomal recessive inheritance pattern presents with early infantile-onset, refractory epilepsy, and severe developmental delay. Seizures are variable, including tonic, myoclonic, and focal types. Neurological examination demonstrates hypotonia and lack of purposeful movements. EEG reveals multifocal epileptiform discharges with background slowing. MRI brain shows diffuse cortical atrophy and delayed myelination. Extensive metabolic and infectious evaluations are unremarkable. Pharmacologic seizure management is ongoing with limited success.
