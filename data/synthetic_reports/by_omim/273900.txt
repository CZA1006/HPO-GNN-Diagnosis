A 2-year-old male is brought for evaluation of easy bruising and recurrent petechiae. Parents report that he has experienced frequent nosebleeds and prolonged bleeding after minor injuries since infancy. There is no known family history of bleeding disorders.

Physical examination reveals multiple ecchymoses over the extremities and trunk along with scattered petechiae. Palpation detects mild hepatosplenomegaly. Complete blood count shows isolated thrombocytopenia with platelet counts around 30,000/µL, while white and red blood cell counts are normal. Peripheral smear reveals enlarged platelets with normal morphology. Bone marrow biopsy demonstrates normal cellularity with increased megakaryocytes. Coagulation studies are within normal limits.
