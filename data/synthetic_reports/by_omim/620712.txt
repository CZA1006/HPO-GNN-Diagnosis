Polydactyly-macrocephaly syndrome (OMIM:620712)

The patient is a 2-year-old male born with postaxial polydactyly of all four limbs and macrocephaly. Developmental milestones are mildly delayed. Physical examination reveals a large head circumference, hypertelorism, and broad nasal bridge. Skeletal survey confirms polydactyly with well-formed extra digits. Neurological examination reveals mild hypotonia and delayed speech. Brain MRI shows mild ventriculomegaly with no major malformations. Multidisciplinary follow-up is planned to address developmental and orthopedic concerns.
