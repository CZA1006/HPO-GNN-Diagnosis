A 6-month-old male is evaluated for feeding difficulties and congenital anomalies. He was born at term with low birth weight and required neonatal intensive care due to cyanosis and respiratory distress. Examination at birth revealed coloboma of the iris and choanal atresia that required surgical intervention.

On current evaluation, he has persistent feeding difficulties, growth retardation, and developmental delay. Additional findings include external ear anomalies with hearing impairment confirmed on audiometry, cardiac malformation (tetralogy of Fallot on echocardiography), and genitourinary hypoplasia. Brain MRI shows hypoplasia of olfactory bulbs. Parents report frequent hospital visits due to recurrent infections and poor weight gain.
