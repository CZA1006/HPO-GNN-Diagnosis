Cornelia de Lange syndrome 3 (OMIM:610759)

The patient is a 4-year-old female presenting with distinctive facial dysmorphisms, growth retardation, and developmental delay. Birth history includes low birth weight and feeding difficulties. Facial features include synophrys, long eyelashes, and thin downturned lips. Limb abnormalities include micromelia and fifth finger clinodactyly. Behavioral assessment reveals self-injurious behaviors and attention deficits. Echocardiogram is normal.
