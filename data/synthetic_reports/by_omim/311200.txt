A newborn female is referred for multiple congenital anomalies including oral clefts, polydactyly, and facial dysmorphism. Family history is non-contributory.

On physical examination, she has median cleft lip and palate, tongue lobulation, and multiple oral frenula. Postaxial polydactyly of the hands and feet is noted. Facial features include hypertelorism, broad nasal bridge, and micrognathia. Neurological exam shows mild hypotonia. Renal ultrasound reveals cystic changes bilaterally.
