Developmental delay, dysmorphic facies, and brain anomalies (OMIM:620535)

The patient is a 7-year-old male with global developmental delay, speech impairment, and distinctive facial features including a broad nasal bridge, epicanthal folds, and a prominent forehead. Birth and family histories are noncontributory. Neurological assessment reveals hypotonia and poor coordination. MRI brain shows corpus callosum hypoplasia and mild ventriculomegaly. Routine metabolic and genetic studies are in progress. Supportive interventions include speech therapy, occupational therapy, and special education.
