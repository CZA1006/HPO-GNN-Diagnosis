Immunodeficiency 119 (OMIM:620825)

A 4-year-old female with recurrent severe bacterial infections and failure to thrive is under evaluation. She has chronic otitis media, recurrent pneumonia, and oral candidiasis. Physical findings include lymphadenopathy and hepatosplenomegaly. Immunological workup reveals hypogammaglobulinemia with markedly reduced memory B cells and impaired vaccine responses. T-cell subsets and function are normal. Bone marrow biopsy is unremarkable. The patient is receiving immunoglobulin replacement therapy, antibiotic prophylaxis, and close infectious disease monitoring.
