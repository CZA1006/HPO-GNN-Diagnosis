Smith-Kingsmore syndrome (OMIM:616638)

The patient is a 2-year-old male with macrocephaly, developmental delay, and seizures starting in infancy. Physical examination reveals a prominent forehead, hypertelorism, and hypotonia. MRI brain shows megalencephaly with ventriculomegaly. EEG demonstrates generalized epileptiform discharges. The patient's seizures are partially responsive to antiepileptic drugs. Developmental interventions focus on motor and cognitive skills.
