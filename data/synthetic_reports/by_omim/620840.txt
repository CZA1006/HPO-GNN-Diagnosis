Premature ovarian failure 24 (OMIM:620840)

The patient is a 29-year-old female who presents with secondary amenorrhea starting at age 25, accompanied by menopausal symptoms including hot flashes and mood fluctuations. She experienced regular menstrual cycles until age 24. Family history is positive for early menopause in the mother. Physical examination is unremarkable with normal secondary sexual characteristics. Laboratory evaluation shows elevated follicle-stimulating hormone (FSH) and luteinizing hormone (LH) levels, along with low estradiol. Pelvic ultrasound reveals small, atrophic ovaries with reduced follicular activity. Thyroid and adrenal function tests are within normal limits. The patient has been started on hormone replacement therapy with regular follow-up.
