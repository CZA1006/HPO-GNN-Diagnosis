Combined oxidative phosphorylation deficiency 30 (OMIM:616974)

The patient is a 7-month-old female presenting with failure to thrive, hypotonia, and lactic acidosis. Neurologic evaluation reveals developmental delays and feeding difficulties. Laboratory measurements demonstrate elevated lactate and alanine levels, with reduced activities of multiple mitochondrial respiratory chain complexes on muscle biopsy. Brain MRI shows symmetrical basal ganglia lesions and cerebral atrophy. Supportive treatment includes nutritional and metabolic management with close monitoring.
