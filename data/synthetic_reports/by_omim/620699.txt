Maple syrup urine disease, type II (OMIM:620699)

A 2-year-old male with intermittent episodes of lethargy, vomiting, and ataxia triggered by infection. Birth history and early development were normal. Plasma amino acid profile shows elevated branched-chain amino acids and keto acids. Urine analysis is consistent with branched-chain ketoaciduria. Neurological examination during crises reveals hypotonia and tremor. MRI shows signal abnormalities in the basal ganglia and cerebellum. Dietary management and emergency protocols for metabolic crises are in place, with ongoing neurologic care.
