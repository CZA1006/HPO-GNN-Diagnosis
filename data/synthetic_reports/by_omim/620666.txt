Spastic ataxia 10, autosomal recessive (OMIM:620666)

The patient is a 15-year-old female with a history of progressive gait difficulties beginning in early adolescence. Initial symptoms included unsteadiness and frequent falls, progressing to pronounced ataxia and spasticity affecting the lower limbs. There is no family history of neurological disorders. Neurological examination reveals increased tone, hyperreflexia, and bilateral extensor plantar responses in the lower limbs, in addition to truncal ataxia and dysmetria. Magnetic resonance imaging of the brain shows cerebellar atrophy and distinct hypomyelination. Nerve conduction studies are normal. The patient receives supportive physical therapy and symptomatic treatment targeting spasticity.
