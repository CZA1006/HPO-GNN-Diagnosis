Thrombocytopenia 8, with dysmorphic features and developmental delay (OMIM:620475)

The patient is a 6-year-old male with chronic thrombocytopenia, characteristic facial dysmorphism, and mild developmental delay. Physical features include low-set ears, broad nasal bridge, micrognathia, and short stature. Laboratory workup confirms persistent low platelet counts with normal coagulation parameters. Bone marrow biopsy demonstrates normal megakaryocyte morphology. Platelet aggregation studies reveal functional defects. Neurodevelopmental evaluation reveals mild intellectual disability with speech delay. Management comprises bleeding prophylaxis, developmental support, and close hematologic follow-up.
