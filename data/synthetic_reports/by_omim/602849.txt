Muenke syndrome (OMIM:602849)

The patient is a 5-year-old female evaluated for abnormal head shape and developmental progression. She was born following a normal pregnancy, with cranial abnormalities identified soon after birth. Family history reveals her father had corrective cranial surgery as a child. On examination, the patient has brachycephaly with coronal craniosynostosis, mild hypertelorism, and midface flattening. Hearing evaluation demonstrates mild bilateral sensorineural hearing loss. Hands show mild brachydactyly and broad thumbs. Developmental milestones have been achieved on time, with normal cognitive function. A cranial CT scan confirms bilateral coronal suture fusion with no evidence of intracranial hypertension. Audiological testing corroborates the hearing deficit. Other systemic evaluations are within normal limits.
