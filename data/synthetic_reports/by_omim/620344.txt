Prolonged electroretinal response suppression 2 (OMIM:620344)

The patient is a 28-year-old female presenting with progressive visual impairment characterized by difficulty adapting to varying light conditions and frequent episodes of photophobia. Ophthalmic examination reveals decreased visual acuity and sluggish pupillary reflexes. Full-field electroretinography shows prolonged suppression of the electroretinal response following light stimulus, with severely reduced cone response amplitudes. Fundus examination identifies subtle pigmentary changes with no vascular abnormalities. Neurological examination is unremarkable. There is no family history of retinal disease. Supportive care with low-vision aids has been initiated.
