Immunodeficiency 123 with HPV-related verrucosis (OMIM:620901)

The patient is a 12-year-old male with a history of recurrent cutaneous viral infections characterized by extensive and persistent verrucous lesions on the hands, feet, and face. These lesions first appeared in early childhood and have progressively worsened despite topical and systemic treatments. Medical history includes recurrent respiratory infections but no significant autoimmune manifestations. Physical examination reveals widespread hyperkeratotic papillomatous lesions with areas of secondary infection. Laboratory evaluation demonstrates lymphopenia with impaired T-cell responses, and decreased natural killer cell activity. Human papillomavirus (HPV) typing confirms multiple HPV types in the lesions. The patient is managed with antiviral therapies, immunomodulators, and supportive care including meticulous skin care.
