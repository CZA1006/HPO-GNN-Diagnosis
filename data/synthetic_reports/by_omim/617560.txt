Spastic ataxia 8, autosomal recessive, with hypomyelinating leukodystrophy (OMIM:617560)

The patient is a 16-year-old female with a history of progressive gait ataxia, spasticity, and mild cognitive impairment. Early motor milestones were delayed. Neurologic exam reveals truncal ataxia, increased muscle tone, and hyperreflexia. Brain MRI exhibits diffuse hypomyelination and cerebellar atrophy. Nerve conduction studies are normal. Routine labs and metabolic screening are unremarkable. Symptomatic management includes physical and occupational therapy to address mobility and coordination difficulties.
