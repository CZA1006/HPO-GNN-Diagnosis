A 7-year-old female is evaluated for short stature, joint hyperlaxity, and frequent fractures after minimal trauma. Parents report delayed walking and multiple orthopedic consultations.

On physical exam, she has disproportionate short stature, ligamentous laxity, and mild kyphoscoliosis. Radiographs show abnormalities of the spine, epiphyses, and metaphyses with mild platyspondyly. Bone mineral density is mildly decreased. Neurologic exam is normal.
