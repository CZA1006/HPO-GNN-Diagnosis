Nephrotic syndrome, type 5, with or without ocular abnormalities (OMIM:614199)

The patient is a 7-year-old male referred for evaluation of significant proteinuria and edema. Birth history was unremarkable. Family history includes a maternal uncle with renal disease. Physical exam reveals periorbital edema, ascites, and hypertension. Urinalysis is positive for heavy proteinuria and microscopic hematuria. Serum albumin is low, and cholesterol is elevated. Renal ultrasound shows normal-sized kidneys. Ophthalmologic evaluation reveals subtle lens opacities. Kidney biopsy is pending. Supportive management including diuretics and blood pressure control is initiated.
