Polymicrogyria with or without vascular-type EDS (OMIM:618343)

The patient is a 4-year-old female with developmental delay, epilepsy, and skin findings suggestive of connective tissue disorder. Seizures started at age 2 years with tonic-clonic episodes. Examination reveals soft, hyperextensible skin with easy bruising. Neurological examination detects mild spasticity and motor delay. Brain MRI demonstrates bilateral polymicrogyria predominantly in the perisylvian regions. Skin biopsy shows decreased collagen density. Antiepileptic treatment and multidisciplinary supportive care are underway.
