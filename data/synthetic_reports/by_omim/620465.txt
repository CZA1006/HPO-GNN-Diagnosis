Epilepsy, early-onset, 3, with or without developmental delay (OMIM:620465)

The patient is an 8-month-old female with onset of seizures at 2 months, characterized by focal clonic and generalized tonic-clonic events. She has delayed developmental milestones, including limited head control and absence of social smiling. Neurological examination reveals generalized hypotonia and brisk deep tendon reflexes. EEG shows multifocal epileptiform discharges and hypsarrhythmia. Brain MRI is unremarkable except for mild delayed myelination. Extensive metabolic testing is normal. Seizures are partially controlled with multiple antiepileptic drugs. Multidisciplinary care includes developmental rehabilitation and seizure management.
