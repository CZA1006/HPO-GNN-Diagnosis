Parkinson disease 7, autosomal recessive early-onset (OMIM:606324)

The patient is a 29-year-old male with a history of progressive motor symptoms including resting tremor, bradykinesia, rigidity, and postural instability, beginning at age 24. There is no family history of Parkinsonism. Neurological examination reveals asymmetric limb rigidity, reduced arm swing, and a masked facial expression. Response to levodopa therapy is partial. Brain MRI is normal without evidence of structural lesions. Routine laboratory tests and metabolic screenings are unremarkable. Neuropsychological testing shows mild executive dysfunction. Genetic testing for known early-onset Parkinson disease genes is underway.
