Luscan-Lumish syndrome (OMIM:616831)

The patient is a 5-year-old male presenting with intellectual disability, macrocephaly, and distinctive facial features including a broad forehead and prominent nasal bridge. Speech delay and hypotonia are noted on neurological examination. Brain MRI reveals mild ventriculomegaly with no structural malformations. Behavioral problems include attention deficit and hyperactivity. Support includes educational and behavioral therapies.
