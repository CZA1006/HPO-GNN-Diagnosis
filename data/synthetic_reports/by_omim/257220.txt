A 7-year-old male is evaluated for progressive ataxia, dystonia, and cognitive decline. Initial symptoms started around age 5 with clumsiness and learning difficulties. Parents report frequent episodes of vertical supranuclear gaze palsy.

Physical exam reveals horizontal and vertical gaze abnormalities, dysarthria, and impaired coordination. Hepatosplenomegaly is present on abdominal palpation. Bone marrow biopsy shows foamy macrophages with lipid inclusions. Filipin staining of cultured fibroblasts demonstrates cholesterol accumulation.
