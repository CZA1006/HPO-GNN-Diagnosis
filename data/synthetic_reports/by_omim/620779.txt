Intellectual developmental disorder, autosomal recessive 82 (OMIM:620779)

The patient is a 3-year-old female born to consanguineous parents, presenting with profound developmental delay, hypotonia, and limited voluntary movements. Birth and neonatal history were unremarkable. Physical examination reveals microcephaly, facial dysmorphisms including hypertelorism and a broad nasal bridge, and poor visual tracking. EEG shows generalized slowing with occasional epileptiform discharges. MRI brain reveals simplified gyral pattern and cerebral atrophy. Metabolic screening is non-contributory. The patient requires assistance with feeding and mobility and is receiving multidisciplinary supportive care.
