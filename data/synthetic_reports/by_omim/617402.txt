Cutis laxa, autosomal recessive, type IIC (OMIM:617402)

A 4-year-old female presents with generalized loose, redundant skin, delayed growth, and joint hypermobility. Birth history was uncomplicated, but developmental milestones were delayed. Examination demonstrates characteristic wrinkled and pendulous skin, mild facial dysmorphisms, and scoliosis. Cardiovascular assessment identifies mild aortic root dilation without valvular dysfunction. Pulmonary function tests suggest mild restrictive disease. Skeletal survey reveals abnormalities including platyspondyly and metaphyseal irregularities. Multidisciplinary management includes respiratory support, cardiological monitoring, and physical therapy.
