Mitochondrial DNA depletion syndrome 13 (encephalomyopathic type) (OMIM:615471)

The patient is a 9-month-old male presenting with global developmental delay, hypotonia, and recurrent episodes of lactic acidosis. No significant family history is reported. On examination, the patient has poor head control, generalized weakness, and failure to thrive. Laboratory studies reveal elevated blood lactate and pyruvate levels. Muscle biopsy shows mitochondrial abnormalities, including ragged red fibers. Brain MRI reveals bilateral basal ganglia signal abnormalities and cerebral atrophy. Supportive management with metabolic and nutritional therapies is provided.
