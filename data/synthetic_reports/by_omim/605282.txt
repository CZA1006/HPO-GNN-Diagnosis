Temtamy preaxial brachydactyly syndrome (OMIM:605282)

The patient is a 3-year-old male referred for short fingers and toes noted since infancy. Birth history was unremarkable. Family history shows no similar limb anomalies. On physical examination, the patient exhibits brachydactyly predominantly affecting the thumbs and great toes, with preaxial polydactyly of the left hand. Additionally, there is mild facial dysmorphism including hypertelorism and a broad nasal bridge. Limb radiographs demonstrate shortened metacarpals and phalanges consistent with acromesomelic shortening. No visceral abnormalities are noted on systemic examination. Developmental milestones are age-appropriate.
