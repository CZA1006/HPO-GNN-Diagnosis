Birt-Hogg-Dube syndrome 2 (OMIM:620459)

The patient is a 35-year-old male presenting with multiple fibrofolliculomas on the face and trunk, a history of recurrent spontaneous pneumothorax, and family history of renal tumors. Skin biopsy confirms the diagnosis of fibrofolliculomas. Lung CT demonstrates multiple pulmonary cysts consistent with Birt-Hogg-Dube syndrome. Renal imaging reveals several small renal cysts but no masses. Ongoing surveillance includes dermatological care, pulmonary function monitoring, and renal imaging to detect malignancies at an early stage.
