Spinocerebellar ataxia, autosomal recessive 12 (OMIM:614322)

The patient is a 19-year-old female presenting with progressive gait ataxia, dysarthria, and mild cognitive impairment. There is a family history of similar symptoms in a consanguineous sibling. Neurological examination shows cerebellar signs including intention tremor, nystagmus, and limb dysmetria. Muscle tone and strength are preserved. Brain MRI reveals cerebellar atrophy predominantly involving the vermis. Nerve conduction studies are normal. Supportive therapies and regular neurological monitoring are ongoing.
