Arthrogryposis, distal, type 12 (OMIM:620545)

The patient is a 2-year-old female with multiple distal joint contractures present since birth, predominantly involving the fingers and toes. Early motor milestones are delayed due to limited joint mobility. Physical examination reveals camptodactyly, clubfoot, and thin, hypoplastic nails. Muscle tone is decreased distally with mild weakness. Radiographs show joint contractures without significant bone deformities. Management focuses on physical and occupational therapy and orthopedic interventions to improve mobility and function.
