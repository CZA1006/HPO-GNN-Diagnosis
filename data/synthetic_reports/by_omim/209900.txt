An 11-year-old male is brought to clinic for progressive visual problems and weight gain. Parents report he developed night vision difficulties around age 7, with worsening vision. He also has global obesity despite dietary interventions, and difficulties with learning and attention in school.

On examination, the child has central obesity, postaxial polydactyly of both hands, and mild developmental delay. Ophthalmological assessment reveals rod-cone dystrophy with attenuated retinal vessels and pigmentary changes. Renal ultrasound demonstrates mildly echogenic kidneys with reduced corticomedullary differentiation. Endocrine testing shows insulin resistance. Genetic evaluation has been recommended based on the syndromic presentation.
