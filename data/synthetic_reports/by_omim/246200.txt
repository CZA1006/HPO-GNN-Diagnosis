A 2-month-old female is referred for failure to thrive, distinctive facial features, and marked insulin resistance. Parents report excessive feeding but poor weight gain, with episodes of hypoglycemia and hyperglycemia. Physical exam reveals progeroid appearance characterized by prominent eyes, thick lips, low-set ears, and lipoatrophy.

On examination, she has generalized muscle wasting with thin extremities and acanthosis nigricans across the neck and axillae. Growth parameters are below the 3rd percentile. Laboratory testing reveals severe fasting hyperinsulinemia, elevated blood glucose levels, and dyslipidemia. Echocardiography is normal. Further metabolic and endocrine evaluation is ongoing.
