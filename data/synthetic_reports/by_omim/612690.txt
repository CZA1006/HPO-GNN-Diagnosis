Spherocytosis, type 5 (OMIM:612690)

The patient is a 5-year-old male presenting with chronic hemolytic anemia and intermittent jaundice. Birth history is unremarkable. Family history is negative. Physical examination reveals pallor and mild hepatosplenomegaly. Laboratory studies show increased reticulocytes, elevated lactate dehydrogenase, and spherocytes on blood smear. Coombs test is negative. Bone marrow biopsy shows erythroid hyperplasia.
