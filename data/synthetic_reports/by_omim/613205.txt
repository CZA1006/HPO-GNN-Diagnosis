LMNA-related congenital muscular dystrophy (OMIM:613205)

The patient is a 2-year-old male presenting with hypotonia, delayed motor milestones, and significant proximal muscle weakness. The family history is negative for neuromuscular disorders. Examination reveals a lack of head control, joint contractures mainly around the hips and knees, and mild facial weakness. Serum creatine kinase levels are mildly elevated. Electromyography shows myopathic findings. Muscle biopsy demonstrates dystrophic features with increased fibrosis and variation in fiber size. Cardiac evaluation reveals early signs of conduction defects without evident cardiomyopathy. MRI of the muscles shows fatty replacement of the proximal muscle groups. Ongoing multidisciplinary management includes physical therapy and cardiac surveillance.
