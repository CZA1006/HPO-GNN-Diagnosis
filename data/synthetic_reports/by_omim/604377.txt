Mitochondrial complex IV deficiency, nuclear type 2 (OMIM:604377)

The patient is a 6-month-old female presenting with failure to thrive, hypotonia, and lactic acidosis. She was born at term with no complications. Parents report poor feeding and delayed milestones, including poor head control. Family history is noncontributory. On examination, the infant is lethargic with generalized hypotonia and weak cry. Growth parameters are below the 5th percentile. Cardiac examination is unremarkable. Laboratory investigations reveal elevated blood lactate and pyruvate levels, with an increased lactate-to-pyruvate ratio. Skeletal muscle biopsy shows decreased activity of mitochondrial complex IV. Brain MRI demonstrates bilateral basal ganglia signal abnormalities and cerebral atrophy. Metabolic and genetic workup is ongoing.
