A 4-year-old female is assessed for pancytopenia and developmental anomalies. She has a history of easy bruising, recurrent infections, and mild growth retardation. Physical examination reveals café-au-lait spots, hypoplastic thumbs, and microcephaly.

Complete blood count shows bicytopenia progressing to pancytopenia. Bone marrow biopsy demonstrates hypocellularity. Chromosomal breakage tests are positive for increased spontaneous and induced chromosomal fragility. Genetic analysis for complementation group C mutations is underway.
