Congenital disorder of glycosylation, type IIbb (OMIM:620546)

The patient is a 10-month-old male presenting with failure to thrive, hypotonia, and developmental delay. Dysmorphic features include inverted nipples and broad nasal bridge. Laboratory investigations reveal abnormal serum transferrin glycosylation profiles indicative of a congenital glycosylation disorder. Brain MRI demonstrates mild cerebral atrophy and delayed myelination. Liver function tests are mildly elevated. Nutritional supplementation and supportive therapies are ongoing with careful monitoring.
