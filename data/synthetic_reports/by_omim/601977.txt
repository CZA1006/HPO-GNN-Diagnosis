Thrombocythemia 2 (OMIM:601977)

The patient is a 45-year-old female referred for hematology assessment after persistent elevation of platelet counts found on routine blood work. She reports frequent headaches and episodic tingling in her fingertips but denies bleeding or bruising tendencies. Family history is notable for her mother, who was treated for a similar blood disorder in her 50s. On physical examination, the patient is well-appearing with no organomegaly or lymphadenopathy. Complete blood count shows persistent thrombocytosis with platelet counts >800 × 10^9/L, while leukocyte and hemoglobin levels are within normal limits. Peripheral smear demonstrates large, mature platelets. Bone marrow biopsy reveals increased numbers of megakaryocytes without dysplastic features. Coagulation studies are normal. No secondary causes of thrombocytosis (such as infection or iron deficiency) were identified.
