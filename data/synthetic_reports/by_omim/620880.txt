Autoinflammation with arthritis and vasculitis (OMIM:620880)

The patient is a 9-year-old female presenting with recurrent episodes of high fever, painful joint swelling, and skin rash. Physical examination during episodes shows erythematous macules and palpable purpura. Laboratory workup reveals elevated C-reactive protein, leukocytosis, and anemia of chronic disease. Autoimmune panel shows positive antinuclear antibodies. Skin biopsy confirms vasculitis with leukocytoclastic features. Therapeutic trial with corticosteroids reduces symptom severity; immunomodulatory therapy is being considered.
