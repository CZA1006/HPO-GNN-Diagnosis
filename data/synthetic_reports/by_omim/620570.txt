Ciliary dyskinesia, primary, 52 (OMIM:620570)

The patient is a 7-year-old male with chronic respiratory infections, persistent productive cough, and nasal congestion since infancy. Chest CT reveals bronchiectasis predominantly affecting lower lobes. Nasal nitric oxide measurement is consistently low. Electron microscopy of nasal epithelial biopsy shows ultrastructural abnormalities of ciliary axonemes, including absent or dysplastic dynein arms. Pulmonary function tests indicate obstructive airway disease. The patient is managed with airway clearance therapies, prophylactic antibiotics, and regular monitoring for pulmonary complications.
