Immunodeficiency 133 with autoimmunity and autoinflammation (OMIM:620565)

The patient is a 9-year-old female presenting with recurrent infections, autoimmune cytopenias, and chronic inflammation. History includes frequent respiratory and skin infections, as well as episodes of fever and rash without clear infectious cause. Physical exam reveals lymphadenopathy and mucocutaneous pallor. Laboratory evaluation shows hypogammaglobulinemia, cytopenias, and elevated inflammatory markers. Immunophenotyping indicates impaired T-cell function and abnormal cytokine profiles. Bone marrow biopsy demonstrates reactive changes without malignancy. Treatment includes immunosuppressants, immunoglobulin replacement, and vigilant infection control.
