Weiss-Kruszka syndrome (OMIM:618619)

The patient is a 6-year-old female with developmental delay, hypotonia, and characteristic facial dysmorphisms. Clinical features include microcephaly, hypertelorism, short nose with anteverted nares, and a triangular face. The patient displays delayed speech development and mild intellectual disability. Neurologic exam reveals decreased muscle tone and delayed gross motor milestones. Brain MRI demonstrates mild ventriculomegaly without structural malformations. Feeding difficulties were noted in infancy but have improved. Supportive care includes early intervention therapies and occupational therapy to facilitate development.
