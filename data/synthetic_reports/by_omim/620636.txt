Neurodegeneration, childhood-onset, with cerebellar ataxia and cognitive decline (OMIM:620636)

The patient is a 6-year-old male with progressive gait ataxia, dysarthria, and cognitive regression. Early developmental milestones were achieved, but the patient began to lose motor skills and speech between ages 3 and 5 years. Neurological examination shows cerebellar signs, spasticity, and impaired coordination. Brain MRI reveals cerebellar atrophy and diffuse white matter changes. Metabolic and genetic testing are ongoing. Management focuses on symptomatic therapy and supportive care with physical and speech rehabilitation.
