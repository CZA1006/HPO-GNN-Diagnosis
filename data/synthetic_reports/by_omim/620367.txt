Pulmonary fibrosis and/or bone marrow failure syndrome, telomere-related, 8 (OMIM:620367)

The patient is a 35-year-old male presenting with progressive dyspnea, chronic dry cough, and fatigue. Pulmonary function tests demonstrate restrictive ventilatory defect. High-resolution chest CT reveals diffuse interstitial fibrosis with honeycombing. Physical examination reveals mild nail dystrophy and premature graying. Laboratory studies show pancytopenia and hypocellular bone marrow on biopsy. Telomere length analysis is pending. Supportive treatment includes oxygen therapy and hematologic monitoring, with consideration for bone marrow transplantation.
