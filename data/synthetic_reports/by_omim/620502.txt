Neurodevelopmental disorder with language delay and variable cognitive abnormalities (OMIM:620502)

The patient is a 7-year-old male with delayed speech acquisition, learning difficulties, and attention deficits. Birth history was unremarkable. On examination, no significant dysmorphic features or neurologic abnormalities are observed. Cognitive assessment indicates borderline intellectual functioning with executive dysfunction. Brain MRI and metabolic testing are normal. Early intervention services, including speech therapy and educational support, are in place.
