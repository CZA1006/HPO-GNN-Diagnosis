Loeys-Dietz syndrome 4 (OMIM:614816)

The patient is a 9-year-old female referred for connective tissue and vascular abnormalities. Physical exam reveals hypertelorism, bifid uvula, translucent skin, and hyperextensible joints. Cardiovascular examination shows aortic root dilation and mild mitral regurgitation. Vascular imaging reveals tortuosity of cerebral and abdominal arteries. Family history includes sudden cardiac death in a first-degree relative. Multidisciplinary follow-up is in place for cardiovascular surveillance and orthopedic evaluation.
