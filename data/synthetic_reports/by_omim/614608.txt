Coffin-Siris syndrome 3 (OMIM:614608)

The patient is a 5-year-old male with developmental delay, coarse facial features, and hypoplastic nails of the fifth fingers. Family history is negative. On examination, the patient displays intellectual disability, sparse scalp hair, and a broad nasal bridge. Growth parameters are below average. Radiological studies reveal delayed bone age and hypotonia on neurological exam. Brain imaging is normal. Multidisciplinary interventions including special education and physical therapy are recommended.
