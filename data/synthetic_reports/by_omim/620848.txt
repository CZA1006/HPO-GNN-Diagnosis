Spermatogenic failure 92 (OMIM:620848)

The patient is a 30-year-old male presenting with primary infertility. Medical and reproductive history is unremarkable except for delayed puberty. Physical examination reveals small, firm testes bilaterally with normal secondary sexual characteristics. Semen analysis shows azoospermia with absent sperm in multiple samples. Hormonal profile indicates elevated FSH and normal testosterone. Scrotal ultrasound excludes varicocele or obstructive lesions. Genetic and chromosomal evaluations are pending. Counseling regarding assisted reproductive options has been provided.
