Galloway-Mowat syndrome 4 (OMIM:617730)

The patient is a 4-month-old male presenting with microcephaly, nephrotic-range proteinuria, and developmental delay. Neonatal period was notable for feeding difficulties and hypotonia. Renal ultrasound shows echogenic kidneys with mild enlargement. Laboratory tests reveal severe proteinuria and hypoalbuminemia. Neurologic examination indicates poor head control and global hypotonia. Brain MRI demonstrates pachygyria and cerebellar hypoplasia. Supportive care includes renal management, nutritional support, and physical therapy.
