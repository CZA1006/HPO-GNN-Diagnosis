Ectodermal dysplasia 9, hair/nail type (OMIM:614931)

The patient is a 4-year-old female presenting with sparse, thin scalp hair and dystrophic nails. Family history is negative for similar features. Physical examination confirms fine hair texture, patchy alopecia, brittle nails, and dry skin. Teeth appear normal on oral examination. No abnormalities of sweat gland function or dental development are observed. Laboratory studies are within normal ranges. Skin biopsy shows abnormal keratinization of hair follicles and nail matrix changes. Management focuses on symptomatic dermatologic care and monitoring hair and nail growth.
