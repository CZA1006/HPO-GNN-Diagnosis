Acrofacial dysostosis, Cincinnati type (OMIM:616462)

The patient is a newborn male presenting with multiple craniofacial anomalies including micrognathia, cleft palate, and ear malformations. He also exhibits limb anomalies such as radial hypoplasia and absent thumbs. Prenatal history was significant for polyhydramnios and fetal growth restriction. Respiratory support was necessary due to airway compromise. Radiographs confirm skeletal dysplasia affecting the facial bones and upper limbs. Multidisciplinary care involves surgical correction, respiratory support, and feeding assistance.
