Auriculocondylar syndrome 2B (OMIM:620458)

The patient is a newborn female with characteristic facial anomalies including micrognathia, mandibular hypoplasia, and prominent ear malformations. Feeding was initially difficult due to glossoptosis. Radiographs reveal hypoplastic mandibular condyles and abnormal temporomandibular joint morphology. Respiratory support was required transiently postnatally. There is no family history of craniofacial malformations. Multidisciplinary management includes surgical evaluation for airway and feeding difficulties, along with developmental monitoring.
