Megalencephaly-polydactyly syndrome (OMIM:620748)

The patient is a 2-year-old male born with megalencephaly and postaxial polydactyly on both hands and feet. Growth parameters include macrocephaly with head circumference above the 98th percentile. Motor development shows delays with hypotonia and impaired coordination. Craniofacial anomalies include hypertelorism and broad nasal bridge. MRI brain reveals cortical dysplasia and ventriculomegaly. Cardiac and renal imaging are unremarkable. Early intervention services and orthopedic evaluations are underway.
