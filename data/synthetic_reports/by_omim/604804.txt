Microcephaly 3, primary, autosomal recessive (OMIM:604804)

The patient is a 1-year-old female referred for evaluation of microcephaly and developmental delay. She was born at term after a normal pregnancy and delivery but was noted to have a head circumference below the 3rd percentile at birth. Family history is notable for consanguinity but no known neurological disorders. On examination, the child demonstrates global developmental delay with poor head control and limited social interaction. Physical examination confirms significant microcephaly, generalized hypotonia, and mild facial dysmorphisms including a prominent nasal bridge and narrow forehead. Brain MRI reveals simplified gyral pattern with reduced brain volume. Routine metabolic and genetic screening are normal.
