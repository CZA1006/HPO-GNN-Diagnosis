Charcot-Marie-Tooth disease, axonal, type 2A2A (OMIM:609260)

The patient is a 19-year-old female with a history of progressive distal muscle weakness and sensory loss starting in adolescence. Family history is positive for similar neuropathy in the father. Clinical examination reveals distal muscle atrophy, foot drop, pes cavus, and diminished distal sensation. Reflexes are reduced in the lower limbs. Nerve conduction studies demonstrate decreased motor and sensory amplitudes consistent with axonal neuropathy. Genetic testing for type 2A2A mutations is pending.
