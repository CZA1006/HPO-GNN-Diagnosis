A 6-month-old male presents with feeding difficulties, hypotonia, and multiple congenital anomalies. Family history reveals a consanguineous marriage.

On physical exam, the infant displays microcephaly, syndactyly of the second and third toes, micrognathia, and hypospadias. Growth is below the 5th percentile. Laboratory testing shows low serum cholesterol and elevated 7-dehydrocholesterol levels. Echocardiography detects a ventricular septal defect.
