El Hayek-Chahrour neurodevelopmental syndrome (OMIM:620820)

The patient is a 6-year-old male presenting with intellectual disability, speech delay, and hypotonia. The pregnancy was notable for mild intrauterine growth restriction, and no family history of neurological disease is reported. Physical examination reveals subtle facial dysmorphisms such as hypertelorism, asymmetric ears, and a broad nasal bridge. Neurological assessment identifies poor motor coordination and impaired fine motor skills. Brain MRI shows mild cerebellar hypoplasia and delayed myelination. Behavioral issues include hyperactivity and social withdrawal. Early intervention includes physical therapy, speech therapy, and behavioral support.
