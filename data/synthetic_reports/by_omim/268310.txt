A 3-year-old male is evaluated for short stature, characteristic facial appearance, and limb shortening. Parents note delayed walking and difficulty with fine motor tasks. No family history of congenital anomalies is reported.

On examination, the child has mesomelic limb shortening, prominent forehead, hypertelorism, a broad nasal root, and midface hypoplasia. His hands exhibit brachydactyly with clinodactyly. Radiographs demonstrate vertebral segmentation anomalies and short, broad ribs. Neurologic examination is normal. Growth parameters are below the 5th percentile.
