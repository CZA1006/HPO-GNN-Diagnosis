Ehlers-Danlos syndrome, spondylodysplastic type, 2 (OMIM:615349)

The patient is a 7-year-old female presenting with proportionate short stature, progressive scoliosis, and joint hypermobility. Birth history was unremarkable, and developmental milestones were delayed but achieved. Family history is negative for connective tissue disorders. Physical examination reveals soft, hyperextensible skin, mild facial dysmorphism including midface hypoplasia, and bilateral genu valgum. Radiographs show platyspondyly, delayed ossification, and metaphyseal irregularities. Neurological examination is normal. Echocardiogram does not demonstrate any valvular abnormalities. Physical therapy and orthopedic management are ongoing.
