Cardiofaciocutaneous syndrome 2 (OMIM:615278)

The patient is a 5-year-old male presenting with distinctive facial dysmorphism, congenital heart defects, and developmental delay. Birth history was notable for polyhydramnios and low birth weight. Physical examination reveals a prominent forehead, bitemporal constriction, downslanting palpebral fissures, and sparse, curly hair. Cardiologic evaluation identifies pulmonic valve stenosis and hypertrophic cardiomyopathy. Neurological exam demonstrates hypotonia and mild intellectual disability. Echocardiography confirms structural heart abnormalities. Multidisciplinary management includes cardiology follow-up, developmental therapies, and genetic counseling.
