Glycine encephalopathy 2 (OMIM:620398)

The patient is a neonate presenting with hypotonia, apnea, and intractable seizures. Birth history is unremarkable. CSF and plasma analyses show elevated glycine levels. EEG reveals burst suppression and multifocal epileptiform discharges. Brain MRI shows diffuse cerebral atrophy and delayed myelination. The patient requires mechanical ventilation during acute episodes. Treatment consists of sodium benzoate and dietary management, with frequent monitoring of metabolic status.
