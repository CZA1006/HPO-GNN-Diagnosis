Microphthalmia/coloboma 11 (OMIM:620731)

A 3-year-old male presents with unilateral microphthalmia, iris coloboma, and visual impairment. No family history of ocular anomalies is reported. Ophthalmologic examination confirms decreased visual acuity and structural defects of the anterior segment. Systemic examination reveals no additional congenital anomalies. MRI brain reveals normal structures without intracranial malformations. Genetic and metabolic workups are negative. Visual rehabilitation and regular ophthalmological follow-up are planned.
