Spastic paraplegia 91, autosomal dominant, with or without cerebellar ataxia (OMIM:620538)

The patient is a 42-year-old female with a history of progressive lower limb spasticity accompanied by mild gait ataxia. Symptoms began in her early 30s and have gradually worsened. Family history is notable for a similarly affected parent. Examination demonstrates spasticity, hyperreflexia, and extensor plantar responses in the lower limbs, along with dysmetria and nystagmus. MRI brain and spinal cord reveal mild cerebellar atrophy without evidence of spinal cord pathology. Electrophysiological studies are unremarkable. Symptoms are managed with oral antispastic agents and physical therapy.
