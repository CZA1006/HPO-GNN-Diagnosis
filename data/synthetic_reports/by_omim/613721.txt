Developmental and epileptic encephalopathy 11 (OMIM:613721)

The patient is a 7-month-old female presenting with intractable seizures beginning at 2 months of age and profound developmental delay. Seizure types include tonic, myoclonic, and epileptic spasms. Neurological examination reveals axial hypotonia and poor visual fixation. EEG demonstrates a hypsarrhythmic pattern characteristic of infantile spasms. Brain MRI shows cortical dysplasia and delayed myelination. Metabolic screening and infectious workup are unremarkable. Genetic analysis identifies a pathogenic variant in a gene implicated in early infantile epileptic encephalopathies. Multimodal antiepileptic treatment provides partial seizure control.
