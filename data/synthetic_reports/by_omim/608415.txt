Prolonged electroretinal response suppression 1 (OMIM:608415)

The patient is a 27-year-old male with complaints of blurred vision and difficulty adapting to changing light conditions. He reports photophobia and slowly progressive vision impairment. Ophthalmologic exam reveals decreased visual acuity and sluggish pupillary light reflex. Full-field electroretinogram shows markedly prolonged suppression of the electroretinal response after light stimulus, with reduced cone response amplitude. Fundus examination reveals mild pigmentary retinal changes without vascular abnormalities. Other neurological exams are normal.
