A 2-year-old male is referred due to recurrent infections, easy bruising, and partial albinism. Parents report frequent upper and lower respiratory infections and persistent skin infections since infancy. They also note that his skin is lighter than expected for the family background, and his hair has a silvery sheen.

Physical examination demonstrates pallor, hepatosplenomegaly, and nystagmus. Skin is fair, with light brown hair streaked with silver. Neurological examination is grossly normal. Laboratory findings show pancytopenia with mild anemia, leukopenia, and thrombocytopenia. Peripheral blood smear demonstrates giant cytoplasmic granules in neutrophils and lymphocytes. Bone marrow aspirate is hypercellular with abnormal lysosomal inclusions.
