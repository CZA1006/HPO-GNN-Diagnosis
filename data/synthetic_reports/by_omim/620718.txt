Orofaciodigital syndrome XX (OMIM:620718)

The patient is a newborn female with multiple congenital anomalies including cleft lip and palate, polydactyly of hands and feet, and facial dysmorphisms such as hypertelorism and broad nasal bridge. Prenatal ultrasounds detected polyhydramnios and limb abnormalities. Physical examination reveals generalized hypotonia and feeding difficulties. Radiographic imaging confirms preaxial polydactyly and craniofacial skeletal anomalies. Neurological assessment notes delayed reflexes consistent with central hypotonia. Multidisciplinary care involving surgery, nutrition, and developmental therapies is underway.
