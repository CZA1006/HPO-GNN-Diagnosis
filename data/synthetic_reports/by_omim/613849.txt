Osteogenesis imperfecta, type XII (OMIM:613849)

The patient is a 7-year-old male presenting with frequent bone fractures from minor trauma and blue sclerae. Growth is below the 5th percentile. Family history is negative for bone disorders. Examination also reveals ligamentous laxity, dental abnormalities (dentinogenesis imperfecta), and mild hearing loss. Radiographs show multiple healed fractures with deformities and osteopenia. Bone densitometry confirms reduced bone mineral density. Laboratory investigations for calcium, phosphate, and vitamin D are normal. Management includes bisphosphonate therapy and orthopedic care.
