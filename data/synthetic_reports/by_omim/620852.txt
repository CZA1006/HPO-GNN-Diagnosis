Neurodevelopmental disorder with hypotonia, feeding difficulties, facial dysmorphism, and brain abnormalities (OMIM:620852)

A 7-month-old female presents with persistent hypotonia, poor feeding, and failure to thrive. Facial features include wide nasal bridge, epicanthal folds, and micrognathia. Neurological examination shows central hypotonia and poor head control. Brain MRI reveals ventriculomegaly and delayed myelination. Metabolic workup is normal. The patient requires nasogastric feeding due to swallowing difficulties. Supportive therapies include physical therapy and nutritional support.
