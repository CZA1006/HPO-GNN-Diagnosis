Basal ganglia calcification, idiopathic, 10, autosomal recessive (OMIM:621018)

A 14-year-old female presents with newly developed movement disorder features including rigidity and tremor. There is no reported family history of neurological disorders. Physical examination reveals bradykinesia, hypomimia, and mild cognitive impairment. Brain computed tomography reveals symmetrical bilateral calcifications in the basal ganglia, thalami, and dentate nuclei. Magnetic resonance imaging shows no significant additional abnormalities. Serum calcium, phosphate, and parathyroid hormone levels are within normal ranges. Metabolic and infectious etiologies have been excluded. Treatment is primarily supportive with physiotherapy targeting motor symptoms.
