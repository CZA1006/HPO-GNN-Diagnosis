Ehlers-Danlos syndrome, classic-like, 2 (OMIM:618000)

The patient is a 12-year-old female presenting with generalized joint hypermobility, skin hyperextensibility, and atrophic scarring. Family history is noncontributory. On examination, the skin is soft and velvety with multiple widened scars on the knees and elbows. Joint examination reveals laxity and recurrent subluxations, predominantly affecting the shoulders and knees. Echocardiography excludes significant valvular abnormalities. Physical therapy and protective joint care are part of ongoing management.
