Seckel syndrome 11 (OMIM:620767)

The patient is a 4-year-old female with severe microcephaly, growth retardation, and characteristic facial features including a beaked nose, receding jaw, and large eyes. Developmental milestones are significantly delayed. Neurologic examination reveals spasticity, hyperreflexia, and intellectual disability. Brain MRI demonstrates simplified gyral pattern and hypoplasia of the corpus callosum. Skeletal survey shows delayed bone age and abnormal skull shape. Supportive therapies include physical and occupational therapy, as well as developmental interventions.
