Multiple mitochondrial dysfunctions syndrome 4 (OMIM:616370)

The patient is a 6-month-old female presenting with profound hypotonia, developmental delay, and lactic acidosis. She was born after an unremarkable pregnancy but displayed feeding difficulties and failure to thrive in early infancy. Neurological examination reveals generalized weakness, poor head control, and minimal spontaneous movements. Laboratory evaluation shows elevated plasma lactate and alanine levels. Muscle biopsy indicates deficient activities of multiple respiratory chain complexes. Brain MRI demonstrates diffuse cerebral atrophy and delayed myelination. Supportive management focuses on nutritional support and seizure control.
