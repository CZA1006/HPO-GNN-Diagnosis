Glass syndrome (OMIM:612313)

The patient is a 5-year-old male presenting with intellectual disability and distinctive craniofacial features including broad nasal bridge, low-set ears, and micrognathia. Developmental milestones are delayed with poor speech and motor coordination. Family history is negative. Brain MRI shows mild ventriculomegaly and delayed myelination. Routine metabolic workup is unremarkable. Genetic studies are ongoing.
