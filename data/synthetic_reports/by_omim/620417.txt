Spastic paraplegia 90B, autosomal recessive (OMIM:620417)

A 17-year-old male presents with progressive lower limb spasticity, gait disturbance, and mild weakness. He was born to consanguineous parents. Clinical examination reveals spastic paraparesis, hyperactive reflexes, and positive Babinski sign bilaterally. Muscle strength is mildly reduced distally. Sensory function remains intact. MRI brain and spine are unremarkable. Neurophysiological testing excludes peripheral neuropathy. Supportive treatment includes spasticity management and rehabilitation.
