Immunodeficiency, common variable, 15 (OMIM:620670)

A 16-year-old female is evaluated for recurrent sinopulmonary infections since childhood. Past medical history includes otitis media and bronchitis requiring multiple antibiotics. Physical exam reveals mild cervical lymphadenopathy. Laboratory testing shows hypogammaglobulinemia affecting IgG and IgA subclasses with poor response to vaccines. Flow cytometric analysis demonstrates decreased switched memory B cells. Pulmonary imaging reveals early bronchiectasis. The patient is initiated on immunoglobulin replacement therapy and prophylactic antibiotics. Long-term surveillance focuses on infection prevention and pulmonary function preservation.
