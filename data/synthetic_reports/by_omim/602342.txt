Pierpont syndrome (OMIM:602342)

The patient is a 6-year-old male with global developmental delay and distinctive physical features. He was born at term following a normal pregnancy, with growth parameters small for gestational age. The family reports limited speech development, hypotonia, and delayed motor skills. Family history is negative for genetic or developmental disorders. Physical examination reveals coarse facial features, wide mouth with full lips, and deep-set eyes. He also has striking fat distribution with distinctive pads of fibrofatty tissue on the heels and distal fingers. Neurological evaluation shows hypotonia and limited fine motor control. Skeletal survey notes mild joint contractures but no bony malformations. Brain MRI demonstrates mild cerebral atrophy without focal lesions. Standard metabolic panels, thyroid studies, and chromosomal microarray were normal.
