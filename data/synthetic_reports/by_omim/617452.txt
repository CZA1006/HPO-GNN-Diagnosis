Intellectual developmental disorder with dysmorphic facies, seizures, and distal limb anomalies (OMIM:617452)

The patient is a 7-year-old female presenting with moderate intellectual disability, a history of focal seizures, and distinct distal limb anomalies including clinodactyly and brachydactyly. Pregnancy and birth were unremarkable. Dysmorphic features include hypertelorism, broad nasal bridge, and thin upper lip. Neurological examination shows hypotonia and mild ataxia. EEG monitoring reveals interictal epileptiform activity. Radiographs of hands confirm shortening and clinodactyly of the fifth fingers. Brain MRI is normal. Current management includes seizure control and supportive therapies.
