Xerosis and growth failure with immune and pulmonary dysfunction syndrome (OMIM:620510)

The patient is a 6-year-old male presenting with severe dry skin (xerosis), failure to thrive, recurrent respiratory infections, and immune dysfunction. Physical exam reveals widespread xerotic and scaly skin, growth parameters below the 3rd percentile, and hepatomegaly. Immunologic studies indicate hypogammaglobulinemia and lymphopenia. Pulmonary evaluation demonstrates bronchiectasis and restrictive lung disease. Supportive care involves immunoglobulin replacement, aggressive pulmonary hygiene, nutritional supplementation, and dermatological treatments.
