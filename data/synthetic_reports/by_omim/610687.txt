Nemaline myopathy 7, autosomal recessive (OMIM:610687)

The patient is a 1-year-old female with marked hypotonia and muscle weakness present since birth. She exhibits delayed motor milestones and poor head control. Family history is negative. On clinical examination, generalized weakness with facial muscle involvement is noted. Serum creatine kinase is normal. Muscle biopsy reveals nemaline rods on electron microscopy. Respiratory function is stable but closely monitored.
