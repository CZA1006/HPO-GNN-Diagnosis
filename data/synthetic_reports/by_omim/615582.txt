Loeys-Dietz syndrome 5 (OMIM:615582)

The patient is an 8-year-old female presenting with craniofacial abnormalities including hypertelorism and bifid uvula, along with hyperextensible joints and translucent skin. Echocardiography detects mild aortic root dilation with no significant valvular dysfunction. Vascular imaging shows tortuosity of cervical and abdominal arteries. Family history is positive for sudden cardiac death in a first-degree relative. Ongoing surveillance includes cardiovascular monitoring and orthopedic evaluation to manage joint laxity.
