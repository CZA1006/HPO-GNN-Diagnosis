Imerslund-Grasbeck syndrome 2 (OMIM:618882)

The patient is a 2-year-old male presenting with failure to thrive, recurrent infections, and megaloblastic anemia. Birth history was unremarkable. Laboratory evaluation reveals marked vitamin B12 deficiency with elevated methylmalonic acid and homocysteine levels. Urinalysis is negative for proteinuria but shows signs of tubular dysfunction. The patient’s renal ultrasound is normal. Neurological examination is normal. Vitamin B12 supplementation has led to improvement in hematologic parameters and growth, with ongoing monitoring for neurological sequelae.
