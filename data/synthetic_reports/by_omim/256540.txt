A 3-year-old male is brought for evaluation of developmental delay, coarse facial features, and hepatosplenomegaly. His parents report feeding difficulties and frequent respiratory infections since infancy. He exhibits hypotonia and delayed motor milestones.

On physical examination, the child has coarse facial features, including full cheeks and a broad nose. Abdominal exam reveals hepatosplenomegaly without tenderness. Neurological exam shows generalized hypotonia and diminished reflexes. Urine analysis reveals elevated oligosaccharides. Enzymatic assay indicates combined deficiency of beta-galactosidase and neuraminidase activities.
