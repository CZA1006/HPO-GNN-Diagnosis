Coenzyme Q10 deficiency, primary, 7 (OMIM:616276)

The patient is a 6-year-old male presenting with muscle weakness, exercise intolerance, and developmental delay. Neurological exam reveals hypotonia and reduced deep tendon reflexes. Serum lactate is elevated. Muscle biopsy shows mitochondrial abnormalities with decreased coenzyme Q10 concentration and respiratory chain enzyme activities. Brain MRI is unremarkable. Supplementation with coenzyme Q10 has been initiated with symptomatic improvement in muscle strength and endurance.
