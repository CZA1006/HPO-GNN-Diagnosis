Poikiloderma with neutropenia (OMIM:604173)

The patient is a 4-year-old female presenting with persistent skin changes and recurrent infections. The parents report a history of easy bruising, photosensitivity, and generalized rash since infancy. She has also experienced multiple episodes of lower respiratory tract infections requiring hospitalization. Family history is negative for similar conditions. Physical examination reveals poikilodermatous changes characterized by mottled pigmentation, telangiectasias, and skin atrophy over sun-exposed areas. There is noticeable generalized lymphadenopathy and mild hepatosplenomegaly. Laboratory studies reveal chronic neutropenia with an absolute neutrophil count below 1000/μL. Bone marrow biopsy shows normal cellularity without malignant infiltration. Immunological workup is pending.
