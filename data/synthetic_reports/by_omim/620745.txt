Deafness, autosomal recessive 123 (OMIM:620745)

The patient is a 5-year-old female presenting with congenital, bilateral, moderate to profound sensorineural hearing loss. There is no significant family history and no associated vestibular dysfunction. Physical examination is unremarkable with normal otologic findings. Auditory brainstem responses confirm bilateral hearing loss. CT imaging of the temporal bones shows normal cochlear and vestibular anatomy. No other neurological or systemic abnormalities are present. Early hearing amplification and speech therapy referrals have been initiated.
