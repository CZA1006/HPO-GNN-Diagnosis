Joubert syndrome 30 (OMIM:617622)

The patient is a 3-year-old female presenting with hypotonia, ataxia, and developmental delay. Examination reveals oculomotor apraxia and episodic hyperpnea. MRI brain demonstrates the classical molar tooth sign with hypoplasia of the cerebellar vermis and elongated superior cerebellar peduncles. There is mild renal enlargement without functional impairment. Ophthalmologic examination shows retinal dystrophy. Multidisciplinary management includes neurologic, renal, and ophthalmologic surveillance and therapies.
