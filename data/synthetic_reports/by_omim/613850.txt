Inosine triphosphatase deficiency (OMIM:613850)

The patient is a 34-year-old female referred for evaluation after adverse reactions to thiopurine drugs used for autoimmune disease management. She reports episodes of severe leukopenia and hepatitis following treatment initiation. Family history is negative. Laboratory testing identifies decreased inosine triphosphatase enzyme activity.  Medication regimens have been adjusted to prevent further toxicity.
