A 3-year-old female is referred for hepatomegaly and mild hypoglycemia. Parents report episodes of lethargy and pallor during prolonged fasting. Growth has been mildly delayed. No significant muscle weakness has been noted.

On examination, hepatomegaly is palpable 4 cm below the right costal margin. Laboratory studies reveal mild fasting hypoglycemia with elevated liver transaminases. Serum lactate and lipids are normal. Liver biopsy shows increased glycogen accumulation without fibrosis. Genetic analysis is underway.
