Cholestasis, progressive familial intrahepatic 4 (OMIM:615878)

The patient is a 6-month-old female presenting with persistent jaundice, pruritus, and failure to thrive. Birth history was normal. Physical exam reveals hepatomegaly and excoriations secondary to pruritus. Liver function tests show cholestasis with elevated direct bilirubin and alkaline phosphatase. Imaging excludes biliary obstruction. Liver biopsy reveals progressive intrahepatic cholestasis with bile duct paucity. Supportive care includes ursodeoxycholic acid and nutritional supplementation. Liver transplantation assessment is ongoing.
