Bleeding disorder, vascular-type (OMIM:620715)

A 9-year-old female presents with recurrent episodes of spontaneous bruising and mucosal bleeding since early childhood. Family history is negative for bleeding disorders. Physical examination reveals multiple ecchymoses on extremities and petechiae on the oral mucosa. Laboratory screening shows normal platelet count and coagulation profile. Platelet function assays demonstrate impaired adhesion and aggregation responses. Vascular imaging is unremarkable. Management includes avoidance of trauma, local hemostatic measures, and monitoring with hematology.
