Loeys-Dietz syndrome 3 (OMIM:613795)

The patient is an 11-year-old male referred for evaluation of connective tissue abnormalities and arterial aneurysms. Physical exam reveals hypertelorism, bifid uvula, and translucent skin with easy bruising. Joint hypermobility is present, along with scoliosis. Echocardiography shows moderate aortic root dilatation and mild mitral valve prolapse. Vascular imaging identifies tortuosity and focal aneurysms in cervical arteries. Family history is significant for sudden cardiac death in a paternal cousin. Multidisciplinary care includes cardiovascular monitoring and orthopedic evaluation.
