Short stature, optic nerve atrophy, and Pelger-Huet anomaly (OMIM:614800)

The patient is a 7-year-old male referred for evaluation of short stature and visual impairment. History includes delayed motor milestones and poor visual acuity noted since infancy. Physical examination shows proportionate short stature and pale optic discs. Complete blood count reveals Pelger-Huet anomaly on peripheral smear. Neuroimaging is unremarkable. Ophthalmological examination confirms bilateral optic nerve atrophy. Laboratory workup is otherwise normal. Management includes visual rehabilitation and growth monitoring.
