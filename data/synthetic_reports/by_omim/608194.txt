Cone-rod dystrophy 13 (OMIM:608194)

The patient is a 24-year-old female presenting with progressive visual decline, predominantly affecting central and color vision. She reports worsening difficulty seeing in dim light and narrowing peripheral visual fields. Family history reveals a maternal aunt with similar progressive visual loss. On ophthalmologic examination, visual acuity is reduced with abnormal color discrimination. Fundoscopy shows retinal pigmentary changes and attenuated vessels. Optical coherence tomography reveals thinning of outer retinal layers involving both cones and rods. Full-field electroretinogram shows decreased cone and rod responses.
