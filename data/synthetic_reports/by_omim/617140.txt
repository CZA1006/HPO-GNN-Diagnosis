ZTTK SYNDROME (OMIM:617140)

The patient is a 6-year-old male with severe intellectual disability, global developmental delay, and multiple congenital anomalies. Pregnancy and birth were complicated by intrauterine growth restriction and polyhydramnios. His physical features include microcephaly, hypotonia, facial dysmorphisms such as broad nasal bridge, hypertelorism, and a prominent forehead. Neurological examination shows spasticity in the lower limbs and persistent primitive reflexes. Brain MRI reveals corpus callosum hypoplasia, ventriculomegaly, and cerebral white matter abnormalities. Additional clinical findings include feeding difficulties, gastrointestinal dysmotility, and impaired speech development. The patient exhibits behavioral challenges including irritability and poor social interaction. Multidisciplinary care, including physical therapy, speech therapy, and nutritional support, has been initiated.
