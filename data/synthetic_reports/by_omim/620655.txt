Alfadhel syndrome (OMIM:620655)

A 7-month-old male infant presents with developmental delay, hypotonia, and feeding difficulties. There is no significant family history. Physical examination reveals facial dysmorphisms including frontal bossing, a broad nasal bridge, and micrognathia. Neurological exam shows generalized hypotonia and poor visual fixation. Laboratory evaluation detects elevated serum bile acids and mildly increased liver enzymes. Brain MRI demonstrates delayed myelination and mild cerebral atrophy. Supportive interventions include nutritional support and physical therapy.
