Immunoskeletal dysplasia with neurodevelopmental abnormalities (OMIM:617425)

The patient is a 5-year-old male presenting with disproportionate short stature, joint contractures, and global developmental delay. Birth history was complicated by intrauterine growth restriction and polyhydramnios. Physical examination reveals a dysmorphic facial appearance with prominent forehead, midface hypoplasia, and short neck. Skeletal survey shows vertebral flattening, short long bones with metaphyseal irregularities, and joint space narrowing. Neurologic assessment identifies hypotonia with delayed motor milestones. Brain MRI shows mild white matter abnormalities without significant cortical malformations. Multidisciplinary management involves orthopedic care and developmental support.
