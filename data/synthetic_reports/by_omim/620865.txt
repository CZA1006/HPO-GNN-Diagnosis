Ehlers-Danlos syndrome, classic-like, 3 (OMIM:620865)

The patient is a 14-year-old female with a history of hyperextensible, fragile skin and generalized joint hypermobility. She has a history of multiple joint dislocations and slow wound healing with wide, atrophic scars. Skin is soft and velvety in texture with easy bruising noted. Echocardiography shows no valvular abnormalities. Physical therapy focuses on joint stabilization and prevention of injuries, with genetic evaluation pending. Psychological support is provided due to chronic pain and physical limitations.
