Robinow syndrome, autosomal dominant 2 (OMIM:616331)

The patient is a 7-year-old male presenting with short stature, mesomelic limb shortening, and distinctive craniofacial features. Physical exam shows a broad forehead, hypertelorism, depressed nasal bridge, and a triangular mouth. Skeletal radiographs reveal vertebral segmentation anomalies and shortened ulnae and tibiae. Developmental milestones are mildly delayed but progressing. Cardiac evaluation is normal. Multidisciplinary care focuses on orthopedic and developmental support.
