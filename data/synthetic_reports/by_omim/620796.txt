Proteasome-associated autoinflammatory syndrome 6 (OMIM:620796)

The patient is a 2-year-old male with early-onset recurrent fever, diffuse skin rash, and arthritis. Episodes typically last 3–7 days with spontaneous resolution. The child exhibits failure to thrive and developmental delay. Laboratory studies during flares demonstrate neutrophilic leukocytosis, elevated acute phase reactants, and elevated liver enzymes. Genetic testing for common autoinflammatory syndromes is negative. Skin biopsy shows neutrophilic dermatosis. Immunological evaluation reveals impaired proteasome function. Management involves anti-inflammatory medications including corticosteroids and biologic agents targeting cytokines.
