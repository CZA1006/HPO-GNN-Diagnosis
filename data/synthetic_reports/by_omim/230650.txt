A 6-year-old female presents with slowly progressive motor decline and cognitive impairment beginning in early childhood. Her parents report that she achieved early developmental milestones appropriately but has since developed gait ataxia, muscle weakness, and speech difficulties over the past two years. There is also a history of recurrent respiratory infections.

On examination, the child shows generalized hypotonia with reduced deep tendon reflexes. Facial features include coarse facies with mild macroglossia and gingival hypertrophy. Hepatosplenomegaly is noted on abdominal palpation. Neurological exam identifies dysarthria and mild cerebellar signs. MRI demonstrates cerebral and cerebellar atrophy. Enzymatic assay shows reduced β-galactosidase activity.
