Hoxha-Aliu syndrome (OMIM:620662)

The patient is a 9-year-old female presenting with intellectual disability, short stature, and distinctive craniofacial features including broad nasal bridge and micrognathia. Developmental milestones are delayed, particularly speech and motor skills. Neurologic exam finds mild hypotonia without spasticity. Skeletal survey shows mild metaphyseal irregularities. Brain MRI is normal. Family history is negative. The patient receives ongoing developmental therapies and educational support.
