Neurodevelopmental disorder with impaired language, behavioral abnormalities, and dysmorphic facies (OMIM:620494)

The patient is a 9-year-old female presenting with delayed expressive and receptive language, autistic behaviors, and mild intellectual disability. She has subtle facial dysmorphisms, including a broad forehead, epicanthal folds, and a short nose. Neurologic exam is unremarkable aside from mild hypotonia. MRI brain is normal. Behavioral assessment identifies repetitive behaviors and social communication deficits. Speech therapy and behavioral interventions have been initiated with ongoing educational support.
