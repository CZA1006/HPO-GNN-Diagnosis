Developmental and epileptic encephalopathy 112 (OMIM:620537)

A 9-month-old female presents with treatment-resistant seizures originating in infancy accompanied by profound developmental delay. Clinical seizures include tonic and myoclonic types with frequent clustering. Neurologic exam shows hypotonia, limited purposeful movements, and lack of visual fixation. EEG reveals severe epileptiform activity with suppression-burst patterns. MRI brain demonstrates diffuse cerebral atrophy with delayed myelination. Extensive metabolic and infectious evaluations are unrevealing. Multidisciplinary care including pharmacological seizure control and therapy to support development is ongoing.
