Immunodeficiency 115 with autoinflammation (OMIM:620632)

The patient is a 7-year-old female who has experienced recurrent febrile episodes, rash, and persistent inflammatory markers since early childhood. She also has a history of recurrent respiratory infections. Physical examination during febrile episodes reveals erythematous skin lesions and swollen lymph nodes. Laboratory workup shows leukocytosis, elevated CRP and ESR, and cytopenias. Immunophenotyping reveals abnormal T-cell activation and cytokine dysregulation. Immunosuppressive therapy has been beneficial in reducing flare frequency. Regular multidisciplinary follow-up is in place.
