Desmosterolosis (OMIM:602398)

The patient is a 4-month-old female evaluated for hypotonia and developmental concerns. She was born after an uneventful pregnancy but noted to have microcephaly and poor feeding after birth. Growth is below the 5th percentile for age. Family history is notable for parental consanguinity but no known diagnoses. On examination, she exhibits global hypotonia, delayed visual tracking, and unusual craniofacial features including a broad nasal bridge, micrognathia, and high-arched palate. There is hepatosplenomegaly on abdominal palpation. Neurological exam shows poor head control and exaggerated startle reflexes. Brain MRI demonstrates lissencephaly-like cortical malformations and dysgenesis of the corpus callosum. Laboratory investigations reveal markedly elevated plasma sterol intermediates, consistent with a defect in cholesterol biosynthesis.
