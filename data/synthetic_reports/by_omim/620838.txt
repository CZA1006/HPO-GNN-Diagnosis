Spermatogenic failure 91 (OMIM:620838)

The patient is a 29-year-old male presenting with primary infertility after one year of unsuccessful conception attempts. Physical examination reveals normal secondary sexual characteristics but small bilateral testes. Semen analysis indicates azoospermia with normal hormonal profile apart from slightly elevated FSH. Scrotal ultrasound confirms reduced testicular volume without masses or varicocele. His medical history is unremarkable with no history of cryptorchidism or gonadotoxic exposures. Counseling about fertility options has started while further investigations continue.
