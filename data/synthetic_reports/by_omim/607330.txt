Lathosterolosis (OMIM:607330)

The patient is a 2-month-old female presenting with failure to thrive, hypothonia, and developmental delay. She was born at term to consanguineous parents. On examination, the infant has microcephaly, facial dysmorphism including hypertelorism and a broad nasal bridge, and hepatomegaly. Biochemical testing reveals elevated plasma levels of lathosterol and other sterol intermediates. Liver function tests are mildly abnormal. Brain MRI shows white matter abnormalities and mild ventriculomegaly. Metabolic testing is consistent with a defect in cholesterol biosynthesis. Genetic testing is ongoing.
