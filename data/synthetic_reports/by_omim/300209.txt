Simpson-Golabi-Behmel syndrome, type 2 (OMIM: 300209)
A newborn male is evaluated for macrosomia, congenital malformations, and hypotonia. Family history reveals previously affected male relatives with overgrowth and developmental delay.

Physical examination reveals coarse facial features with a prominent forehead, macroglossia, and broad nasal bridge. The infant has a large body habitus with organomegaly and polydactyly. Cardiac examination is notable for a ventricular septal defect on echocardiogram. Neurological examination shows generalized hypotonia and delayed reflexes. Genetic testing is planned.
