Fanconi anemia, complementation group I (OMIM:609053)

The patient is a 4-year-old male referred for pancytopenia and recurrent infections. There is a history of congenital anomalies including short stature, café-au-lait spots, and hypoplastic thumbs. Family history is unremarkable. On physical exam, he displays pallor, petechiae, and mild hepatosplenomegaly. Laboratory tests reveal pancytopenia with elevated fetal hemoglobin. Chromosomal breakage analysis shows increased chromosomal instability consistent with Fanconi anemia. Bone marrow biopsy shows hypocellularity without blasts. Genetic testing for complementation group I mutations is initiated.
