Tan-Almurshedi syndrome (OMIM:620641)

The patient is an 8-year-old female with developmental delay, characteristic facial features including broad nasal bridge, low-set ears, and macrostomia. She exhibits delayed speech and mild intellectual disability. Neurological examination reveals hypotonia and decreased fine motor skills. Skeletal survey identifies mild brachydactyly. No cardiac or renal abnormalities are present. Behavioral interventions and speech therapy have been initiated with ongoing developmental support.
