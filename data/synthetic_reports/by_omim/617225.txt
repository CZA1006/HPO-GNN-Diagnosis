Spastic paraplegia 78, autosomal recessive (OMIM:617225)

The patient is a 14-year-old male with a 2-year history of progressive lower limb stiffness and gait disturbance. Family history includes an affected sibling with similar symptoms. Clinical assessment reveals spastic paraparesis with hyperreflexia, clonus, and bilateral Babinski signs. Muscle strength is mildly reduced distally. Sensory examination is intact. MRI of the spine and brain is unremarkable. Electromyography and nerve conduction studies show no signs of peripheral neuropathy. Routine investigation including vitamin B12, folate, and metabolic screening is normal. Physical rehabilitation and symptomatic treatment are underway to preserve mobility.
