A 2-year-old male is brought to the hospital due to generalized edema and decreased urine output. Parents report the child has been increasingly lethargic and has abdominal swelling over the past week. No family history of kidney disease is known.

On examination, he is edematous with periorbital puffiness, ascites, and hypertension. Laboratory investigations show severe proteinuria, hypoalbuminemia, hyperlipidemia, and elevated serum creatinine. Renal ultrasound shows normal kidney size and echogenicity. A kidney biopsy is scheduled to clarify the etiology.
