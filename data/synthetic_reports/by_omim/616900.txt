Hypotonia, infantile, with psychomotor retardation and characteristic facies 3 (OMIM:616900)

The patient is a 9-month-old female born at term with early onset hypotonia and developmental delay. Birth history was unremarkable. On physical examination, distinct facial features including a broad nasal bridge and micrognathia are noted. Neurological evaluation reveals generalized hypotonia, poor head control, and delayed motor milestones. MRI brain shows nonspecific mild cerebral atrophy. Metabolic and infection screens are normal. Supportive care with physical therapy is ongoing.
