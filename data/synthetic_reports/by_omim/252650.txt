A 1-year-old male presents with significant developmental delay and progressive visual impairment. Parents report poor head control and lack of eye tracking. There is hypotonia and feeding difficulties. No organomegaly is present.

On examination, the infant has marked hypotonia, poor visual responsiveness, and is non-verbal. Ophthalmologic exam reveals corneal clouding and retinal degeneration. Brain MRI shows hypoplasia of the corpus callosum and cerebral atrophy. Biochemical testing reveals elevated lysosomal enzymes in plasma. Genetic studies are pending.
