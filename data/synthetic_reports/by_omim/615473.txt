Developmental and epileptic encephalopathy 17 (OMIM:615473)

The patient is a 5-month-old female with early-onset seizures characterized by tonic and myoclonic movements. Seizures are refractory to initial antiepileptic therapies. She exhibits profound developmental delay, poor visual fixation, and hypotonia. EEG reveals hypsarrhythmia and multifocal discharges. Brain MRI shows diffuse cerebral atrophy and delayed myelination. Metabolic workup is negative. Multimodal seizure management and supportive therapies are ongoing.
