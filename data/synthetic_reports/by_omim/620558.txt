Craniometadiaphyseal osteosclerosis with hip dysplasia (OMIM:620558)

The patient is a 7-year-old male presenting with diffuse skeletal sclerosis, craniofacial dysmorphism, and hip dysplasia. Radiographic imaging shows thickened cranial bones with widened metaphyses and diaphyseal sclerosis of long bones. Clinical features include frontal bossing, macrocephaly, and restricted hip mobility with pain. Developmental milestones are normal. Skeletal surveys reveal characteristic osteosclerotic changes primarily affecting cranial and long bones. Management includes orthopedic interventions aimed at improving hip function and monitoring for potential complications such as cranial nerve compression.
