Autoinflammatory syndrome, familial, with or without immunodeficiency (OMIM:619375)

The patient is a 5-year-old male with recurrent fevers, rash, and systemic inflammation. Episodes last several days and are characterized by fever, erythematous plaques, joint pain, fatigue, and lymphadenopathy. There is no family history of autoimmune or immunodeficiency disorders. Laboratory studies during flares show elevated inflammatory markers, neutrophilia, and mild anemia. Immunological evaluation reveals variable immunodeficiency features, including hypogammaglobulinemia and decreased T-cell subsets. Bone marrow biopsy is negative for malignancy. Treatment with anti-inflammatory agents and immunomodulatory therapy has reduced the frequency and severity of flares.
