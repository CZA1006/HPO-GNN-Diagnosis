Epidermolytic hyperkeratosis 2B, autosomal recessive (OMIM:620707)

The patient is a neonate presenting with generalized blistering and erythema at birth, evolving into thickened, hyperkeratotic skin with erosions. Family history is negative for similar skin conditions. Histopathology shows epidermolytic hyperkeratosis characterized by vacuolar degeneration of the upper epidermis. The patient is at risk of secondary infections and fluid loss. Management includes intensive wound care, infection prevention, and emollient therapy. Multidisciplinary follow-up is essential for growth and skin integrity monitoring.
