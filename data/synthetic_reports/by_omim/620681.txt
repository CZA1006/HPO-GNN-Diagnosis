Myoclonic epilepsy of Lafora 2 (OMIM:620681)

The patient is a 12-year-old female who developed myoclonic seizures and cognitive decline at age 10. Seizures are frequent, nocturnal, and refractory to multiple antiepileptic drugs. Neurological examination reveals myoclonus, ataxia, and progressive intellectual deterioration. EEG shows generalized polyspike and wave discharges. Brain MRI is unremarkable in early stages. Skin biopsy is pending. Supportive care includes seizure management and physiotherapy.
