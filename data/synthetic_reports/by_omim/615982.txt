Bardet-Biedl syndrome 4 (OMIM:615982)

The patient is a 12-year-old female with obesity, learning difficulties, and early-onset retinal dystrophy. There is no family history of similar conditions. Examination notes brachydactyly of the fingers, dental anomalies, and mild hearing loss. Visual examination reveals pigmentary retinopathy and reduced visual fields. Renal imaging identifies cortical cysts. Regular monitoring for endocrine, renal, and ocular complications is in place.
