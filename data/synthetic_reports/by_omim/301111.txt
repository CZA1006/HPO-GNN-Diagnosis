Intellectual developmental disorder, X-linked 112 (OMIM: 301111)
A 7-year-old male is evaluated for global developmental delay and moderate intellectual disability. Parents report delayed speech, poor social interaction, and learning difficulties. There is no family history of intellectual disability.

On examination, the child has subtle facial dysmorphisms including a broad forehead and mild hypertelorism. Neurologic examination reveals mild hypotonia and impaired fine motor coordination. Growth parameters are within normal limits. Brain MRI is unremarkable, and metabolic workup is normal.
