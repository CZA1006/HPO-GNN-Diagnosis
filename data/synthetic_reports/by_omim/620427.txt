Dystonia 37, early-onset, with striatal lesions (OMIM:620427)

The patient is a 7-year-old male who presented with early-onset dystonia characterized by involuntary twisting movements and sustained muscle contractions affecting the limbs and trunk. Symptoms began in infancy with progressively worsening motor impairment leading to significant functional restriction. Neurological examination demonstrates generalized dystonic posturing, impaired voluntary movement, and oromandibular involvement causing speech difficulties. MRI of the brain reveals bilateral symmetric hyperintensities in the striatum without evidence of atrophy or necrosis. Routine laboratory investigations, including metabolic and infectious panels, are unremarkable. Symptomatic treatment with anticholinergic agents and botulinum toxin injections is ongoing, alongside multidisciplinary rehabilitation.
