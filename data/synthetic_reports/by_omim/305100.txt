A 4-year-old male is evaluated for dry skin, sparse hair, and heat intolerance. Parents report that he rarely sweats and has recurrent episodes of hyperthermia during hot weather. There is a family history of similar features in maternal uncles.

On examination, he has sparse scalp hair, absent eyebrows, and thin eyelashes. Dental evaluation reveals hypodontia with conical-shaped teeth. Skin is dry and thin with decreased sweating noted on thermoregulatory testing. There is mild facial dysmorphism with frontal bossing and saddle nose.
