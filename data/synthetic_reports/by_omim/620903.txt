Polycystic kidney disease 8 (OMIM:620903)

A 26-year-old female reports progressively worsening hypertension and flank pain over the past year. Family history is significant for renal failure in a parent. Physical examination notes palpable bilateral flank masses. Laboratory studies reveal normal serum creatinine but mild proteinuria. Renal ultrasound demonstrates multiple bilateral renal cysts varying in size, with preserved kidney size and cortical thickness. MRI confirms cystic involvement without evidence of hepatic cysts or other extra-renal abnormalities. Blood pressure is medically controlled. The patient is undergoing regular nephrological monitoring for disease progression and management of hypertension.
