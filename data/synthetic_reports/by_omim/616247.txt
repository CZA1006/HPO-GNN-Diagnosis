Long QT syndrome 14 (OMIM:616247)

The patient is a 16-year-old male presenting with syncope episodes precipitated by exercise and emotional stress. Family history is notable for sudden cardiac death in a sibling during adolescence. Physical and cardiac examinations at rest are unremarkable. Resting ECG reveals prolonged QT intervals with intermittent T-wave abnormalities. Exercise stress testing induces ventricular arrhythmias consistent with long QT syndrome. Laboratory studies, including electrolytes, are normal. Beta-blocker therapy has been initiated with arrhythmia monitoring.
