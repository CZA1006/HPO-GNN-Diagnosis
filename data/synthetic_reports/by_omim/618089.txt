Intellectual developmental disorder with dysmorphic facies and behavioral abnormalities (OMIM:618089)

The patient is a 6-year-old female with moderate intellectual disability, delayed speech, and behavioral challenges including hyperactivity and anxiety. Examination reveals facial dysmorphisms such as frontal bossing, hypertelorism, and a broad nasal bridge. Neurological examination is notable for hypotonia and mild ataxia. Brain MRI is unremarkable. Management involves educational support, behavioral therapies, and occupational therapy.
