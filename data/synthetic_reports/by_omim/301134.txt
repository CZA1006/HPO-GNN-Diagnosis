A 6-year-old male is brought for assessment of developmental delay and speech impairment. Parents report limited vocabulary and difficulty in social interactions. There is no significant family history of neurodevelopmental disorders.

Physical examination is notable for subtle facial dysmorphism including mild hypertelorism and elongated philtrum. Neurological exam reveals hypotonia and impaired fine motor skills. EEG and brain MRI are normal. Metabolic screening is unremarkable.
