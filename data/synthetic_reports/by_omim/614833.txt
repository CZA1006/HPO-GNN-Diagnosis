Microcephaly, short stature, and polymicrogyria with seizures (OMIM:614833)

The patient is a 3-year-old female presenting with microcephaly, global developmental delay, and refractory seizures beginning within the first year of life. Pregnancy was reportedly uncomplicated, but microcephaly was noted on prenatal imaging. Physical examination demonstrates significant short stature and microcephaly with a head circumference well below the 3rd percentile. Neurologically, she shows axial hypotonia and impaired motor milestones. EEG monitoring reveals multifocal epileptiform discharges correlating with clinical seizures. Brain MRI demonstrates widespread polymicrogyria predominantly affecting the cerebral cortex. Metabolic and infectious workups are negative. Supportive care includes anti-epileptic medications and physical therapy.
