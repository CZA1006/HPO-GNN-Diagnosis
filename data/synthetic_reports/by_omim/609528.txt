Cerebral dysgenesis, neuropathy, ichthyosis, and palmoplantar keratoderma syndrome (OMIM:609528)

The patient is a 2-year-old female with developmental delay, ichthyosiform skin changes, and neuropathy. She was born from a consanguineous marriage. Physical examination reveals dry, scaling skin with thickened palms and soles keratoderma. Neurological examination shows hypotonia and decreased deep tendon reflexes. Brain MRI shows cortical dysgenesis with reduced white matter volume. Nerve conduction studies reveal sensorimotor neuropathy. Routine labs are within normal limits.
