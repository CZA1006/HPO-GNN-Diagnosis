Houge-Janssen syndrome 2 (OMIM:616362)

The patient is a 12-year-old female with intellectual disability, skeletal anomalies, and distinct facial dysmorphia. Clinical features include brachydactyly, joint hyperextensibility, and growth retardation. Neurological assessment reveals mild hypotonia with delayed motor milestones. Cranial imaging shows no significant structural abnormalities. Genetic screening and metabolic workup are pending. Multidisciplinary developmental support and monitoring are implemented.
