Holoprosencephaly 5 (OMIM:609637)

The patient is a newborn female presenting with midline facial anomalies and severe neurological impairment. Prenatal ultrasounds suggested brain malformation, and delivery occurred at term. Clinical examination reveals cleft lip and palate, hypotelorism, and a flat nasal bridge. Neonatal neurological assessment shows poor spontaneous movements and feeding difficulties. Brain MRI demonstrates alobar holoprosencephaly with absence of separation of the cerebral hemispheres and single dilated ventricle. Other systemic anomalies are absent.
