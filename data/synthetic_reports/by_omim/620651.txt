Diabetes, deafness, developmental delay, and short stature syndrome (OMIM:620651)

The patient is an 8-year-old male who presents with insulin-dependent diabetes mellitus diagnosed at age 5, bilateral sensorineural hearing loss, and delayed developmental milestones including speech and motor skills. He also demonstrates short stature below the 3rd percentile for age. Family history is negative for diabetes or hearing impairment. On physical examination, pale skin and mild facial dysmorphisms including a broad nasal bridge and micrognathia are noted. Audiological assessment confirms moderate to severe sensorineural hearing loss. Neurological examination reveals mild hypotonia and coordination difficulties. Laboratory evaluation shows poor glycemic control despite insulin therapy. Brain MRI is unremarkable.
