Neurodevelopmental disorder with language impairment, autism, and attention deficit-hyperactivity disorder (OMIM:620782)

The patient is a 9-year-old male with delayed speech, diagnosis of autism spectrum disorder, and attention deficit-hyperactivity disorder (ADHD). Parents report delayed language acquisition and difficulties with social communication. Behavioral assessment identifies impulsivity, hyperactivity, and restricted interests. Physical examination reveals no dysmorphic features. Neurological evaluation shows mild hypotonia without focal deficits. Brain MRI is normal. Educational and behavioral interventions have been instituted, and pharmacologic treatment is being considered for ADHD symptoms.
