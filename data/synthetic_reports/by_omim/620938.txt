Spastic paraplegia 93, autosomal recessive (OMIM:620938)

The patient is a 16-year-old female from consanguineous parents presenting with a 3-year history of progressive lower limb spasticity and gait disturbance. Early milestones were normal, but recent difficulties include frequent falls and muscle stiffness. Neurologic examination shows increased tone, brisk deep tendon reflexes, clonus, and bilateral Babinski signs in the lower extremities. Sensory examination is intact. MRI of the brain and spinal cord shows mild corticospinal tract hyperintensities without structural anomalies. Electrophysiological studies are unremarkable. Supportive care with physiotherapy and antispastic medications has been initiated.
