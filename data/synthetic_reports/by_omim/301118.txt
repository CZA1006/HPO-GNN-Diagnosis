Intellectual developmental disorder, X-linked syndromic 37 (OMIM: 301118)
A 6-year-old male with global developmental delay, seizures, and behavioral abnormalities is under evaluation. The child experiences frequent generalized tonic-clonic seizures despite antiepileptic treatment. Parents report hyperactivity and poor sleep patterns.

Physical exam reveals microcephaly and mild facial asymmetry. Neurological assessment confirms spasticity and increased deep tendon reflexes. EEG shows generalized epileptiform discharges. MRI brain exhibits hypoplastic corpus callosum and mild cerebral atrophy. Laboratory studies are unremarkable aside from mild anemia.
