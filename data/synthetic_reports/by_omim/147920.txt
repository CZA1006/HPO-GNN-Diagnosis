A 5-year-old female presents with developmental delay and feeding difficulties observed since infancy. She has distinctive facial features including long palpebral fissures with eversion of the lateral third of the lower eyelids, arched and broad eyebrows with sparseness in the lateral third, a short columella with a depressed nasal tip, and large, prominent ears. The child has mild hypotonia and short stature relative to age. Parents report frequent respiratory infections and chronic otitis media, leading to mild hearing impairment. There is a history of cardiac murmurs identified shortly after birth, with echocardiography revealing a ventricular septal defect and coarctation of the aorta. Musculoskeletal examination shows joint hypermobility, clinodactyly of the fifth fingers, and scoliosis.

Speech and cognitive development are delayed with mild intellectual disability. Dental examination reveals widely spaced teeth and hypodontia. Genitourinary anomalies including cryptorchidism have been recorded in male relatives. Feeding challenges continue with gastroesophageal reflux and episodes of failure to thrive. Immunological evaluation shows low levels of immunoglobulins and frequent infections suggesting immune deficiency.

Neurological assessment indicates hypotonia with some balance and coordination difficulties. Imaging studies reveal no major brain malformations. The overall clinical presentation includes multisystem involvement with characteristic craniofacial dysmorphisms, growth delays, skeletal abnormalities, cardiac defects, immunodeficiency, and developmental delays. Comprehensive genetic testing for confirmation has not yet been performed
