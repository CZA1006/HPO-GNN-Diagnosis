Noonan syndrome 3 (OMIM:609942)

The patient is a 7-year-old male with short stature, facial dysmorphism, and congenital heart defect. Birth history was unremarkable. Family history reveals a paternal uncle with mild similar features. Physical assessment shows ptosis, low-set ears, short neck, and sandal gap toes. Cardiac evaluation identifies hypertrophic cardiomyopathy. Growth parameters are below the 5th percentile. Developmental milestones are mildly delayed. Echocardiography confirms asymmetric septal hypertrophy.
