Neurodevelopmental disorder with progressive movement abnormalities (OMIM:620785)

The patient is a 6-year-old female with progressive movement disorder characterized by dystonia, chorea, and tremor, accompanied by global developmental delay. Birth history was unremarkable. Initial milestones were normal until regression began at age 2. Neurological examination reveals dystonic posturing, hyperkinetic movements, and impaired gait. Brain MRI shows diffuse cerebral atrophy and basal ganglia involvement. EEG is normal outside of movement episodes. Supportive management involves physical therapy, symptomatic treatment of movement disorders, and developmental support.
