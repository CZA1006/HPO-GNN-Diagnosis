Auroneurodental syndrome (OMIM:620830)

The patient is a 7-year-old female with characteristic dental anomalies including yellow-brown discoloration and enamel hypoplasia. She also presents with seizures, mild intellectual disability, and ocular abnormalities including strabismus and nystagmus. Physical examination reveals non-dysmorphic facies with normal growth parameters. Brain imaging shows mild cortical atrophy. Electroencephalography reveals generalized epileptiform discharges. Dental radiographs confirm enamel defects and delayed tooth eruption. Multidisciplinary care includes seizure management, dental rehabilitation, and developmental support.
