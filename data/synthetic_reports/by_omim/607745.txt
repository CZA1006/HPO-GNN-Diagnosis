Seizures, benign familial infantile, 3 (OMIM:607745)

The patient is a 4-month-old male with a history of afebrile seizures starting at 3 months of age. Family history reveals several relatives with similar seizure presentations in infancy. On examination, the infant has normal growth and developmental milestones. Neurological exam is otherwise unremarkable. EEG demonstrates focal epileptiform discharges but is normal interictally. Brain MRI is normal. Seizures have been predominantly brief, responsive to treatment, and have decreased in frequency over time. Metabolic screening is negative.
