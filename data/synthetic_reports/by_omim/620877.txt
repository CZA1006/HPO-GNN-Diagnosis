Deafness, autosomal recessive 125 (OMIM:620877)

A 3-year-old male is evaluated for bilateral, sensorineural deafness noted on newborn hearing screening. He has normal motor development and no history of infections or syndromic features. Otoscopic examination is normal. Audiological tests confirm moderate to severe hearing loss. Temporal bone CT reveals normal cochlear structures. The patient is receiving early hearing amplification and enrolled in auditory-verbal therapy.
