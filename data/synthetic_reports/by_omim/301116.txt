Intellectual developmental disorder, X-linked 113 (OMIM: 301116)
An 8-year-old boy is evaluated for moderate intellectual disability and autistic behaviors. Parents report social withdrawal, limited speech, and repetitive behaviors. There is a history of developmental delay since infancy. No familial history is available.

On clinical examination, the child has no major dysmorphic features. Neurologic exam reveals hypotonia and poor coordination. EEG is normal, and brain MRI shows no structural abnormalities. Genetic and metabolic workups are ongoing.
