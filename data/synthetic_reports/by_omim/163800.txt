A 68-year-old male presents with a history of episodic dizziness, fatigue, and occasional palpitations lasting several months. He reports experiencing multiple episodes of fainting and near-fainting, often occurring during physical activity or while standing. The patient describes episodes of rapid, fluttering heartbeats followed by periods of slow and irregular heartbeats. He also reports shortness of breath during exertion and occasional chest discomfort. There is no significant history of hypertension or coronary artery disease.

Physical examination reveals bradycardia with an irregular pulse. Cardiovascular exam is otherwise unremarkable with no murmurs or signs of heart failure. ECG shows sinus bradycardia with episodes of sinus arrest and prolonged pauses exceeding three seconds. Ambulatory 24-hour Holter monitoring reveals alternating episodes of atrial tachyarrhythmias such as atrial fibrillation interspersed with marked sinus bradycardia and sinus pauses. Exercise testing indicates chronotropic incompetence with inadequate heart rate response to exercise.

Laboratory evaluation including thyroid function tests, electrolytes, and metabolic panel are within normal limits. Echocardiography shows normal left ventricular function and no valvular abnormalities. No evidence of ischemic heart disease is found on stress testing.

The clinical picture is consistent with a disorder of the heart's natural pacemaker causing alternating episodes of abnormally slow and fast heart rhythms with symptomatic bradycardia, syncope, and exercise intolerance. Further diagnostic assessment and consideration of pacemaker implantation are planned.
