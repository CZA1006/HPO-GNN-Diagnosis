Bleeding disorder, platelet-type, 15 (OMIM:615193)

The patient is a 9-year-old male with a history of mucocutaneous bleeding including frequent epistaxis and easy bruising. Family history is negative. Physical examination reveals petechiae and ecchymoses without organomegaly. Complete blood count is normal apart from mild thrombocytopenia. Platelet function testing reveals impaired aggregation and secretion in response to multiple agonists. Coagulation studies are within normal limits. Bone marrow examination shows normal megakaryocytes. Management includes bleeding precautions and supportive platelet transfusions during bleeding episodes.
