Deafness, autosomal recessive 122 (OMIM:620714)

The patient is a 4-year-old male presenting with congenital, non-progressive, sensorineural hearing loss. Birth and family histories are unremarkable, with no known consanguinity. Audiological evaluation reveals bilateral profound hearing impairment confirmed by auditory brainstem response testing. Tympanometry and otoacoustic emissions are absent. The patient exhibits delayed speech development consistent with his hearing loss. Physical and neurological examinations are normal, with no additional dysmorphic features or systemic involvement. Early initiation of hearing aids and speech therapy has been implemented.
