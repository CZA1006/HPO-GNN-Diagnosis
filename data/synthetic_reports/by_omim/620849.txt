Spermatogenic failure 93 (OMIM:620849)

A 33-year-old male reports infertility after three years of unprotected intercourse. The patient has no history of genital infections, cryptorchidism, or trauma. Physical examination reveals microorchidism and absence of vas deferens bilaterally. Semen analysis demonstrates azoospermia with low ejaculate volume. Hormone levels are within normal limits. Imaging studies exclude obstructive azoospermia. Further testing is planned to explore genetic causes. The patient is referred to fertility specialists for management options.
