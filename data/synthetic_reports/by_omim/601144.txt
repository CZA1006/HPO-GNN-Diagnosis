Brugada syndrome 1 (OMIM:601144)

The patient is a 32-year-old male with no significant comorbidities, presenting with a history of recurrent syncopal episodes, usually occurring at rest or during sleep. He denies chest pain but reports occasional palpitations. Family history is notable for sudden unexplained death of his father at age 41 and a paternal uncle in his late 30s. Physical examination is unremarkable, with normal cardiovascular and neurological exams. Routine laboratory tests and echocardiography are within normal limits. However, an electrocardiogram performed during evaluation reveals episodic right precordial ST-segment elevation with intermittent conduction abnormalities. An exercise stress test and ambulatory Holter monitoring confirmed variable episodes of ventricular arrhythmia.
