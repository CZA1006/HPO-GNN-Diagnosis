Chitayat syndrome (OMIM:617180)

The patient is a 5-year-old male with dysmorphic features, developmental delay, and respiratory complications. He was born at term with low birth weight and exhibited hypotonia and feeding difficulty from birth. Dysmorphisms include micrognathia, broad nasal bridge, low-set ears, and camptodactyly. The patient suffered recurrent respiratory infections necessitating hospitalizations. Neurologic evaluation reveals moderate cognitive impairment with delayed speech development and mild spasticity in the lower limbs. Chest imaging shows chronic lung disease consistent with bronchiectasis. Supportive care includes respiratory physiotherapy, nutritional support, and developmental therapies.
