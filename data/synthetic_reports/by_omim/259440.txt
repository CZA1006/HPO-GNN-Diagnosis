A 10-month-old female is evaluated after multiple fractures with minimal trauma. Parents note increasing irritability and decreased mobility. She exhibits blue sclerae and dentinogenesis imperfecta.

On exam, growth parameters are below normal, with bone fragility evident. Radiographs reveal generalized osteopenia and multiple healing fractures. Laboratory workup shows normal serum calcium, phosphate, and alkaline phosphatase. Bone biopsy shows decreased bone matrix quality. Genetic testing is being planned.
