A newborn male is referred for evaluation of multiple vertebral and rib anomalies detected prenatally by ultrasound. He was born at term with mild respiratory distress requiring supplemental oxygen.

Physical examination reveals a short trunk with a chest deformity and limited neck mobility. Radiographs show multiple segmentation defects of the vertebrae, including hemivertebrae and fused ribs resulting in a narrow thoracic cage. Cardiac and abdominal ultrasounds are normal. Neurological examination is unremarkable. Genetic testing for mutations in the associated gene is underway.
