Craniofrontonasal dysplasia (OMIM: 304110)
A newborn female is evaluated for craniofacial abnormalities including coronal craniosynostosis and hypertelorism. Prenatal ultrasound detected skull asymmetry and midline facial defects.

On physical exam, she exhibits broad nasal root, bifid nasal tip, and coronal craniosynostosis with frontal bossing. Syndactyly of the hands is noted. Neurological examination shows normal tone and reflexes. Brain imaging reveals normal intracranial structures. Cardiac evaluation is normal.
