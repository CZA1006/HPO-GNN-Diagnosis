Chudley-McCullough syndrome (OMIM:604213)

The patient is a 2-year-old male referred for developmental delay and sensorineural hearing impairment. He was born at term after an uncomplicated pregnancy. Family history is negative for neurological disorders. On examination, the child has global developmental delays affecting speech and motor milestones. Hearing assessment confirms bilateral severe sensorineural hearing loss. Cranial ultrasound and subsequent brain MRI reveal bilateral polymicrogyria and partial agenesis of the corpus callosum. Fundoscopic examination is normal. There are no dysmorphic features or other systemic abnormalities. Routine laboratory investigations and metabolic screening were unremarkable.
