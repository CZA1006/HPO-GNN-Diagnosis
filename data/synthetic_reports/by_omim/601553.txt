Hypotrichosis, congenital, with juvenile macular dystrophy (OMIM:601553)

The patient is a 12-year-old boy brought in by his parents for progressive vision problems and longstanding sparse scalp hair present since infancy. The family reports that he had thin, fragile hair from birth, with no periods of normal growth. His maternal uncle also had early-onset baldness and severe visual impairment by adolescence. On physical examination, the patient exhibits sparse scalp hair, eyebrows, and eyelashes, with otherwise normal skin and nails. Ophthalmic exam shows bilateral decreased central vision, fundus examination with pigmentary changes at the macula, and OCT showing early degeneration of the photoreceptor layer in the central retina. Visual field testing reveals central scotomas. Routine blood work was normal, and no systemic anomalies detected.
