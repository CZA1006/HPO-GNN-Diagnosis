Intellectual developmental disorder with dysmorphic facies and ptosis (OMIM:617333)

The patient is a 7-year-old male with mild to moderate intellectual disability, speech delay, and characteristic facial features including bilateral ptosis, hypertelorism, and a broad nasal bridge. No family history of similar conditions is reported. Neurological examination shows hypotonia with preserved reflexes. Behavioral assessment notes attention deficits and social anxiety. MRI brain is normal. Early intervention services including speech therapy and special education have been initiated.
