Loeys-Dietz syndrome 1 (OMIM:609192)

The patient is a 12-year-old female with a history of aortic root dilatation diagnosed during evaluation for a heart murmur. Family history is positive for sudden cardiac death in a paternal uncle. On examination, she has hypertelorism, bifid uvula, and translucent skin with easy bruisability. Joint hypermobility and mild scoliosis are noted. Echocardiography confirms significant aortic root enlargement with mild aortic regurgitation. Vascular imaging reveals tortuosity of the cerebral arteries. Routine laboratory tests are normal.
