An 8-year-old male presents with disproportionate short stature and developmental delay. Parents noticed delayed walking and progressive stiffness of the spine. There is notable limited neck mobility and scoliosis. Cognitive development is mildly impaired.

On physical exam, he has short trunk dwarfism with barrel-shaped chest, short neck, and limited cervical spine movement. Joint contractures and genu valgum are observed. Radiographs show spondyloepimetaphyseal dysplasia with double-humped vertebral bodies and lacy iliac crests. Neurological exam is unremarkable. Genetic studies are being pursued.
