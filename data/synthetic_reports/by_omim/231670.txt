A 7-month-old male is evaluated for macrocephaly and delayed motor development. Parents noticed head circumference larger than peers since infancy. At approximately 6 months, he developed sudden onset hypotonia and dystonia after a febrile illness.

Physical examination reveals hypotonia with exaggerated startle reflex and dystonic posturing. Brain MRI shows frontotemporal atrophy with widened Sylvian fissures. Urine organic acid analysis demonstrates elevated glutaric acid and 3-hydroxyglutaric acid. Enzyme assay confirms deficiency of glutaryl-CoA dehydrogenase.
