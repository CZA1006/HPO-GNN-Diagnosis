Spastic paraplegia 45, autosomal recessive (OMIM:613162)

The patient is a 12-year-old female with progressive difficulty walking and lower limb stiffness developing over the past 4 years. Birth and early development were normal. There is a history of similar symptoms in a cousin. On neurological examination, lower limb spasticity, hyperreflexia, and bilateral Babinski signs are observed. Muscle strength is mildly decreased in the lower limbs. Sensory examination is normal. Spastic gait with scissoring is noted. MRI brain and spine are unremarkable. Electromyography and nerve conduction studies do not reveal peripheral neuropathy. Routine investigations including vitamin levels and metabolic screening are normal. Genetic testing is underway to confirm a hereditary spastic paraplegia diagnosis.
