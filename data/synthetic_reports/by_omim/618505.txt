Neurodevelopmental disorder with coarse facies and mild distal skeletal abnormalities (OMIM:618505)

The patient is an 8-year-old male presenting with global developmental delay, speech impairment, and distinct coarse facial features. The facial phenotype includes a broad forehead, synophrys, a bulbous nasal tip, and a wide mouth with thick lips. Mild distal skeletal abnormalities are noted, including brachydactyly and clinodactyly of the fifth fingers and toes. Neurologic examination reveals hypotonia and mild motor coordination difficulties without spasticity. Imaging studies, including skeletal surveys, confirm subtle shortening of distal phalanges without other skeletal malformations. MRI brain is within normal limits. Behavioral evaluation reveals mild attention deficit but no significant autistic features. Interventions focus on speech therapy, physical therapy, and educational support.
