A 4-year-old female presents with chronic mucocutaneous candidiasis, hypoparathyroidism, adrenal insufficiency, and growth retardation. Parents also report dental abnormalities, including delayed eruption of teeth and enamel hypoplasia. The child has experienced multiple hospital admissions for infections and electrolyte disturbances. No family history of similar illness is reported.

Examination shows pigmentation abnormalities of the oral mucosa and nails, hypoplastic nails, and short stature. Laboratory tests demonstrate hypocalcemia, elevated parathyroid hormone, and low cortisol levels. Bone X-rays reveal metaphyseal irregularities compatible with dysplasia. Autoimmune screens show positive antibodies against adrenal cortex and parathyroid glands.
