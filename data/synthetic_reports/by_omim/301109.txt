A 5-year-old male is referred for recurrent fevers, skin rash, and failure to thrive. Parents report episodes of high fever lasting several days accompanied by erythematous rash and joint swelling. There is a history of recurrent infections with poor response to standard therapies. Family history reveals an affected maternal uncle with similar symptoms.

On examination, he is underweight with diffuse erythematous maculopapular rash and synovitis of the wrists and knees. Laboratory analysis shows elevated acute phase reactants, anemia of chronic disease, and lymphopenia. Immunologic assays reveal impaired T-cell function. Imaging studies reveal hepatosplenomegaly.
