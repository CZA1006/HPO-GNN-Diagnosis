Cutis laxa, autosomal recessive, type IID (OMIM:617403)

The patient is a 5-year-old male with generalized cutis laxa characterized by inelastic skin and a prematurely aged appearance. Growth retardation and mild intellectual disability are present. Physical examination reveals facial laxity, joint hyperextensibility, and mild scoliosis. Cardiovascular screening shows borderline aortic dilatation and mild mitral regurgitation. Pulmonary imaging demonstrates early emphysematous changes. Skeletal abnormalities include vertebral flattening. Supportive management focuses on respiratory care, orthopedic interventions, and regular cardiovascular monitoring.
