Arrhythmogenic cardiomyopathy with variable ectodermal abnormalities (OMIM:620519)

The patient is a 35-year-old female presenting with palpitations, syncope, and a history of arrhythmogenic right ventricular cardiomyopathy. Cardiac MRI reveals fibro-fatty replacement of the right ventricular free wall and right ventricular dilation. Electrocardiogram shows epsilon waves and frequent ventricular ectopy. Dermatologic examination demonstrates mild palmoplantar keratoderma and variable hair thinning. Family history includes sudden cardiac death in a first-degree relative. Management involves antiarrhythmic medication, implantable cardioverter-defibrillator placement, and dermatology follow-up.
