A newborn female is admitted for erythroderma and failure to thrive. She presented with generalized red, scaly skin and brittle hair at birth. There is a history of recurrent skin infections and severe atopic dermatitis since infancy.

On examination, she has diffuse ichthyosiform erythroderma with areas of scaling and peeling. Hair shafts appear thin and fragile, with microscopic examination revealing trichorrhexis invaginata (“bamboo hair”). Growth parameters are below the 5th percentile. Laboratory tests reveal elevated IgE levels and peripheral eosinophilia.
