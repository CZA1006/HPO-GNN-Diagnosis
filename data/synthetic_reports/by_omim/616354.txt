Spinocerebellar ataxia, autosomal recessive 20 (OMIM:616354)

The patient is a 20-year-old female with slowly progressive gait ataxia, dysarthria, and limb incoordination over 5 years. Family history reveals consanguinity without overt neurological disease. Neurological exam identifies cerebellar signs with intention tremor, nystagmus, and truncal ataxia. MRI shows cerebellar atrophy affecting the vermis and hemispheres. Electrophysiological studies are normal. Supportive management and physical therapy are ongoing.
