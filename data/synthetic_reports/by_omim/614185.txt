Geleophysic dysplasia 2 (OMIM:614185)

The patient is a 4-year-old female with short stature, joint contractures, and characteristic facial features. She presents with a “happy” facial expression, thickened skin, and progressive cardiac valve thickening. Family history is negative for connective tissue disorders. Physical exam reveals brachydactyly, restricted joint movement, and hepatomegaly. Echocardiography shows mitral and tricuspid valve thickening with mild regurgitation. Skeletal survey identifies shortening of long bones and delayed bone age. Management focuses on cardiac monitoring and symptomatic treatment.
