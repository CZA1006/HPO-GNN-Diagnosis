Intellectual developmental disorder, autosomal dominant 7 (OMIM:614104)

The patient is a 6-year-old male presenting with developmental delay, speech impairment, and behavioral difficulties. The pregnancy was uneventful. Family history is negative for neurodevelopmental disorders. On examination, the patient demonstrates microcephaly, poor social interaction, and mild hypotonia. Cognitive assessment reveals moderate intellectual disability. Neurological examination shows hyperreflexia but no focal deficits. MRI brain is normal. Routine laboratory investigations are within normal limits. Supportive therapies including speech and occupational therapy are ongoing.
