Neurodevelopmental disorder with motor regression, progressive spastic paraplegia, and oromotor dysfunction (OMIM:620515)

The patient is a 5-year-old male who showed normal development until age two, followed by progressive loss of motor skills, spasticity, and speech decline. Examination shows spastic paraparesis with hyperreflexia, tongue fasciculations, and dysarthria. MRI brain reveals diffuse cerebral and cerebellar atrophy. EMG suggests chronic denervation. The clinical course is progressive with increasing disability. Supportive treatments include physical therapy, speech therapy, and symptomatic management.
