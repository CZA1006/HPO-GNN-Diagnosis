Ehlers-Danlos syndrome, musculocontractural type 1 (OMIM:601776)

The patient is a 9-year-old female referred for orthopedic evaluation due to recurrent joint dislocations and congenital contractures. Birth history was unremarkable, but she presented in infancy with bilateral clubfoot requiring casting. Parents report excessive bruising with minor trauma and delayed motor milestones. Family history is noncontributory. On physical examination, the patient has generalized joint hypermobility, multiple skin scars with a thin, hyperextensible texture, and flexion contractures at the elbows and fingers. She also exhibits mild scoliosis. Echocardiography was performed due to concern for connective tissue involvement, showing a borderline aortic root dilation but normal valve function. Imaging of the joints demonstrated mild hip dysplasia and ligamentous laxity. Routine laboratory investigations, including coagulation testing, were normal.
