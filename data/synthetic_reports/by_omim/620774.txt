Developmental and epileptic encephalopathy 114 (OMIM:620774)

The patient is a 6-month-old female presenting with early-onset seizures characterized by multiple seizure types including tonic spasms, clonic seizures, and myoclonic jerks. The seizures began within the first weeks of life and are refractory to several antiepileptic medications. Developmental milestones are significantly delayed, and the patient demonstrates poor spontaneous movements and absent social smile. Neurological examination reveals generalized hypotonia, poor head control, and absence of purposeful limb movements. EEG shows a marked suppression-burst pattern and multifocal epileptiform discharges. Brain MRI demonstrates diffuse cerebral atrophy and delayed myelination. Metabolic and infectious workups are negative. The patient is receiving supportive care including seizure management and physical therapy for developmental support.
