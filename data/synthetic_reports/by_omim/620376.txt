Autoinflammatory disease, systemic, with vasculitis (OMIM:620376)

The patient is a 12-year-old male who experiences recurrent episodes of fever, rash, arthralgia, and constitutional symptoms. Physical exam during flares shows palpable purpura and livedo reticularis. Laboratory evaluation reveals elevated inflammatory markers, anemia, and leukocytosis. Skin biopsy confirms small vessel vasculitis. Immunological workup detects low complement levels and variable immune dysregulation. Management consists of corticosteroids and immunosuppressive agents with close clinical and laboratory follow-up.
