Multiple congenital anomalies-hypotonia-seizures syndrome 2 (OMIM: 300868)
A 6-month-old female presents with profound hypotonia, seizures, and multiple congenital anomalies. Parents report poor feeding and abnormal jerky movements since infancy. There is no family history of neurological disorders.

On physical exam, the infant exhibits generalized hypotonia, dysmorphic features including micrognathia and low-set ears, and failure to thrive. EEG shows frequent epileptiform discharges correlating with clinical seizures. Brain MRI reveals cortical dysplasia and delayed myelination.
