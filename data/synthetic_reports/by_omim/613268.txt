Corneal dystrophy, Fuchs endothelial, 4 (OMIM:613268)

The patient is a 55-year-old female presenting with progressive bilateral visual impairment characterized by glare and blurred vision. Family history reveals a mother affected with corneal dystrophy. Slit-lamp examination shows bilateral corneal guttae and endothelial cell loss. Pachymetry indicates increased central corneal thickness. Specular microscopy confirms decreased endothelial cell density. Visual acuity is reduced with early signs of corneal edema. No history of ocular trauma or surgery is reported. Management includes monitoring and evaluation for endothelial keratoplasty if vision deteriorates.
