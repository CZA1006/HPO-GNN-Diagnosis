Kleefstra syndrome 1 (OMIM:610253)

The patient is a 5-year-old male referred for developmental delay, hypotonia, and distinctive facial features. Family history is noncontributory. Examination reveals brachycephaly, hypertelorism, flat midface, and thin upper lip. Behavioral abnormalities include autistic features and sleep disturbances. EEG is normal and brain MRI shows mild delayed myelination. Routine metabolic and genetic workup has been initiated.
