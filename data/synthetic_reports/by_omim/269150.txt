A 2-year-old female is referred for severe developmental delay, distinctive craniofacial features, and recurrent seizures. Parents report poor growth and hypotonia since infancy. Birth history was unremarkable.

On examination, she has midface hypoplasia with retracted nasal bridge, prominent forehead, and widely spaced eyes. Neurologic evaluation reveals profound global developmental delay and frequent myoclonic seizures. Radiographs demonstrate multiple skeletal anomalies including widened cranial sutures and spina bifida occulta. Brain MRI reveals ventriculomegaly and white matter abnormalities.
