Neuronopathy, distal hereditary motor, autosomal recessive 9 (OMIM:620402)

The patient is a 15-year-old male with slowly progressive distal muscle weakness and wasting, primarily involving lower limbs. There is no family history of neuromuscular disease. Physical examination reveals pes cavus, foot drop, and absent ankle reflexes. Sensory examination is intact. Nerve conduction studies show reduced motor amplitudes with preserved sensory responses. Electromyography supports a chronic denervation process. The clinical course is slowly progressive, with supportive management focused on mobility and orthopedic care.
