Spastic paraplegia 7, autosomal recessive (OMIM:607259)

The patient is a 15-year-old male with gradually worsening lower limb spasticity and gait abnormality. He was born after a normal pregnancy. Family history reveals a younger sibling with similar features. On neurological examination, there is increased tone, exaggerated deep tendon reflexes, and bilateral extensor plantar responses. Muscle strength is mildly reduced in the lower extremities. Sensory examination is normal. MRI spine and brain show no compressive or demyelinating lesions. Nerve conduction studies are normal. Laboratory evaluations including metabolic workup are unremarkable.
