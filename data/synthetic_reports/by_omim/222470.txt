A 6-month-old male infant is referred for severe diarrheal illness and failure to thrive. His parents report persistent intractable diarrhea since birth, requiring frequent hospitalizations for dehydration and electrolyte imbalances. Additionally, there is noted hair abnormality with woolly, brittle hair that has not grown normally. He has recurrent infections, including pneumonia and otitis media.

On examination, the infant is severely malnourished with weight below the 3rd percentile despite adequate caloric intake. His scalp hair is sparse, brittle, and easily pluckable. Hepatomegaly is noted on abdominal palpation. Laboratory studies reveal hypoalbuminemia and elevated liver enzymes. Immunologic workup shows lymphopenia and low immunoglobulin levels. Small bowel biopsy reveals villous atrophy. Genetic testing is pending for confirmation.
