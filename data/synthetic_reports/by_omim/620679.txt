Lipodystrophy, familial partial, type 8 (OMIM:620679)

The patient is a 28-year-old female with progressive loss of subcutaneous fat in the limbs and trunk onset in early adulthood. She reports insulin resistance and hypertriglyceridemia. Physical examination reveals prominent muscularity due to fat loss, acanthosis nigricans, and hepatic enlargement. Laboratory evaluations confirm dyslipidemia and hyperglycemia. Liver imaging shows steatosis without fibrosis. Management includes insulin sensitizers, lipid-lowering agents, dietary modifications, and monitoring for metabolic complications.
