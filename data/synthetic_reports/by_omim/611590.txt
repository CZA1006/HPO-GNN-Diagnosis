Distal renal tubular acidosis 4 with hemolytic anemia (OMIM:611590)

The patient is a 3-year-old female presenting with failure to thrive, polyuria, and pallor. Family history is negative for renal or hematologic disorders. Laboratory analyses reveal metabolic acidosis with a normal anion gap, hypokalemia, and hemolytic anemia with elevated reticulocyte count and indirect bilirubin. Urinalysis shows alkaline urine. Renal ultrasound shows nephrocalcinosis. Genetic testing is in progress.
