Spermatogenic failure, X-linked, 8, susceptibility to (OMIM: 301119)
A 25-year-old male presents for infertility evaluation after 2 years of unsuccessful attempts to conceive. He denies any significant past medical history. Family history reveals several male relatives with fertility issues but no known genetic disorders.

On physical examination, testicular size is at the lower limit of normal with normal secondary sexual characteristics. Semen analysis shows severe oligospermia with impaired sperm motility. Hormonal assays, including FSH, LH, and testosterone, are within normal ranges. Testicular biopsy reveals maturation arrest with absence of mature spermatozoa. No structural abnormalities are detected on scrotal ultrasound.
