Neurodevelopmental disorder with hypotonia and seizures (OMIM:620790)

A 9-month-old female infant was evaluated for delayed developmental milestones and recurrent seizures beginning at 3 months of age. Birth history was normal, and there is no known family history of neurological disorders. The patient exhibited global hypotonia and poor head control. Clinical seizures included tonic and clonic movements affecting limbs, often clustering in series. EEG revealed multifocal epileptiform discharges with diffuse slowing. Brain MRI demonstrated cortical dysplasia with delayed myelination and mild cerebral atrophy. Metabolic screens were negative. The patient is managed with multiple antiepileptic drugs, physical therapy, and developmental support services.
