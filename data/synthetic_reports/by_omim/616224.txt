Myasthenic syndrome, congenital, 22 (OMIM:616224)

The patient is a 2-year-old female with congenital hypotonia, episodic muscle weakness, and fatigability evident soon after birth. There is no family history of neuromuscular disorders. Examination shows ptosis, facial weakness, and proximal limb weakness with fatigability. Electrophysiological studies reveal decreased compound muscle action potentials with post-exercise decrement, suggestive of neuromuscular junction dysfunction. Muscle biopsy shows features consistent with congenital myasthenic syndrome. She is receiving symptomatic treatment with acetylcholinesterase inhibitors and supportive therapies.
