Intellectual developmental disorder, autosomal dominant 75 (OMIM:620988)

A 10-year-old male is evaluated for moderate intellectual disability and speech delay. There is no relevant family history or prenatal complications. Physical examination reveals mild dysmorphic features including a broad nasal bridge, thin upper lip, and prominent chin. Neurological assessment shows hypotonia and reduced coordination. Brain MRI is normal. Behavioral evaluation demonstrates attention difficulties and social anxiety. Early intervention therapies including speech, occupational, and behavioral therapy have been established. Routine metabolic and genetic evaluations are ongoing.
