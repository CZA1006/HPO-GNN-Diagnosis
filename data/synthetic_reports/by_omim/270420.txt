A newborn female is admitted for severe, persistent watery diarrhea since birth, resulting in dehydration and electrolyte imbalances. There is failure to thrive despite nutritional support. Family history is non-contributory.

On physical examination, she is dehydrated with sunken eyes and dry mucous membranes. Laboratory analyses reveal hyponatremia, metabolic acidosis, and elevated stool sodium concentration consistent with secretory diarrhea. Renal ultrasound is normal. Small bowel biopsy shows nonspecific villous atrophy.
