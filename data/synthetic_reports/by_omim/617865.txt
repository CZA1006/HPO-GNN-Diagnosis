Neurodevelopmental disorder with movement abnormalities, abnormal gait, and autistic features (OMIM:617865)

The patient is an 8-year-old male with delayed speech, motor impairment, and behavioral issues consistent with autism spectrum disorder. He walks with an unsteady, wide-based gait and exhibits abnormal repetitive movements. Family history is unremarkable. Physical examination reveals hypotonia and mild joint hypermobility. Neurologic evaluation shows impaired coordination and mild dysmetria. Brain imaging is reportedly normal. Interventions focus on behavioral therapies, motor skill development, and social skills training.
