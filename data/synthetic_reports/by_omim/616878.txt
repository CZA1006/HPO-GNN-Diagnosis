Metabolic encephalomyopathic crises, recurrent, with rhabdomyolysis, cardiac arrhythmias, and neurodegeneration (OMIM:616878)

The patient is a 2-year-old male presenting with recurrent episodes of encephalopathy, muscle weakness, and cardiac arrhythmias. Crises are often triggered by febrile illness or fasting. Physical exam during crises reveals hypotonia and decreased responsiveness. Laboratory evaluation during episodes shows elevated creatine kinase, metabolic acidosis, and electrolyte disturbances. ECG reveals ventricular arrhythmias. Between crises, the patient displays developmental delay and mild muscle weakness. Brain MRI shows progressive white matter changes. Supportive management includes metabolic stabilization, arrhythmia monitoring, and avoidance of fasting.
