A 22-year-old female presents with gait disturbance, resting tremor, and rigidity progressing over 3 years. There is no significant family history of movement disorders. The patient denies cognitive changes or psychiatric symptoms.

On neurological exam, she has asymmetric resting tremor predominantly affecting the right hand, bradykinesia, cogwheel rigidity, and postural instability. Reflexes are normal and there is no cerebellar involvement. Brain MRI is unremarkable. Dopamine transporter imaging shows decreased uptake in the basal ganglia. Laboratory investigations including copper and ceruloplasmin levels are normal.
