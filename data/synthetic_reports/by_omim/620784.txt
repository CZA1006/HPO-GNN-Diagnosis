Neurodevelopmental disorder plus optic atrophy (OMIM:620784)

The patient is a 5-year-old male presenting with developmental delay, poor visual acuity, and bilateral optic atrophy noted on ophthalmologic examination. There is a family history of vision impairment. Neurological examination reveals hypotonia with mild muscle weakness. Visual evoked potentials show delayed responses consistent with optic nerve dysfunction. Brain MRI demonstrates thinning of the optic nerves and mild cerebral atrophy. Metabolic testing is negative. Supportive care includes visual rehabilitation and developmental therapies.
