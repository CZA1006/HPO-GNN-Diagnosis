Al Kaissi syndrome (OMIM:617694)

The patient is a 6-year-old female with severe short stature, multiple skeletal anomalies, and developmental delay. Birth history was complicated by polyhydramnios and intrauterine growth restriction. Clinical examination reveals a short neck, broad nasal bridge, micrognathia, joint contractures, and scoliosis. Radiographic imaging demonstrates platyspondyly, metaphyseal irregularities, and brachydactyly. Neurologic assessment shows hypotonia with delayed developmental milestones. Multidisciplinary management includes orthopedic care, physical therapy, and close developmental monitoring.
