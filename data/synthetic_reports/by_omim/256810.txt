A 4-month-old female presents with progressive hypotonia, failure to thrive, and liver dysfunction. Parents report poor feeding and decreased activity over the past month. There is no family history of similar illness.

On examination, the infant is lethargic with marked hypotonia and hepatomegaly. Liver enzymes are markedly elevated; coagulation profile shows prolongation. Serum lactate is increased. Brain MRI demonstrates cerebral atrophy and delayed myelination. Muscle biopsy reveals ragged-red fibers and decreased mitochondrial DNA content.
