Garg-Mishra progeroid syndrome (OMIM:620601)

The patient is a 5-year-old female with features of accelerated aging, growth retardation, and distinctive craniofacial characteristics including frontal bossing, micrognathia, and beaked nose. She presents with thin, sparse hair and marked skin wrinkling. Developmental milestones are delayed, with hypotonia and feeding difficulties. Skeletal survey reveals osteoporosis and metaphyseal irregularities. Laboratory tests show normal metabolic panel. Supportive management includes nutritional support and physical therapy, with ongoing endocrinologic and orthopedic surveillance.
