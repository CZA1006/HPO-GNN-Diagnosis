Trichothiodystrophy 2, photosensitive (OMIM:616390)

The patient is a 3-year-old male with brittle hair, photosensitivity, and developmental delay. Parents report hair thinning and scalp scaling since infancy. On physical examination, hair microscopy reveals characteristic alternating light and dark bands (tiger-tail pattern), consistent with brittle hair disorder. Skin shows erythema and scaling on sun-exposed areas. Neurologic examination reveals mild hypotonia and delayed motor milestones. Additional findings include ichthyosis and nail dystrophy. Management involves sun protection, dermatologic care, and developmental support.
