Amelogenesis imperfecta, type IJ (OMIM:617297)

The patient is a 10-year-old male presenting with generalized enamel hypoplasia, enamel pitting, and increased tooth sensitivity. Family history is negative for dental abnormalities. Oral examination reveals yellow-brown discoloration with enamel that is thin and fragile leading to rapid wear and frequent fractures. Radiographs confirm loss of enamel thickness and relative normality of dentin. The patient reports difficulty eating and speech articulation issues. Dental restorations are planned alongside regular monitoring for secondary caries and periodontal disease. Preventive oral hygiene education is provided.
