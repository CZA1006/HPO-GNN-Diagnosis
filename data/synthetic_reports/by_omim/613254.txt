Tuberous sclerosis-2 (OMIM:613254)

The patient is a 3-year-old female presenting with seizures, developmental delay, and hypopigmented skin lesions noted since infancy. There is no family history of neurological disorders. On examination, multiple hypomelanotic macules (“ash leaf spots”) are seen on the trunk and limbs. Neurological evaluation shows global delay with poor language acquisition. MRI brain demonstrates subependymal nodules and cortical tubers consistent with tuberous sclerosis complex. Cardiac evaluation reveals rhabdomyomas. Ophthalmologic examination is normal. 

Treatment includes antiepileptic therapy and multidisciplinary follow-up.
