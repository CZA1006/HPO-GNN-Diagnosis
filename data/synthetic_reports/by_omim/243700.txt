A 10-year-old female presents with recurrent skin abscesses, pneumonia, and fungal infections since infancy. Parents report multiple episodes of eczema-like dermatitis and recurrent candidiasis. There is a history of delayed wound healing and persistent coarse facial features. No significant family history of immunodeficiency is noted.

Physical examination reveals coarse facies with a broad nasal bridge, deep-set eyes, and prominent forehead. The skin shows eczema and scarring from previous abscesses. Laboratory studies demonstrate markedly elevated serum IgE levels, eosinophilia, and impaired neutrophil chemotaxis. Chest imaging reveals bronchiectasis consistent with chronic pulmonary infections. Lymphocyte subsets and serum immunoglobulins aside from IgE are otherwise normal.
