Structural heart defects and renal anomalies syndrome (OMIM:617478)

The patient is a newborn male with multiple congenital anomalies including a ventricular septal defect and bilateral renal hypoplasia. Prenatal diagnosis raised suspicion due to abnormal ultrasounds. Postnatal echocardiography confirms the presence of a moderate ventricular septal defect with left-to-right shunting. Renal ultrasound demonstrates small, hyperechoic kidneys with poor corticomedullary differentiation. Physical exam reveals facial dysmorphism with low-set ears and micrognathia. Laboratory tests show impaired renal function. Multidisciplinary care includes cardiology, nephrology, and nutrition support.
