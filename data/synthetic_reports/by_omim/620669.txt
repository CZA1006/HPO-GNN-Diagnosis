Neurodegeneration with brain iron accumulation 9 (OMIM:620669)

The patient is a 12-year-old female presenting with progressive dystonia, rigidity, and spasticity. Symptoms began around age 8 with clumsiness and gait disturbance. Neurological exam reveals generalized hypertonia, bradykinesia, and oromandibular dystonia. Cognitive function is mildly impaired. Brain MRI shows pronounced hypointensities in the globus pallidus and substantia nigra consistent with iron accumulation. Iron studies and metabolic screening are normal. Support focuses on symptomatic management of movement disorders and physical therapy.
