Neurodevelopmental disorder with early-onset parkinsonism and behavioral abnormalities (OMIM:620747)

The patient is a 12-year-old female who developed parkinsonian features, including bradykinesia, rigidity, and postural instability at age 9. She also exhibits behavioral abnormalities including impulsivity and social withdrawal. No family history of movement disorders is reported. Neurologic examination reveals reduced facial expressivity, cogwheel rigidity, and resting tremor. Brain MRI is unremarkable. Trial of dopaminergic therapy has led to moderate symptom improvement. Multidisciplinary care includes neuropsychology and physical therapy.
