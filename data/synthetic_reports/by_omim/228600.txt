A 1-year-old male infant is brought for painful joint contractures, skin nodules, and gingival hypertrophy. Parents report progressive stiffness and swelling of large joints since infancy with difficulty in movement and feeding. Multiple subcutaneous nodules have developed over the scalp and back.

On examination, the skin is thickened and waxy with limited joint mobility. Oral examination reveals marked gingival overgrowth. Growth parameters are below the 5th percentile. Histopathology of skin nodules shows hyaline material deposition in the dermis. Laboratory tests are unremarkable, and genetic confirmation is pending.
