Macular dystrophy with or without cone dysfunction (OMIM:620762)

The patient is a 25-year-old male with progressive central vision loss and photophobia since early adulthood. Ophthalmic examination reveals macular atrophy with pigmentary changes. Visual acuity is reduced to 20/100 bilaterally. Fundus autofluorescence imaging shows patchy hypoautofluorescent areas corresponding to atrophic lesions. Optical coherence tomography demonstrates loss of the photoreceptor layer in the macula. Electroretinogram indicates mild cone dysfunction. No systemic manifestations are present. Visual rehabilitation and monitoring are ongoing.
