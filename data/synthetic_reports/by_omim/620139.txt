Combined oxidative phosphorylation deficiency 56 (OMIM:620139)

The patient is an infant presenting with failure to thrive, hypotonia, and lactic acidosis. Clinical course is complicated by episodes of metabolic decompensation and neuroregression. Laboratory investigations include elevated plasma lactate and abnormal mitochondrial respiratory chain enzyme activities identified on muscle biopsy. Brain MRI reveals symmetric basal ganglia lesions and diffuse cerebral atrophy. Multidisciplinary supportive care includes metabolic stabilization, nutritional management, and physical therapy to support neurodevelopmental progression.
