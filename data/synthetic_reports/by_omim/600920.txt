Van den Ende-Gupta syndrome (OMIM: 600920)
A 7-year-old female is assessed for craniofacial anomalies and contractures. Parents report a long face and nasal abnormalities noticed since birth, along with limited finger extension.

Physical exam reveals a long narrow face, blepharophimosis, hypoplastic nasal alae, and camptodactyly of the fingers. Joint contractures are present at elbows and knees. Radiographs show hypoplastic clavicles and mild scoliosis. Neurologic examination is unremarkable.
