Optic atrophy 16 (OMIM:620629)

A 15-year-old female complains of progressive vision loss in both eyes. Ophthalmologic examination confirms bilateral optic nerve pallor and reduced visual acuity. Visual field testing shows central and paracentral scotomas. Optical coherence tomography reveals thinning of the retinal nerve fiber layer. No systemic neurological deficits are observed. MRI of the brain is unremarkable apart from optic nerve thinning. Family history is negative for hereditary optic neuropathies. The patient is monitored regularly with visual rehabilitation support.
