Multiple mitochondrial dysfunctions syndrome 7 (OMIM:620423)

The patient is a 4-month-old female presenting with profound hypotonia, lactic acidosis, failure to thrive, and developmental arrest. Laboratory investigations reveal lactic acidemia and elevated alanine. Muscle biopsy shows reduced activities of multiple mitochondrial respiratory chain complexes. Brain MRI demonstrates diffuse cerebral and cerebellar atrophy with delayed myelination. Supportive care includes nutritional support, metabolic stabilization, and symptomatic management. The condition is progressive with a guarded prognosis.
