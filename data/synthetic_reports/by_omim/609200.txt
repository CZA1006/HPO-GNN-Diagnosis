Myopathy, myofibrillar, 3 (OMIM:609200)

The patient is a 27-year-old male presenting with slowly progressive proximal muscle weakness and myalgia over five years. Family history reveals a similarly affected maternal uncle. Neurological examination shows decreased strength in shoulder and hip girdle muscles with mild scapular winging. Deep tendon reflexes are preserved. Serum creatine kinase is mildly elevated. Electromyography demonstrates myopathic changes. Muscle biopsy reveals myofibrillar disarray, accumulation of protein aggregates, and inclusions on histopathology. Cardiac evaluation is unremarkable.
