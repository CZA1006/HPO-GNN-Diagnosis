Intellectual developmental disorder, autosomal dominant 21 (OMIM:615502)

The patient is a 6-year-old male referred for evaluation of intellectual disability and mild behavioral abnormalities. Pregnancy and neonatal history were unremarkable. Family history does not reveal similar presentations. Physical examination shows mild facial dysmorphisms including broad nasal bridge and thin upper lip. Neurological exam reveals no focal deficits. Developmental assessment highlights delayed speech and learning difficulties. Brain MRI is unremarkable. Educational and behavioral therapies have been initiated.
