Bardet-Biedl syndrome 5 (OMIM:615983)

The patient is a 7-year-old male with delayed milestones, obesity, and polydactyly. Family history is negative for genetic conditions. On examination, postaxial polydactyly is present on both feet, along with hypogenitalism and mild intellectual disability. Visual field constriction and retinal pigmentary changes are detected on ophthalmologic assessment. Renal function is normal, with ultrasounds showing no anomalies. Supportive care focuses on developmental therapies and regular surveillance for multisystem involvement.
