Developmental and epileptic encephalopathy 113 (OMIM:620772)

The patient is a 7-month-old female with early-onset refractory epilepsy and global developmental delay. Seizures include tonic spasms, myoclonic jerks, and focal seizures. Neurologic examination reveals marked hypotonia and poor visual tracking. EEG demonstrates multifocal epileptiform activity and burst suppression patterns. MRI brain shows diffuse cerebral atrophy and delayed myelination. Extensive metabolic evaluations are normal. Seizure management includes multiple antiepileptic drugs with partial control. Supportive rehabilitation services are underway.
