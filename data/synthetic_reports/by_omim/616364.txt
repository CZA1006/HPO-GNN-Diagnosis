White-Sutton syndrome (OMIM:616364)

The patient is a 4-year-old male presenting with developmental delay, speech impairment, and subtle craniofacial dysmorphism. Family history is noncontributory. On examination, he exhibits hypotonia, poor social engagement, and mild facial asymmetry with a broad nasal bridge. MRI brain shows mild ventriculomegaly and delayed myelination. EEG is unremarkable. Routine laboratory investigations and metabolic screening are normal. Early intervention therapies are underway.
