Microcephaly 5, primary, autosomal recessive (OMIM:608716)

The patient is an 11-month-old female presenting with congenital microcephaly and marked global developmental delay. Birth history was notable for intrauterine growth restriction. The infant has feeding difficulties and poor head control. Examination confirms severe microcephaly and axial hypotonia. Brain MRI reveals diffuse cortical thinning and ventricular enlargement. Metabolic and genetic investigations are in progress.
