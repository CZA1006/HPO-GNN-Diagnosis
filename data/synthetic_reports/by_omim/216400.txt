A 4-year-old male is referred for evaluation of developmental delay, growth failure, and progressive neurological decline. His parents note that he has had global developmental delays since infancy, with limited speech and unsteady gait that has worsened over the past year. Photosensitivity is reported, with a history of sun intolerance and freckling on exposed skin. Hearing loss and sensorineural deficits have recently been documented.

On examination, the child shows severe growth retardation with height and weight below the 3rd percentile. He has a thin, frail appearance with noticeable cachexia. Facial features include a beaked nose, sunken eyes, and microcephaly. Neurologic exam reveals spasticity, ataxia, and diminished deep tendon reflexes. Ophthalmologic assessment identifies retinal dystrophy and cataracts. Brain MRI reveals cerebral and cerebellar atrophy with white matter abnormalities.
