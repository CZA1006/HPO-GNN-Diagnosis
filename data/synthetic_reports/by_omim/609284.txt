Congenital myopathy 4B, autosomal recessive (OMIM:609284)

The patient is a 6-month-old male referred for hypotonia and delayed motor milestones. He was born at term without complications. Family history is negative for neuromuscular disorders. On examination, the infant shows poor head control, generalized weakness, and absent deep tendon reflexes. Creatine kinase is normal. Muscle MRI shows selective involvement of certain muscle groups. Muscle biopsy reveals characteristic structural abnormalities consistent with congenital myopathy. Respiratory function remains stable.
