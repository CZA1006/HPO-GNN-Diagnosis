Charcot-Marie-Tooth disease, type 1C (OMIM: 601098)
A 14-year-old male presents with progressive distal muscle weakness and sensory loss. Symptoms began with foot drop and frequent tripping at age 10. Family history reveals paternal relatives with similar gait disturbances.

Physical exam reveals distal muscle wasting of the lower limbs, pes cavus deformity, and decreased vibration and pinprick sensation distally. Reflexes are diminished in the ankles and knees. Nerve conduction studies demonstrate slowed motor and sensory conduction velocities consistent with a demyelinating neuropathy. Genetic testing is pending.
